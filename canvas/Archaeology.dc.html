Each world family has its own reducer, and incentives shipped broken (“an absurd price wins inside the model”).

What this changes about the commission

  1. The truth grammar exists in three dialects — real/derived/modeled/fiction; FACT/YOUR BOOKS/FORECAST/SIGNAL/RUMOR; then/act/model/later/never — and the brief adds a fourth. The frontier job is to unify them into one perceptible grammar, not coin another list. Proposal: truth as texture (00).
  2. Addresses already exist at the level of one event in one world. The five-coordinate address is an extension of working code, not a leap (Browser A).
  3. WHAT IF? has been killed once, for an honest reason. Any fork must turn other actors’ responses into declared assumptions or marked unknowns (P5 does exactly this).
  4. Standing strategy lines this program crosses: Decision Challenges rules out generative runtime agents (“no LLM-authored money, terms, obligations or consequences”), keeps Foundry internal, and makes every world beat “a matched simpler case.” P7 and P9 go past those lines on purpose. That is a founder decision, flagged — not assumed.
  5. Nothing has met a human. Across 125 branches there is no witness who is a real student, teacher or user. This canvas is pre-evidence too.
