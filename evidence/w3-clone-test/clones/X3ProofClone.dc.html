HOW DO WE KNOW?
Entries are OBSERVED: outside reporting, one source per line where the fixture names one, every line still flagged [verify]. Seals are COMPUTED: SHA-256 of the previous seal plus the entry’s canonical JSON, calculated by this page with crypto.subtle. Nothing is looked up and nothing is stored: change a letter and every later seal changes. Orange is a branch: someone’s declared assumptions in their own ink, sealed to the record at one point. It never writes on the record. Dashed means not yet checked.
