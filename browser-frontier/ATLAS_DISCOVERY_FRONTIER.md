# Atlas and discovery frontier — past search, past the home page

BOW Browser Founding Frontier · Wave 1 · 29 Sep 2026 · design reading. Sources: research A (Search
Destroyer), E (Atlas, scale, composition), H §H2 and §H5, C §2.6, B §2.4; prototypes P5 `Bench.html`,
P4 `Guest.html`, P7 `Parting.html`, P3 `Jurisdiction.html`. Verdicts on the prototypes are in
`BROWSER_MENTAL_MODEL_TOURNAMENT.md`.

## 1. The claim

Search exists because web links carry no meaning: untyped, one-way, unverified — only a corpus-wide
guess can find anything. Executable systems can publish relations that are **typed, executed,
two-way and dated**. Discovery then splits into three cases, and only one of them needs anything like
search:

| The person holds… | Discovery is… | Mechanism | AI |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Something true** (a link, a place, a clock, a seat, a kept branch, a number on a screen) — almost always | **following a relation** from it | press a true mark; typed doors open with counts before you open them (A) | NO AI |
| **A question and no anchor** | **finding the system behind the question** | the Bench: the question becomes a named dynamic; parts arrive with their origin printed on them (E) | SMALL to route; NO AI to assemble; FRONTIER only for missing couplings |
| **A question nothing answers** | **an honest hole** | the Want: an addressable empty room on the anchor it names, filled when a system appears (A) | SMALL to dedupe |

"Search shrinks to anchor lookup: a name, a place or a time, never a question." (A)

## 2. Following: the doors on a true mark

Every mark that asserts something can be pressed. Only doors that exist are offered, each showing what
lies behind it before it opens (research A, F):

- **Because** — its executed causes, as strata, never "the" cause; ink changes where a World rule or
  a fork enters. (P1's trade file, P4's article figure.)
- **Meanwhile** — what else is on the same clock (a deadline, a game night, a ground stop).
- **Who** — the seat, not the person: a line of seats with what each can decide and cannot see (A's
  Seat Line: a fan finds 2017 through the chair, not the name).
- **Here** — what runs in this place, filtered by the place rule (happened, promised, owed,
  decidable).
- **Who says yes** — the Table: everyone whose yes an act needs, including those who have not given
  it (P3's Instrument).
- **What's different** — your branch against Reality; the fracture where Reality broke an assumption.

Every arrival states its `via` ("… · via cause: your tax"); a chain of `via`s is a **wake**, and a wake
is what you share — the route with its reasons, not just the destination (A).

### How it scales to billions (PROPOSED)
Systems do not link pairwise. At registration and each run, a runtime emits reads, clock bindings,
seats, place attachments and branch assumptions against **shared anchors** (calendars, institutions,
places, seats, rule sets); the registry inverts them into five indexes (cause, clock, seat, place,
assumption). A pull is one to three index lookups and costs the local degree of one address, not the
corpus size. Hubs show a deterministic top-k by materiality, imminence and live-ness — **never
popularity** — plus a maintained count of the rest. Precedents with proven size: Wikidata-scale
identity layers (≈123.5M items, as read 29 Sep 2026), hierarchical geo-cells (H3 / S2) for "what runs
here". The warning: OpenAlex indexes ~271M works on typed edges, yet ~64% list no references (arXiv
2512.16434) — following only reaches what declares, so **declaring relations must be a condition of
publishing**, and only edges a BOW-hosted or attested runtime executed are drawn solid.

## 3. Home: not a page

Research A's verdict, adopted here as the leading hypothesis (tested in P4):

- **Doorstep** (no history): what sent you (a link, a Reading, an Offer, with its wake), else the place
  you chose, else the nearest shared clock with its reason on its face — never "nothing here yet".
- **Round** (return): the Browser opens inside the first stop and states its length ("4 stops · 1
  closes tonight"); a deterministic order (what lapses first · fractures of your kept branches ·
  things addressed to you · live within a day · drift); each stop is a **seat diff** (what your seat
  can now see, can no longer do, owes); one cause touching three of your things shows once with three
  doors; the last stop says **"Level with the record"** and the frame goes quiet.
- **Standing** (an edge, not a place): the finite ledger of relations you hold — seats, kept branches,
  follows, standing orders, wants — each with an end condition.
- **Rings** (H5): a seat you hold may ring when its clock needs a person, with a brief, a deadline and
  what happens if you don't answer; standing orders answer while you're away; a hard ring budget.

The incumbents keep a role: the **Clock Hall**'s kinds of time become the time-stamp on every line; the
**Arrive** landing becomes the Bench's answer when you have only a question.

## 4. Finding the system behind the question (P5)

"Why are airline delays spreading?" → BOW restates it as a claim to test and names the dynamic
(PROPAGATION THROUGH SHARED RESOURCES), keeping rejected neighbours as tabs. The **Bench** fills an
empty time × airport frame as parts arrive with their origin printed on them (reviewed parts with
reviewer and version; generated couplings stippled; tonight's hold as an authored "typical day"; a
missing input as a dashed socket) and a count line ("4 reviewed · 3 generated couplings · 2 assumed ·
1 gap"). The **Loom** (a Marey/Ibry time–space chart) runs the mechanism; you find its edge by pushing
(the crew's duty clock cuts leg 4) — and **the edge of a mechanism is where a seat begins**: "a person
can extend this, up to 2 h (14 CFR 117.19, verify) — who holds it?" You **unbolt** a part to learn what
it is for (lift the aircraft rotation and the spread stops). You **turn the plate** over to see what it
is made of and where it plugs into larger systems — ports as tabs, never lines.

**Scale is not zoom** (E): each step is a change of subject along a named relation (PEERS · RUN BY ·
PAID TO · WRITTEN AS · BOUND BY · MADE BY) with one quantity pinned — Boston's payroll pins through the
league's thirty payrolls, the seats that can move it, Tatum's contract, the second-apron rule and the
bargaining agreement that made the rule. Continuity is identity, not geometry. The Descent lost to a
list because it drew nested altitudes over things that are not nested.

**Composition** (P3): an act is a form whose lines other systems hold, with five kinds of yes (RULE ·
WILL · TIME · MONEY · KNOWING); the ground's register says whose rules apply; the standing line says
whose word counts; a block always names who, which yes, and what you can change.

## 5. The external-reality frontier (SPECULATIVE FRONTIER — BOW cannot do this today)

"Why are semiconductor shortages happening?" → "This is several questions" (AI-driven memory demand;
automotive parts after the Nexperia dispute; the 2021 shortage — trade press, verify). For the one you
pick: an open bench of **source slips** clipped to observed ground, each stamped PRIMARY DATA /
OFFICIAL STATEMENT / REPORTING / ANALYST ESTIMATE with date and the claim it supports; unread sources
stay blank, never paraphrased from memory. A **working model on tracing paper** over the observed
ground, with a **title block** that survives a screenshot ("WORKING MODEL · NOT REVIEWED · compiled …
· 11 sources (9 read) · 4 reviewed parts · 3 generated couplings · 7 assumed · 4 unknown · hindcast:
none · CHECKED: — · APPROVED: —") and a visible life (72 hours, proposed). **The what-if is the
assumptions ledger**: your only levers are what the compile admits it guessed; the answer moves as a
band stamped "scenario on a working model, not a forecast"; past the evidence, OUTSIDE EVIDENCE.
**Perception is backed by permission**: a working model cannot be followed, embedded in a Moment, used
as a seat's ledger or forked into a Council. **Promotion** is a drawing office's status ladder —
SKETCH → ISSUED FOR REVIEW → REVIEWED v1 → SUPERSEDED — with named reviewers signing per knot and a
hindcast against a recorded window; **discard** leaves a tombstone, and shared links resolve to the
frozen sketch "DISCARDED BY ITS AUTHOR".

**Honest feasibility** (research E): reachable now — reading sources, extracting claims with citations,
checking each quoted figure against fetched text, proposing qualitative structure (published results:
an LLM recovered ~59% of causal links from text, 2024; a May 2026 benchmark finds modelling tools
better at discussion than at causal reasoning and quantitative error fixing). Not reachable — choosing
between structures that fit the same history; quantifying unobserved decisions; validating a live
event (it has no hindcast). Cost: roughly 10⁶ tokens and minutes per compile (estimate; dollars, not
cents), and hours of expert review — so compiles cache per freshness window and review is earned by
popular questions. **The real danger is a plausible wrong model that runs: execution lends authority.**

## 6. Trust of what you discover (research B §2.7)

The frame is the Browser's, never the system's. Provenance of a whole system shows by frame, not badge:
OFFICIAL BOW (house ink, closed double rule, calm) · THIRD-PARTY VERIFIED (guest ink inside a rule BOW
closes) · THIRD-PARTY UNVERIFIED (raw edge, coarse ground, one unskippable line before you act) · LOCAL
(your ink, no BOW rule) · TEMPORARY (thin frame thinning toward expiry) · LIVE REALITY (observed solid,
running clock) · MODELED (hatch on outputs, model named) · ARCHIVED (matted, clock stopped). Risk is
loud by presence; trusted kinds are calm; unverifiable degrades to dashed.

## 7. Falsifiers and the cheapest human tests
- Newcomers with only a blank ask line reach a first meaningful entry as fast, and say why they are
  there as clearly, as those given a Doorstep or a mark to pull → search stays primary (A).
- People use one door and ignore the rest → six axes are over-built; keep two (A).
- Systems will not declare relations → edges stay sparse; the substrate degrades to untyped links (A).
- Returning people skip the Round for the Standing every time, or feel scolded → home is a ledger (A).
- A one-line provenance footer matches the Bench and the Plate on recall and calibrated distrust →
  provenance-as-structure is over-built (E).
- Polished and tracing-paper working models draw the same reliance → temporariness is not perceptible;
  only permission can enforce it (E).
