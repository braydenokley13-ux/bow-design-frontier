independent critics; no person has used any of this. Board: `prototypes/EnterTournament.html` (seven

## 1. The question
ENTER must mean more than opening 3D. It is the semantic transition OBSERVER → PARTICIPANT. Does it
change identity, role, information, authority, responsibility, time, place, available actions? What
does the person give up? How does leaving work? Could an AI agent enter the same way?

## 2. The shared seat
A fork of the recorded moment "Deadline week · 2 Feb 2026, before the first report · the tax bill is
the problem"; Boston's decision-maker; three open matters (authored); one available first act (shed a
bench salary with a second-round pick — authored, never the real move). Before ENTER the observer can
open SINCE (what happened later); after ENTER the desk knows only what was knowable then.
