# OPEN ADVOCATE: BOW as an open medium

*2026-09-29. Position paper: argued to win, honest about facts and failure modes. Labels: FACT (checked or from the brief), ANALOGY (history, not proof), SPECULATION (mine).*

## Thesis

If BOW stays an education product, ignore this paper: close everything and ship. But the stated thesis is a medium in which institutions, creators and AI agents make systems executable. A medium cannot be closed. No institution puts records in a format it cannot exit, no agent vendor builds to a private protocol, and no district trusts a receipt only the vendor can check. So: **open the thin waist (record, address, execution contract, legend, conformance tests); keep canonical instances, data rights, trust roots and the best experience as the company.**

## Why open, given today's facts

1. **Closed protects nothing we fear losing.** FACT: an AI copier rebuilt four engine boards from screenshots in 12 to 22 calls. It could not copy the canonical World, the sourced rulebook, the accumulated history or the signed head, and publishing a spec gives none of those away.
2. **Verifiability is the product.** FACT: Decision Receipt v1 is verified by another browser replaying under a pinned model, which is an open protocol in miniature. A receipt only BOW can check is an assertion; one anybody can replay is evidence.
3. **Exit rights sell to institutions.** A small, agent-built vendor with no users hears "what if you vanish?" first. HyperCard and Flash died with their single runtime (ANALOGY, wave-1 history). A published record plus a single-process classroom runtime (E-main D12 already is one) is free escrow. Districts already procure on interoperability (1EdTech LTI; secondary source).
4. **Agents write public grammars.** SPECULATION: models author HTML, SQL and Git fluently because those are public; a private World format is one outside agents cannot write, and BOW will not train the model that could. MCP is the live case (disclosure: Anthropic's): launched Nov 2024, adopted by OpenAI, Google and Microsoft within about a year, moved to Linux Foundation governance in Dec 2025.

## Where value is captured

Openness surrenders exclusive rights to code, not these:

- **Canonical instances and time authority (L3).** World One League, seasons, Commissioner clock, seats, "runs while you're away." Git is open; GitHub sold for $7.5B (2018) because hosting, graph and default place lived there.
- **Trust roots.** Signed heads, a public transparency log, key registry, runtime certification. Precedent: Certificate Transparency (RFC 6962, 2013) and certificate authorities.
- **Licensed Reality (L6).** Rights are contracts, not code, and do not fork. Licence rides in provenance ("who may see it, for how long," per the economic-architecture board), so a portable World degrades honestly instead of leaking a feed.
- **Certification and the mark.** A voluntary "BOW-Verified" mark plus public suite (Wi-Fi Alliance style). Caution: Android's compatibility gating, tied to must-have Google apps, drew a EUR 4.125B fine, upheld by the EU Court of Justice in July 2026. Never tie the mark to paid services.
- **Enterprise receipts and audit** ("Receipts are the enterprise product"), **intelligence orchestration by seat**, **marketplace take**, and the **education vertical** (curriculum, evidence service, district contracts).

Red Hat (about $34B to IBM, 2019) shows open substrate plus certification and support can be huge; Alphabet shows a giant can live on open HTTP/HTML by running the best product on it. The founder-facing economics board already says "never charge for the format."

## Security and trust with third-party runtimes

Three mechanisms, chosen by the claim:

- **Re-execution:** the verifier recomputes seed plus act log under pinned semantics. Strongest and cheapest, and the lockstep fold and Receipt replay already do it; classroom-scale Worlds replay in milliseconds. It fails when inputs are private (seat-scoped), nondeterministic (an AI seat's output is recorded as an act, never regenerated) or external (feeds).
- **Verification** of cheap-to-check claims: hash chain, signatures, inclusion in a public log, full 64-hex heads (clone-test bug: an 8-hex compare with no anchor).
- **Attestation** where neither works (private inputs, live feeds, real-world writeback): trust shifts from math to identity and liability, as a signed "this build passed conformance suite vN" or an accountable operator's signature.

Proposal (SPECULATION): every record carries a **verification basis** beside the seven truth statuses: REPLAYED, SIGNED, ATTESTED, CLAIMED. Assessment counts only REPLAYED by a pinned or certified verifier; a runtime that swaps the observer cannot mint a "5," because observer and caps are part of what replays. Foreign rules run sandboxed (bounded, deterministic, no ambient I/O); World text is data to AI seats; student PII never travels in the format, and the district chooses which runtime touches child data. Honest gap: CloneVerdict shows seat secrecy guards display, not inference, so no certification may claim secrecy until derived-secret tainting exists.

## Adoption, ecosystem, network effects

Winners published a thin waist after one small tool had users, and let engines compete: web (URL, HTML; CERN public domain, 30 Apr 1993), LSP (2016; M editors times N languages becomes M plus N), Git, PDF (Adobe 1993, ISO 32000-1 in 2008, Adobe still sells the software), OpenUSD (Pixar 2016; Alliance with Adobe, Apple, Autodesk, NVIDIA, Aug 2023), Kubernetes (Google 2014, CNCF 2015). The ecosystem is four audiences: outside verifiers (days of work, no permission), World authors (view-source and fork on any World), runtime hosts (classrooms, districts, labs) and agent builders (seat contract). Network effects live at the shared record: each party able to verify a canonical head raises its value (SMTP, URLs). They accrue to the thin layers a company controls (registry, log, mark, canonical Worlds), not to the format.

## Per-layer call

| Layer | Call | Reason |

| L7 intelligence | **Adopt existing protocols (MCP-style); open seat contract; closed orchestration** | Do not invent one. |
| L8 tools, market | **Closed product; marketplace not yet** | Supply first. |
| L9 verticals | **Closed products** | Where revenue is. |

## Failure modes of my strategy

- **Premature standard.** VRML97, OpenDoc, Semantic Web. BOW has zero outside implementers. Rule: two implementations before v1; spec follows code.
- **Free-riding.** Elastic vs AWS (SSPL 2021, OpenSearch fork, AGPL 2024), Redis (2024, Valkey, AGPL 2025), HashiCorp (BSL 2023, OpenTofu fork, IBM bought it 2025): relicensing later burns trust, so choose licences before publishing. Docker gave away its container format (OCI, 2015), lost orchestration to Kubernetes and sold its enterprise unit (2019). Today BOW's moat is zero: history starts at first start.
- **Semantic drift.** Doom demos desync, Excel's 1900 leap year. Version frame in every record; public suite before any third-party runtime.
- **Nobody comes.** Open protocols without a killer genre stay empty. Needs a genre, not a spec.
- **Steward burden.** WordPress/WP Engine (2024 blocking, injunction Dec 2024). Small team; each compatibility promise is a tax.
- **Closed can win.** Roblox, Excel, Figma, Photoshop. My claim is conditional on institutional records and agent access.

## Next 12 months

1. **Months 0 to 3:** make the waist singular: one canonical-JSON, one `bow://` grammar, golden vectors from Receipt v1 and the World record, full 64-hex signed heads. Publish draft v0 and a permissively licensed browser verifier. No foundation, federation, registry or marketplace.
2. **Blind-implementer test:** the clone test aimed at the spec. Agents build a verifier in another language from the spec alone; pass is every vector and tamper case. Cheap now.
3. **Months 3 to 9:** run World One on a real shared store with real people and start the public log; only time produces history. Recruit five outsiders and one non-BOW author to ship a World unaided (VisiCalc-style kill test).
4. **Months 9 to 12:** conformance kit, voluntary mark policy, documented self-hosted classroom runtime and receipt export for districts.
5. **Kill criteria at month 12:** no outside implementer, author or verifier means archive the spec and return to closed product. Classroom truth still outranks all of this: nothing here is user-proven.

## Strongest objection and answer

*"You hand a format to whoever has distribution and cloud, no user asked, standards tax a small team, and school assessment wants one accountable vendor, not a federation."* Answer: (a) the clone test shows closed never protected the parts we fear losing; canonical instance, sourced rules, history and signature stay ours. (b) Docker, Elastic and Redis lost when value was code anyone could run; BOW places value where a cloud cannot fork it, but that is unproven until history exists. (c) Open in increments of proven waist, never a big bang. (d) The accountable vendor remains: certified hosting is the product. Openness adds exit rights, which small vendors need to close institutional deals.
