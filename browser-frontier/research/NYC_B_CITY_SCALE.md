## 0. The answer

**Is SCALE a native verb beside ENTER / WHY / WHAT IF / TIME / ROLE / FOLLOW?** Yes, under conditions. SCALE is the only verb that changes the *system* coordinate of the address while carrying a *pin* through a *conversion*. Without the pin and the conversion it is a hyperlink. Without the typed relation it is a camera. Without a rewrite of the standing line it lies about authority. (PROPOSED)

**Can the strongest grammar (Jurisdiction acts + Cut time + Concordance views on one record) navigate a Reality City?** Yes for structure and authority, no for sums, and only as fast as *bridges* get authored. The prototype carries one quantity (tonight's arena crowd) from the arena through transit and neighborhood to the city, past a club HQ and a League on the side, on the 4,142 real Midtown footprints, and it held. What it found:

- The map is cheap and the bridges are not. Footprints, lots and facilities arrive free as RECORDED joins. Every conversion that matters (crowd to rail riders, riders to street density) needs an owner, a kind and a source. Bridges, not tiles, are the scarce object.
- A place is not an institution. One structure (BIN 1082908) sits on a lot titled to a rail corporation, hosts an arena the registries do not name, and has a station below it that no footprint sees.
- Geography answers one family of question (what is physically here). It is silent on the League, the money, the rule, and the yes.
- Sums fail honestly. "The city" stops the pin because a denominator is missing, not because the map ended.

## 1. What SCALE is not

1. **Not camera zoom.** L1 plan and L3 massing are *views* of the subject. In the prototype the two buttons never change the subject; a hop always moves the camera to the new subject's extent, never the reverse. Law: the camera follows the subject; the subject never follows the camera.
2. **Not up and down.** From the arena, "up" is six different things: the neighborhood it sits in, the rail corporation that holds the lot, the club that runs it, the League that binds it, the transit that serves it, the kind of thing it is. A rule is above a club in authority and below it in size (E4: rules are hinges).
3. **Not a hierarchy of places.** MSG's lot is class U6, land use 7, title "National Railroad Passenger Corp", year built 0, building area 0 [M, PLUTO 26v2]. The parcel holds a structure, a title, an arena and a station. None is the parcel.
4. **Not a graph.** Only the relations that exist for the subject in view render (5 to 9 hinges). No global edge set is drawn.

## 2. The verb

**SCALE = follow one typed relation from the subject you are in to another, carrying a pin through a bridge.**

The address is `system · moment · branch · viewpoint · view`. Each verb owns a different coordinate:

| Verb | Changes | Keeps |
|---|---|---|
| ENTER / ROLE | viewpoint (seat) | system, moment |
| TIME | moment | system |
| WHAT IF | branch | system, moment |
| WHY | focus onto causes (a SCALE along CAUSES, with a residual check) | moment |
| FOLLOW | what Home keeps (a subject, or a pin) | — |
| **SCALE** | **system, and re-derives viewpoint; adds a pin** | **moment, branch** |
| (view) | representation: Room, Table, L1, L3 | everything |

**Five marks.** (1) RELATION: typed, printed on the hinge, reversible. (2) PIN: a quantity or an act chosen at the start, never "a place". (3) BRIDGE: the conversion from the pin's terms here to its terms next door, with an owner, a kind and an as-of. (4) GROUND: the standing line and register rewrite, because SCALE changes jurisdiction; a pin carries quantity, never authority. (5) END: the chain stops where a bridge has no source, as UNKNOWN with a reason and a fileable Want.

**Six laws** (five are exercised in the prototype; the third shows only as a hole):

1. **SITED-1: location carries only what is physically there.** The crowd is on the street, so SITED AT carries it as a density. The crowd is not in a clinic 290 m away, and nothing there serves the arena, so the hop is refused and the pin stays behind.
2. **A pin wears the kind of its weakest bridge.** 18,000 (AUTHORED) × 55% (AUTHORED) = 9,900 riders is arithmetic, and it is still AUTHORED. Nothing downstream of an authored scenario becomes COMPUTED.
3. **A sum is a definer's claim.** Any "up" over a predicate (all clubs located in NYC) needs a predicate, an owner and a coverage line.
4. **The camera follows the subject.**
5. **Hops are trail, not record.** Navigation is private and never hashed; only acts fold into the SHA-256 chain.
6. **A neck exists only where a limit is loaded.** TIGHTEST HERE is computed among sheets that have a limit. Here only the transit sheet has one (load ÷ seats 1.24); the neighborhood and city have none, and say so.

**SCALE against the other verbs.**

- SCALE × TIME: the pin stays at the same cut. The footprints have no past (the portal keeps only the latest [A]), so "Midtown in 1965" from this file would be the surviving fabric, not the city. It needs the 2014 model or old maps and must say so.
- SCALE × ROLE: the seat does not travel. At the arena you hold "the club's site and the desk's requests"; at the transit desk you can see the shape of its timetable and cannot add a train.
- SCALE × WHAT IF: a pencil push on the pin (crowd 20,000) redraws every sheet in the branch's ink; release erases it. Keeping it forks one branch, diverged at act #12, and the record stays beside it.
- SCALE × WHY: every number opens a trace to acts and sources; the trace states the kind of the weakest input.
- SCALE × FOLLOW: what a person keeps can be a pin ("keep watching tonight's crowd wherever it goes"). PROPOSED; not built.

## 3. The typed relation families

Nine families cover the ten the brief names. Each family fixes what happens to the pin (its *algebra*) and what it refuses. Every edge is a record with an asserter, a kind and an as-of.

| Family (tab, forward / reverse) | Brief's names | What the pin becomes | Refuses | In the prototype |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| COMPOSES (PART OF / MADE OF) | contains, part-of | additive: parts sum to the whole, if membership is defined | a sum with no definer or coverage | neighborhood → city |
| SITES (SITED AT / SITES, NEAR) | located-in | a density over what is physically there | money, duty, rule, load on a system not served | arena → neighborhood; neighborhood → clinic (refused) |
| SEATS (RUN BY / RUNS) | operates, employs | a duty held by a seat; no conversion | a quantity | arena → club HQ |
| TITLES (OWNED BY / OWNS) | owns | a claim; title is not operation | inferring an operator from a title | a row on the site, not a hop |
| BINDS (BOUND BY / MADE BY) | governed-by | meets a limit or rule; may re-express (crowd becomes reportable attendance) | | arena → League |
| FLOWS (PAID TO / TAKEN FROM) | pays | conserved: one ledger falls, one rises (money is the case here; matter and energy elsewhere) | creating stuff | the fee at the last yes |
| SERVES (SERVED BY / SERVES) | supplies | demand becomes load on a capacity; a ratio and possibly a neck | | arena → transit |
| KINDS (PEERS) | instance-of | a position in a distribution | | other arenas (no sheet; Want) |
| SHOWS (WRITTEN AS) | (represented-as) | the same thing in another form | | not built (Boston's contract staircase) |

Registry joins by BIN, BBL and facility uid are free, RECORDED edges. SERVES, BINDS and FLOWS are authored one at a time. A Reality City therefore has a very uneven edge population: dense where a city registry exists, sparse where authority lives.

## 4. Geography is one family, not the index

**Four jobs for SITES.**

1. **Join key.** BIN names a structure, BBL a lot, a FacDB uid a facility. They differ: footprint BIN 1020338 carries BBL 1009180021 while FacDB lists the same building on 1009187501 (a condo billing lot), so the join must be by BIN [M].
2. **Grouping predicate** for aggregates: "clubs located in NYC" is a query over SITES, owned by whoever defines it.
3. **Catchment.** The one place geography does real arithmetic. Within 400 m of the arena centroid there are 357 footprints, 13 without a year, 0 without a height, and about 223,400 m² of open ground (circle minus footprints, grid-sampled at 4 m; it includes courts, so it overstates walkable ground) [M]. The crowd per hectare ceiling (18,000 ÷ that area) is 806, and it is AUTHORED because the crowd is.
4. **Display substrate.** L1 and L3.

**What SITES cannot carry:** authority (a League rulebook has no lot; the prototype's League sheet says "off the map by design"), money, rules, contracts, and anything below ground. Penn Station has no footprint: the extent is drawn as a dashed ring, UNKNOWN.

**Geography by chain** (Section 5): it appears once or twice in the two sports chains, as a predicate; not at all in the chemistry, biology and invoice chains. Where it is absent, a map is expensive decoration. Where it appears, it is a hop like any other.

**"What is here?" returns a site.** Clicking a building opens the site sheet: structure (BIN, RECORDED), lot (BBL, PLUTO 26v2), operators (FacDB 26v1), BOW tenancies (AUTHORED), and namespaces kept apart (`nyc:bin:…`, `nyc:bbl:…`, `bow:lamplighters/…`). A site is where authorities meet; none of them is the site.

## 5. The chains

Design analysis; only the arena chain is built. Pin, then the family of each hop. G marks a geographic (SITES) hop.

| Chain | Hops | G | Pin | Where it ends honestly |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| contract → player → club → league → NYC sports economy → global | SHOWS/FLOWS · SEATS · COMPOSES · SITES-as-predicate · COMPOSES | 1 | a salary line on a cap sheet | "NYC sports economy" is a saved question: N of M clubs have a sheet, finances mostly AUTHORED |
| object → person → organization → neighborhood → city → league → economy | TITLES · SEATS · SITES · COMPOSES · SITES (reverse: city hosts clubs) · COMPOSES | 2 | a provenance claim, or a count | the neighborhood-to-league jump needs a definer |
| molecule → organelle → cell → tissue → organ → organism | COMPOSES × 5 | 0 | mass or free energy | none until a level lacks a model |
| document → person → institution → neighborhood → city → country | SHOWS · SEATS · SITES · COMPOSES · COMPOSES | 1 | a record or right | a country-level denominator |
| molecule → reaction → experiment → lab → industry → environment | SERVES · COMPOSES · SEATS · KINDS · FLOWS | 0 to 1 | moles, or emitted mass | environment: a flow needs a receiving model |
| invoice → department → company → supplier network → industry | FLOWS · COMPOSES · COMPOSES · SERVES · KINDS | 0 to 1 | a payable | industry: a definer |

Two things fall out. Only the biology chain is honestly nested, so it is the one place camera zoom and SCALE nearly coincide; even there each level has laws the parts lack, so the subject really does change. And every chain ends on an aggregate or a denominator, never on "the map runs out".

## 6. How the grammar navigates a Reality City

**6.1 Substrate.** Building footprints (RECORDED, weekly), PLUTO (quarterly), FacDB (six-monthly), GTFS-realtime (live, mirrored) [A]. The prototype fetches the 4,142-footprint GeoJSON from `_shared/nyc/` and embeds a small dated PLUTO/FacDB excerpt (about 8.7 KB, fetched 30 Sep 2026) for the cluster's lots and facilities. Nothing else is fetched. Page: 108.9 KB.

**6.2 Three doors.** *Ask* ("What does tonight's crowd do to the city?"), *site* (click a building or use the Direct list), *pin* (carry a quantity). The map is one door, the one that answers "what is here". A typed ask that no system answers becomes "NO SYSTEM FOR THIS", the words are kept, and the person may file it as a Want.

**6.3 The cluster** (authority per system):

- **Arena desk**: AUTHORED tenancy on BIN 1082908. The real operator is UNKNOWN: FacDB has no row on that BIN [M]. Its nearest MSG-named row is a privately-owned-public-space record on a different lot (BBL 1007810002, BIN 1083026), operator "Not Available".
- **Transit desk**: a practice seat that stands in for separate real operators. Its OBSERVED slots (trains, alerts, lag) are UNKNOWN with the reason "no feed runs here"; a toggle stages an AUTHORED sample that is labelled STAGED, never OBSERVED. FacDB's one rail row in the box is "C YARD", operator MTA, on BIN 1084820, the box's largest footprint (25,888 m²) [M]: a different building from the station.
- **Club HQ** (the Lamplighters): AUTHORED, placed only through the tests below.
- **League office**: AUTHORED rulebook, no place.
- **Neighborhood and city**: saved questions, no seat, defined by BOW.
- **Clinic and museum**: registry rows only (35th Street Health Center, BIN 1014421; Museum of Modern Art, BIN 1081128). BOW has no system for them and does not invent one. Of 30 hospital-and-clinic rows FacDB lists in the box, none is typed plain HOSPITAL: 12 are hospital extension clinics, 18 are diagnostic and treatment centers or their extension clinics [M, my query].

**6.4 Placing an AUTHORED institution.** Four candidate sites are tested against the real footprints and the dated lot and facility rows. The tests run; they are not scripted:

| Site | Overlap | Vacancy | Institution | Render |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A · over BIN 1082908 | FAIL: meets the arena | FAIL: land use 7, class U6, building area reads 0 (UNKNOWN, not zero) | WATCH: no FacDB row; title to a rail corporation; operator UNKNOWN | refused as a building; a tenancy flag is allowed |
| B · Fifth Avenue block-front | PASS: none of 4,142 | PASS: 8 lot points, land use 11 | PASS: no FacDB row (absence is not proof) | AUTHORED dashed outline, a proposal |
| C · Second Avenue, City-owned lot | WATCH: meets BIN 1020204, filed on another lot | PASS: 2 lot points, vacant | WATCH: owner type City | CAN'T TELL |
| D · over BIN 1014421 | FAIL | FAIL: commercial/office | WATCH: 18 FacDB rows, 16 operators in one structure | tenancy allowed; both shown |

Findings from building it:

- **The overlap test needs geometry the PLUTO table does not give.** It holds a label point per lot; lot boundaries live in MapPLUTO, which this page does not load. For site C the guess rectangle meets a building PLUTO files on a neighboring lot. The honest verdict is CAN'T TELL, the same third verdict Jurisdiction's rehearsal uses. The checker refuses to draw a guess.
- **Registry vacancy is not physical vacancy.** Block 1262 has 16 vacant-land lots (101,383 sq ft), including one of 51,459 sq ft, all land use 11 in PLUTO 26v2, and no footprint stands on them in the 27 Sep file [M]. Two registries agree; neither says whether a crane or a hoarding stands there. The row says so.
- **One structure, many institutions.** BIN 1014421 is a 1922, 12-floor office building carrying 18 FacDB rows from 16 operators: schools, a health clinic, sanitation drop-off sites [M]. "The institution at this address" has no singular.
- **Authored things are outlines or flags, never fills.** An authored footprint that meets a real BIN renders as a tenancy flag; on vacant ground as a dashed outline with a paper halo. Nothing authored is drawn solid.

**6.5 The pin and its bridges** (numbers from the run; every one AUTHORED except where marked):

| Subject | Pin reads | Bridge |
|---|---|---|
| Arena desk | 18,000 people | scenario, act #1 |
| Transit desk | 9,900 rail riders in 60 min | × 55% rail share |
| Transit desk | 8,000 seats in window; load ÷ seats 1.24; 1,900 still waiting at minute 60 | 8 trains an hour × 1,000 seats (practice values) |
| Neighborhood, 400 m | 806 per hectare ceiling; 1,900 still here | ÷ open ground (COMPUTED from RECORDED); the ceiling is AUTHORED |
| League | reportable attendance 18,000 | rule L-ATT-1 (authored) |
| City | share of the city's evening trips: UNKNOWN | no ridership denominator loaded; Want |

The ribbon prints this chain as a receipt. Each earlier sheet folds to an edge (and to a button in the ribbon on narrow screens) that reads the pin in its own terms. The city's coverage line is the one COMPUTED thing there: 4,142 of 1,083,062 footprints, 0.38% [A count; verify].

**6.6 Crossing an authority.** "Ask for extra post-game trains" is a form whose lines other systems hold: ARENA · WILL (yours), TRANSIT · RULE (R-EV-1, authored: carry a rider count of at least the pinned riders), TRANSIT · TIME (free/busy slots, shape only), TRANSIT · WILL (their discretion, on their clock, UNKNOWN), MONEY (two ledgers, conserved). The run:

1. Rehearse: NO, naming R-EV-1 (no count attached). A rehearsal never says the WILL-yes.
2. Sign line 1 and send. Notice copies stay with the arena desk and club HQ. The page takes the transit desk's register. It is **returned at intake naming R-EV-1**.
3. Re-form: ask for 3 trains, attach 9,900, pick three slots. Rehearse: CAN'T TELL (rule and time pass; will is theirs). Send: queued. The disclosure is count and result only; others' contents: nothing.
4. Wait for their clock. Their WILL-yes is partial by their own practice: 3 asked, 2 granted. The fee moves (arena desk $250,000 to $210,000; transit desk $0 to $40,000). Everything is illustrative and AUTHORED.
5. State changes in six places at once: the transit sheet (seats 8,000 to 10,000; ratio 1.24 to 0.99; waiting 1,900 to 0), the map (the backlog ring disappears), the pin ribbon, the neighborhood sheet, the record (acts #9 to #12), and the three logs.

Records are one hash-folded log per system (SHA-256, Web Crypto); one act number is shared. Reload restores the address, the seat (a lease held on this device) and the record, and verifies the fold against the stored heads.

The verification caught a real design fault while I built it. Replay first ran before the footprints had loaded, so the HQ act's text (which quotes the footprint count) differed and the club log read MISMATCH. The repair was to load the city first. The lesson is larger: an act that recomputes its tests from live data cannot verify once that data drifts (footprints refresh weekly [A]). A real record must pin the as-of and hash of the evidence an act was tested against, and replay must read the pin, not today's rows. PROPOSED.

**6.7 Reality semantics against 3D realism.** The risk is that a solid, lit city makes BOW's things look real. Rules applied: (1) L3 is flat-roofed prisms in one paper tone with dark roof edges, no facades, no photographic texture; the map note says COMPUTED. (2) AUTHORED is dashed outline only, with a paper halo so it survives against dark masses. (3) Footprints with no height are drawn flat with a dashed edge. (4) The unknown extent under the ground is a dashed ring. (5) Perspective drops the scale bar and says so. (6) The seven kinds carry texture on the legend; MODELED and GENERATED appear as "none ran / none here". (7) No Google photoreal tiles: Lane A reads the terms as forbidding geometry derived from them and leaves hit-testing ambiguous [A, verify], so they could only be a view-only "as photographed" lens. PROPOSED.

Measured: L1 (roofs only) draws 13,906 triangles; L3 (roofs and walls) draws 58,116 after simplifying rings to 1.6 m [M]. The prototype contract budget is 30k, so L3 is about twice over. In L3 the real fix is level of detail by subject extent (extrude the focus radius, keep the rest as plan), not a smaller city. Chromebook frame time is not measured.

## 7. The prototype

**Concept.** Ask what tonight's crowd does to the city, and watch the pin change jurisdiction at every hop while geography carries none of it.

**Flow (one steps file, 40 shots per viewport).** Arrive as observer → How do we know? / What's real? → L3 → click a building (a site) → typed ask with no system → arrive at the arena, the question becomes the pin → ENTER (take the operations seat) → HQ site tests, refusals naming OVERLAP-1 and OVERLAP-2, placement → League (no place) → SCALE to transit → neighborhood → NEAR refused (SITED-1) → city (END, Want) → the form (rehearsal, returned at intake, re-form, queue, their clock, partial yes) → state change in the record, three records → WHY → WHAT IF (pencil, crowd 20,000) → keep as a branch (compare with the record: waiting 0 vs 1,000; minutes to clear 59.4 vs 67.5) → return → staged feed → reload.

**Weakest parts.** (1) Bridges are authored constants. The design's central claim, that bridges are the scarce object, is shown by their absence, not by a tool for making them. (2) The transit desk is a scripted stand-in. No feed runs; the MTA mirror terms [A] are untested. (3) The pin path is one chain; nothing shows two pins colliding. (4) The 1024×600 layout is dense and the sheet body scrolls inside its panel; ribbon buttons are 32 px tall and map markers 30 px, under the 44 px target; markers are map labels anchored to places, not cards. (5) L3 has no level of detail and exceeds the triangle budget. (6) The neighborhood's "open ground" cannot separate streets from courtyards. (7) No human has used it.

**Facts I am unsure of.** Footprint heights are feet (inferred: the two tallest read 1,401 and 1,239, matching One Vanderbilt and the Empire State Building's roof [verify]). The 1,083,062 city total is Lane A's count. PLUTO land-use names are the standard PLUTO codes. The "C YARD" row is the only rail row in my FacDB query of the box; a wider query might find station rows [verify]. The clinic 290 m from the arena is the FacDB row's BIN centroid distance, not a walking distance.

## 8. What would falsify this

- **A list with a pin and push ties the sheets** (E4's test), or a plain place page with "nearby" links answers three questions as well: *who can add a train?*, *does the clinic on 35th Street get the crowd?*, *why can't the club go on the Garden?* Ten adults, clickable, five minutes each. If it ties, keep the pin and the refusals, drop the drawn sheets.
- **People read the ribbon as a route, not a receipt.** If they cannot say why the number changed from 18,000 to 9,900, the bridge is invisible.
- **SITED-1 reads as a bug.** Testers who expect the crowd to reach the clinic because it is close are telling us proximity is what they think a relation is. Then the refusal must teach, not just block.
- **Bridges cannot be authored fast enough.** If each bridge needs an expert day, a Reality City has a few hundred hops, not millions, and the value is in the reviewed few.
- **Testers insist on up and down** whatever the tabs say. Then "change of subject" is a false theory (E's own falsifier).
- **A definer never appears.** If nobody will own "NYC sports economy", the aggregate hops stay UNKNOWN and the two sports chains stop at the club.
