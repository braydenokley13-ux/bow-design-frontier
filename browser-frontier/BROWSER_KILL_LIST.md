# Browser kill list — what this wave killed, parked, demoted or corrected

BOW Browser Founding Frontier · Wave 1 · 30 Sep 2026. Every line names who killed it and why, so the
founder can resurrect anything with the evidence in view. KILL = do not build again in this form.
PARK = sound idea, wrong time or wrong scale. DEMOTE = survives inside something else. LAW = a
correction that now binds every build. Sources: `critique/CRITIC_1_MEDIUM.md` (C1),
`critique/CRITIC_2_PLATFORM.md` (C2), parent synthesis (P), research (A–H), NYC lane A (NYC-A).
