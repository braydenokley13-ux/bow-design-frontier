## What this board must prove

The founder asked what replaces the page, the result, tabs, the address, open, back, follow and
home. Critics chose parts from ten challengers. This board shows them as ONE Browser, on one path,
so the founder can react to the whole:

| Old idea | Replaced by |
|---|---|
| HOME | The Clock Hall: your presences, each keeping its own kind of time. The ask line sits above it. |
| TAB | A presence: a small clock that keeps ticking while you're elsewhere. |
| SEARCH / RESULT | A question places you in the state that answers it (an arrival), not a list. |
| PAGE | An arrival, with four edges: top = provenance, bottom = the river of doors, left = seats, right = WHAT IF?. |
| ADDRESS | One editable mono line that rewrites on every move. |
| OPEN (to act) | A door (named by its question), then a seat's horizon, then signing for named open matters. Your clock starts. |
| BACK | Three verbs: Leave the seat (hand over / vacate), Rewind (navigation), Back to the branch point. |
| FOLLOW | "Keep this" hangs the arrival on your wall as a new presence. |

## The path (six states; a slim state rail across the top shows where you are)

**1. HOME · THE CLOCK HALL.**
- The ask line sits at the top, large: "Ask anything a system can answer…"
- Below it hangs ONE ROW of seven presences, faces about 120px, each with a two-line label. Kind
  is told by the kind of time:
  1. Boston Celtics · Reality: sweeping second hand, "live", and a pulse "2 changes since you
     looked" [from the Reality feed].
  2. Boston · Year Two, your World: calendar dial at Week 9, "advances Mondays".
  3. 17 Jun 2017 · a Moment: stopped face, with the future half sealed.
  4. Your fork · Keep No. 1: two hands diverging from one pivot, with "by you" on it.
  5. A public fork: "Holiday kept · by [author] · diverged Jun 2025". Two hands, grey-violet,
     author stamped.
  6. Second-apron consequences · model v0.3: run counter "RUN 000 · sketch, not a run".
  7. Garden nights · by [creator] · unreviewed: a stippled face.
- Under the row, a one-line key: "Kind is told by time: live · calendar · stopped · diverged ·
  counter · stippled = unreviewed". Words for each face appear on hover or focus.
- The address line reads `bow://home · as you`.

**2. ASK → ARRIVE.**
- Clicking a suggested question (or pressing Enter in the ask line) with "Can Boston keep this
  roster under the second apron?" makes the hall recede upward (about 500ms) and ARRIVES you in
  the answering state.
- Payroll: a big "$203.6M".
- The answer sentence in ultramarine: "Yes, by about $18.1M. It's $3.2M past the tax line and
  $5.4M short of the first apron." COMPUTE it from the fixture lines.
- The payroll bar against the cap, the tax line and the two aprons.
- Kind as ground: an 8px full-height band at the left edge in Reality's material (solid black
  with a small live tick), plus a whisper line "Boston Celtics · Reality · observer · live".
- Address: `bow://celtics/reality@2026-09-29/payroll?as=observer`.
- THE FOUR EDGES are thin, labelled tabs at the board edges. Each opens a 260–320px drawer that
  pushes content aside; it never overlays it.
  - **TOP · How do we know?** A provenance list for each number (payroll: fixture · verify; lines:
    league, June 2026 · verify; gaps: COMPUTED).
  - **BOTTOM · The river.** A thin strip of this system's recorded time, with three doors named
    by their QUESTION:
    - "17 Jun 2017 · Philadelphia calls about No. 1";
    - "12 May 2025 · Your best player is down. What now?";
    - "5 Feb 2026 · Keep Simons or take Vučević?".
    Doors are outlined uprights. Opening one is out of scope: show "doors open into Moments —
    see the River board".
  - **LEFT · Seats.** "Who could you be here?" Three seats:
    - Observer: you, now, solid.
    - "GM · in your World, Boston · Year Two" (green #007A33 = yours to take).
    - "Boston's real front office · observed with sources, not played". Its outline is
      observe-only, and it cannot be taken; the refusal names the rule "real people are
      observed, not worn".
  - **RIGHT · WHAT IF?** One pull handle on the payroll bar, dragging $180M–$235M. A frame in
    fork orange #C2410C with a stamp "your pull · not Reality", and the computed consequence
    (which lines are crossed; simplified incremental tax, labelled). "Let go" returns it; nothing
    is kept unless you press "Cut a branch" (stamped). Keep this minimal.
- A "Keep this" button beside "Share" hangs this arrival on the wall (see state 6).

**3. SEAT → THRESHOLD.**
- Choosing the green GM seat moves you to your World: the ground band changes to the World's
  double-rule, and the whisper reads "Boston · Year Two · your World · Week 9 · not NBA history".
- The THRESHOLD appears: four compact columns, KNOW / CAN / CAN'T / NOBODY IN THIS SEAT KNOWS.
  - Know: "Payroll ≈ $222.8M — over the World's second apron by ≈ $1.1M."
  - Can: "Answer Denver; call up or hold; take Butler's meeting."
  - Can't: "Go past the second apron at the deadline without the owner."
  - Nobody knows: "What Denver's GM will do if you wait."
- Below it, the open matters as a HANDOVER SHEET (from E2), three lines:
  - (1) Denver called about your backup guard · expires 11:00;
  - (2) Roster at 15 with a two-way call-up pending · before Wednesday's game;
  - (3) Owner's note: under the second apron by the deadline · Week 14.
- Each line has "Take this one / Hand back".
- An initials field with "Sign". The signature is the act. Beside it: "Signing makes these clocks
  yours."

**4. OCCUPY.**
- On signing, all of these change on ONE frame:
  - the header splits into "World clock Tue 09:13" and "Your clock · started 09:13";
  - taken matters show running countdowns (amber fill, black text);
  - a ledger line: "You gave up: the outside view. You got: 3 matters, 3 clocks, the pen";
  - the RECORDED strip: "signed KM as GM · 09:13:06".
- One act is available on Denver: "Take their second / Decline". Terms: "2029 second for your
  backup guard ($2.7M, authored). If he goes: payroll $220.1M, under the World's second apron,
  roster 14 (computed)."
- Or toggle "×60 · AUTHORED" to let 11:00 pass. The countdown becomes a dashed "LAPSED", and the
  record gets "Tue 11:00 · NO ACT · Denver's offer lapsed — against the seat held by KM".
- The address updates: `bow://boston-year-two/w9/front-office?as=gm:KM`.

**5. LEAVE (three backs).** A small back cluster offers:
- "Leave the seat". This must be a handover (one editable line per open matter, then sign) or
  "Vacate · matters revert to the owner". Either is recorded.
- "Rewind". Navigation back to the arrival, with no change to anything.
- "Back to the branch point". Enabled only if you cut a branch in state 2.

**6. HOME AGAIN.**
- The hall returns, but it is not the same:
  - The World presence shows your act on its face: a small green tick "Denver: took their
    second · 09:14", or a dashed "lapsed 11:00".
  - If you pressed "Keep this", a NEW small presence hangs at the end of the row: "Kept · Boston
    under the second apron? · follows Reality", with a live hand.
  - If you cut a branch, a new two-hands face "your pull · $X" appears.
- A one-line summary: "While you were in the seat: 1 act recorded in your World. Reality: no
  change you caused (it doesn't take your acts)."
- Offer "Let Monday come without me": the World face advances to Week 10, and any open matter
  that lapses is recorded "against the seat · vacant" if you vacated, or "against KM" if you are
  still holding it.

## Look — the Browser shell's own language (not the research cream, not any vertical's skin)
- Ground #F7F7F4 near-white; ink #111111.
- Hairlines #CFCFCA; the drawers are #EFEFEA.
- Colour carries ONE meaning each:
  - ultramarine #2B3FD6 = the answer;
  - fork orange #C2410C = a branch or pull;
  - green #007A33 = yours (seat and acts);
  - amber #E8A317 (fill only, black text) = your running clocks.
- Type:
  - "Inter Tight" for UI;
  - "Instrument Serif" italic for answer sentences;
  - "IBM Plex Mono" for the address, provenance and clocks' digits.
- Clock faces are black line drawings on the near-white ground.
- No cards: separators are hairlines and white space.
- Motion: 300–600ms eases. The hall recedes up on arrival and returns down on home.
