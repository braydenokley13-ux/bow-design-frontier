# ENTER tournament — what changes when an observer becomes a participant

BOW Browser Founding Frontier · Wave 1 · 29 Sep 2026 · design reading of rendered prototypes judged by
independent critics; no person has used any of this. Board: `prototypes/EnterTournament.html` (seven
lanes into one seat). ENTER also appears inside every model board: P1 (sit then sign), P2 (take the
seat at a cut), P3 (warrant, standing line), P4 (redeem an Offer; answer a ring), P5 (the edge of the
mechanism is where a seat begins), P6 (your seat is what you may lift), P7 (sit where she sat).
Sources: research C §2.1, D C1, B §2.3, H §H3–H5, F §2.9; Wave 2's ENTER tournament (Handover winner;
Clock Starts runner-up; Narrowing and Descent killed).

## 1. The question
ENTER must mean more than opening 3D. It is the semantic transition OBSERVER → PARTICIPANT. Does it
change identity, role, information, authority, responsibility, time, place, available actions? What
does the person give up? How does leaving work? Could an AI agent enter the same way?

## 2. The shared seat
A fork of the recorded moment "Deadline week · 2 Feb 2026, before the first report · the tax bill is
the problem"; Boston's decision-maker; three open matters (authored); one available first act (shed a
bench salary with a second-round pick — authored, never the real move). Before ENTER the observer can
open SINCE (what happened later); after ENTER the desk knows only what was knowable then.

## 4. What the critics found (both played every lane; `critique/CRITIC_1_MEDIUM.md`, `CRITIC_2_PLATFORM.md`)

**Critic 1 (medium) ranking:** E0 Handover > E4 Ring > E5 Take a Face > E2 Seal > E3 Offer > E1 Sit, then
Sign > E6 Lever.
- E0: "REFUSED every line is taken or handed back. A blank line would be a choice nobody made."
- E4: "RANG OUT The default stands … Nobody acted, so nothing was chosen."
- E5: "REFUSED the League's face is held by the League office … only an unheld face is a seat."
- E1: "Ledger check: gave-up list = frosted panes (4 = 4)" — spatial dressing of E0; the Room already
  does it.
- E6: its own strip says "Nothing about who you are changes."
- Method critique: seven lanes ending in the same act judge ENTER by its ceremony, not by what it
  changes about acting. Owed matters never came due, so the lanes do not differ in consequence.
- Bug: E0 marks "A club has called … Who it is: withheld until you answer." as *answered* while the
  club stays withheld.

**Critic 2 (platform):** seven entry ceremonies means seven things to learn; a platform needs ONE entry
protocol whose carriers can be swapped. E3 works as a capability token; E4 plus standing orders is
delegation; E6 is the cheapest. The matrix row "An AI agent would enter by" (warrants with limits and an
expiry) is the most useful platform text on the board. The "Survives voice / spatial / screen reader"
column holds authored claims beside a strip marked computed — nothing was tested.

## 5. Verdict

**ENTER is a protocol with carriers, not a ceremony: GRANT → REDEEM → LEASE.**

| Lane | Verdict | Becomes |
|---|---|---|
| E0 Handover | **CANON (core)** | Entry = taking named open matters; a blank line is refused; lapses are recorded as NO ACT; leaving is a handover or vacate |
| E3 Offer | **CANON (rule)** | Authority arrives only as an Offer, redeemed by a present person in chrome only BOW draws; the address never carries it |
| E2 Seal | **CANON (rule)** | Hindsight is a stamp that rides every act and fork; never a gate |
| E4 Ring | **CANON (second mode)** | The seat calls you; picking up is entering for the call; standing orders answer while you are away; silence has a receipt |
| E5 Take a Face | **CANON for multi-party acts** | A seat is the unheld face of a shared paper; the League's face is not yours |
| E1 Sit, then Sign | **FOLD into the Room** | The spatial view of E0 (sit is free rehearsal; sign frosts what the seat cannot read) |
| E6 Lever | **KILL as ENTER** | Kept as the Pull instrument ("draft, not forked") |
| The board | **PARK** | Its job — an evaluation instrument — is done |

The visible sign of having crossed is not any one lane's animation. It is two things both critics
picked out on other boards: Jurisdiction's **standing line** ("your word counts for: signing, trading,
payroll · not for: approving trades, setting the tax line") and Guest's **five-part threshold** (who
offers · which seat · what you will see · what you give up · what stays private).

**What ENTER changes** (the eight dimensions, as the canon composes them): IDENTITY (your ink and name
enter the record) · ROLE (the seat) · INFORMATION (narrows to what the seat may know; hindsight
stamped) · AUTHORITY (the lease; whose word counts for what) · RESPONSIBILITY (named matters with clocks;
NO ACT recorded) · TIME (your clock beside the World's; you cannot scrub) · PLACE (optional: the Room
view seats you at the desk) · ACTIONS (the instruments change from reading to acting).

**An AI agent enters by the same protocol:** a warrant the principal signs (scope, limits, expiry,
act-rate budget), a seal that names model and version, refusals that name rule / warrant / budget, and
acts stamped by the agent, never as the person.

**Still untested:** whether signing feels like a EULA to a real person (Wave 2's open question stands);
whether owed matters coming due make the difference the critic says the identical act hid. The
convergence prototype `OneRecord.html` runs the canon on one record (redeem an Offer → the Ring →
standing line → the paper).
