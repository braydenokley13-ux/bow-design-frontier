# E · Atlas, Scale and Composition: parts with owners
RESEARCH · 2026-09-29 · design reading, not evidence of use

## 0. The claim

A system should reach a person as **parts with owners**, not as a picture. Every part says where it came from. Every coupling is a claim with a source: threads are things, knots are claims. Every lever says whose hand it is in. Every relation to a larger system is a tab on the part, never a line to a node. You learn what a mechanism is made of by taking a part out and where it stops by pushing, and **the edge of a mechanism is where a seat begins**: rule-governed motion ends and someone's authority starts. Scale is not zoom; it is following a named relation with one quantity pinned. Composition is an act whose lines other systems hold, in five kinds of "yes". Where no reviewed system exists, the compile shows itself as unfixed: tracing paper over observed ground, whose only knobs are what it admits it guessed. The incumbent truth grammar labels values; this labels structure and authority.

## 1. What the incumbents get wrong or leave out

- **Mechanism is one hand-built board with a label.** Four authored rules, one slider, "re-skinned for parcel hubs". The stamp says "assembled from 6 components" but nothing is assembled in view, and GENERATED STRUCTURE is one flat stamp over a mix: reviewed parts, generated wiring, observed inputs, guessed parameters.
- **The edge is dressed as physics.** "At 96 min the crew's limit runs out" hides a decision: 14 CFR 117.19 lets the captain and airline extend a duty period up to 2 h for unforeseen circumstances, and the crew's report time is not public (the board authors 13 h). The repair added one lever (reserve crew); extending, swapping aircraft and releasing the hold have no holder.
- **Scale by metaphor.** The Descent put nested altitudes over non-nested kinds and lost to a list with the same information and actions. The Lens had the right instinct (sheets of different kinds: possession, five-man unit, roster money, deal, memo, owner's budget) but one verb, "because", hides every relation; sheets stack rather than stay live; there is no rule or league sheet; nothing pushes across sheets.
- **Borders are variables; the Handshake is two-party.** "Signed messages only" never says whose word counts per line, which kind of yes is missing, what stays behind when something crosses, whose clock the wait runs on, or when you must go elsewhere. Boston's real trade has four parties and a calendar.
- **Open world is placeholders** ("[from the feed]"): no design for how compiling feels, what temporary looks like, or how a sketch becomes reviewed.

## 2. Concepts

### E1 · THE BENCH: progress is provenance (Part 1, the system assembling)
- **Interaction.** (1) The person types or speaks "Why are airline delays spreading?" BOW restates it as a claim to test ("delay at one airport shows up later, elsewhere") and names the dynamic, PROPAGATION THROUGH SHARED RESOURCES; two rejected neighbours sit beside it as tabs (AIR-TRAFFIC PROGRAMS, AIRLINE-WIDE OUTAGE). (2) An empty frame (time axis, rows BOS, PHL, ORD) fills as parts arrive with their origin printed on them: reviewed parts solid, with reviewer and version (rotation, turn buffers, duty clock, connection bank); tonight's ORD hold as a thin slip, OBSERVED if a feed is bound, else AUTHORED "typical day"; couplings stippled (GENERATED) until someone reviews the recipe; a missing input as a dashed socket ("crew report time: no public source"). (3) A count line ticks: "4 reviewed · 3 generated couplings · 2 assumed · 1 gap". "Run what you have" works from the first second. (4) The bench slides away, the frame becomes the Loom, and the count line remains as the door to E3. Assembly is never slowed for show; the bench lingers only while something is being generated. *Boston:* "Why did Boston trade a starter?" fetches the roster ledger (RECORDED), CBA rules (REAL engine), tax brackets (AUTHORED) and other clubs' interest (dashed).
- **Canonical vs representation.** Canonical: the *recipe* (part versions, bindings, generated couplings, assumptions), content-addressed, kept as the compile receipt. The choreography only redraws.
- **Segments.** DISCOVER, ARRIVE, ORIENT.
- **Scale.** A system is a recipe over a catalogue: billions of systems are billions of small recipes [estimate]; the scarce object is the reviewed part.
- **Cross-domain.** Chemistry: reaction steps and a thermal bath; socket = unknown rate constant. Supply chain: lead-time queue, buffer, single source; socket = allocation policy.
- **Label.** PROPOSED PLATFORM CAPABILITY. AI: SMALL / CHEAP MODEL (question to dynamic and part shortlist); NO AI REQUIRED (typed-port assembly); FRONTIER MODEL OCCASIONAL when a coupling has no declared port.
- **Fails if** assembly is theatre and provenance becomes fatigue.
- **Test.** 12 adults, 6 teens, clickable: Bench vs a one-line footer ("4 reviewed parts, 3 generated links"). Ten minutes on: which part was made up? which number would you not trust? Fails if the footer does as well.

### E2 · THE LOOM: threads are things, knots are claims, pull a part to learn what it is for (Part 1, the mechanism)
- **Interaction.** (1) The frame becomes a time-space chart, the Ibry/Marey train graph (Tufte, 1983): rows are airports, x is time. The aircraft's day is one zig-zag thread BOS→PHL→ORD→PHL→BOS; turns are flat runs (dark = minimum, light = slack); the crew is a bar ending at a limit tick; the PHL bank is a vertical band that passenger threads join and leave; the ORD hold is a hatched closed band on its row (a socket to weather); the schedule is a ghost thread. (2) Aircraft, crew and passengers are threads. Each turn, duty limit and connection is a knot whose stamp says where the coupling comes from ("13 h: simplified from 14 CFR 117 Table B; report time UNKNOWN"). (3) **Push.** Drag the hold: the thread shears right, slack absorbs, and at the edge the crew bar meets its tick and leg 4 snips. The edge wears a hand: "a person can extend this, up to 2 h". Tap it: *who holds it?* opens the crew desk as a practice seat (an authored day; nobody acts on tonight's airline). (4) **Unbolt.** Press-hold a part and the run repeats without it inside a pencil frame ("part removed: not how the network is"). Lift the aircraft rotation (a fresh aircraft each leg) and the spread stops. Roles compute as labels and depend on the shock: SPREADS (aircraft), ABSORBS (turn slack), CUTS (crew clock; "at 40 min it does nothing"), CONVERTS minutes into missed connections (bank). That answers the question: one aircraft flies four legs. (5) **Levers by holder:** captain and airline extend; the airline swaps aircraft; ATC releases the hold; the traveler rebooks. (6) Toggle "typical day / your flight": their leg and the inbound aircraft's thread stay bright, the rest ghosts (needs a flight feed; none exists). *Boston:* threads are contracts by season, knots the four lines; the push is a salary crossing one.
- **Canonical vs representation.** Canonical: a deterministic run, a pure function of parts, parameters and hold. Push and Unbolt are structural probes: nothing forks or saves (a fork needs the Council). Only the chart redraws.
- **Segments.** ORIENT, WHY?, WHAT IF? (bounded), ENTER (the edge earns the seat).
- **Scale.** Legible to about 10² threads; beyond that threads bundle (a bundle is a distribution). Not a national overview tool.
- **Cross-domain.** Chemistry: species threads on concentration rows, knots are stoichiometric couplings. Supply chain: trailer, driver and pallet threads; dock slots are knots.
- **Label.** PROPOSED PLATFORM CAPABILITY. AI: NO AI REQUIRED.
- **Fails if** the chart is unfamiliar, "knot" is opaque, or Unbolt teaches "the world is the model".
- **Test.** 10 lay travelers, incumbent leg bars vs Loom: "why is leg 4 late?" then "if each leg had a fresh aircraft, what happens to leg 4?" Fails if fewer than 7 predict correctly with the Loom, or the bars do as well.

### E3 · THE PLATE: turn it over (Part 1, made of what, plugged into what)
- **Interaction.** (1) Flip the Loom. The back is a data plate, like the rating plate on a machine: the parts, each with a truth signature (share of parameters OBSERVED / RECORDED / AUTHORED / GENERATED / UNKNOWN), version and reviewer; the couplings, SOURCED or ASSERTED; and **ports** on the perimeter where it plugs into larger systems (WEATHER from forecasts, SLOTS from air-traffic programs, RULE from FAA duty limits, REBOOKING to airline policy), each marked LIVE, FIXTURE (dated), ASSUMED or EMPTY. (2) Ports are **tabs, not lines**: "GOVERNED BY · FAA duty limits". Tap to step out into that system carrying the pin (96 min, leg 4); the ground changes register; on return, revision clouds ring what changed. (3) "Where would I distrust this?" marks the knot with the most assumptions and fewest sources, computed. *Boston:* the plate of Boston's tax bill lists the CBA (League and players' union), arena revenue (separate business, terms UNKNOWN), media money, contracts (RECORDED), owner's budget (World).
- **Canonical vs representation.** Canonical: registry metadata (versions, reviewers, port bindings). Stepping out is navigation; the address rewrites.
- **Segments.** ORIENT, FOLLOW.
- **Scale.** Tabs render only for the thing in view (about 12); the registry indexes typed relations; no global graph is drawn.
- **Cross-domain.** Chemistry: thermal bath, thermodynamic tables (RECORDED). Supply chain: supplier, customs, carrier.
- **Label.** PROPOSED PLATFORM CAPABILITY (no registry or address protocol exists). AI: NO AI REQUIRED.
- **Fails if** nobody flips it; it becomes fine print.
- **Test.** 10 people asked "would you rely on this?" with one planted assumption. Fails unless 7 flip it unprompted and 5 find the plant.

### E4 · ONE PIN, SIX SHEETS: scale across kinds (Part 2)
- **Interaction, Boston.** (1) **Arrive at the neck, not the top.** A fan asks "Why did Boston trade a starter?" (the June 2025 Holiday deal; fixture, verify). BOW opens the sheet where the answer binds: Boston's payroll against four lines, both numbers shown (cap payroll ≈ $203.6M; tax salary $198,722,406; tax line $200.428M; fixture, verify). The question becomes the **pin**. A band runs from it across the sheet, its width the room left; it pinches where a line is close. (2) Five **hinges** sit on the edge as tabs, each a different relation: PEERS (30 payrolls against the same lines), RUN BY (basketball ops: which seat can move which slab, on whose warrant), PAID TO (Tatum ≈ $58.5M, verify), WRITTEN AS (his contract, a staircase of seasons), BOUND BY (the second apron). Follow one and the new sheet opens beside; the old folds to a thin edge that still reads the pin in its own terms ("league: N clubs within $5M of a line"): Furnas's degree of interest (1986), distance counted in relation-steps. Each sheet is a different drawing (30 ticks; rooms with seats; a career line with recorded moments; a staircase; a plane with verbs), but all are cuts through one ledger, cap dollars, so the pin is a horizontal in each. (3) **Push anywhere.** Drag Tatum's number, the payroll or the plane; every open sheet redraws in pencil. On the rule sheet, when the stack crosses the second-apron plane, verbs are struck: aggregate salaries, send cash, taxpayer MLE (first-round pick frozen; verify). This is the incumbent's Pull, reused; release erases the pencil; "keep as fork" opens the Council. (4) **Where's the neck?** The narrowest point is labelled TIGHTEST HERE (computed). A reverse search on the rule sheet lists acts that get Boston back under a line, each tagged with whose yes it needs: the hand-off to E5. (5) **Back up by MADE BY:** the rule's tab leads to the league in its governance reading, the CBA, League and players' association (runs to 2029–30; verify), where the plane appears with its history.
- **ANSWER: what zoom means across non-nested levels.** In a nested world zoom is magnification and the relation ("inside") can stay unsaid. Here each step is a different relation, and the smallest thing on the path, the rule, outranks the club it binds. So zoom is **a change of subject along a named relation, with one quantity pinned.** (a) Pin a quantity or an act, never "a place". (b) Follow a relation, printed on the tab and reversible; the address line rewrites. (c) Each subject brings its own hands and eyes; the standing line (E5) says which. (d) One ledger underneath: every sheet cuts the same limited quantity (cap dollars; delay minutes; wafer starts), so the pin shows in each and pushes in any. (e) Rules are hinges, not depths: the league makes the rule, the contract feels it, the club answers it, the clubs' answers make the league (Coleman's macro-micro-macro, 1990), so "up" from a rule goes to where it is made. Continuity is identity, not geometry: one state, sheets as pure projections, the pin a set of state paths.
- **Canonical vs representation.** Canonical: one state and its causal engine. Pushes are a pencil overlay, never canon. Sheets are views.
- **Segments.** ORIENT, WHY?, WHAT IF? (Pull), FOLLOW.
- **Scale.** Only the pin's typed edges render (6 to 12 tabs). League sheets use distribution strips (30 clubs or 10⁴ firms), not nodes; sheets load lazily.
- **Cross-domain.** Chemistry: system, reaction, species, rate law, equilibrium constant; ledger is moles or free energy. Supply chain: network, firm, plant, part, contract clause; ledger is wafer starts or days of stock.
- **Label.** PROPOSED PLATFORM CAPABILITY. The rules engine, dated fact store and state-bound spatial surfaces it draws on are REAL CURRENT PRODUCT CAPABILITY; sheets, hinges and neck are not built. AI: NO AI REQUIRED.
- **Fails if** it meets the Descent's fate: a list with linked push beats it.
- **Test.** The Descent's toggle, but the list gets the pin and push too. 12 people: "which level binds?", "if Tatum's number rose $5M, what changes at each level?", "who wrote the rule?" Fails if the list ties or wins on time and accuracy; then keep pin and hinges, drop the drawing.

### E5 · THE INSTRUMENT: an act whose lines other systems hold (Part 3)
- **Interaction, Boston.** (1) Recorded moment, 5 Feb 2026 deadline; you hold the GM seat; Chicago calls about Simons (fixture, verify). The act is a **form**, its lines derived from the rulebooks of the systems it touches: BOSTON accepts (GM warrant); CHICAGO signs (WILL-yes, a seat); LEAGUE approves (RULE-yes); PLAYER consent is drawn struck through because no no-trade clause applies (needs 8+ years' service and 4+ with the team, not in extensions; verify), since an absent line is information; MEDIA has no line, because media never says yes but does learn. A **knowing strip** shows who knows now (Boston ●, Chicago ●, League ○, player ○, media ○) and what a leak would do. (2) A **standing line** runs across the top: "You are in: Boston front office · seat: GM · your word counts for: signing, trading, payroll · not for: approving trades, setting the tax line." You can sign line 1 only. (3) **Rehearse.** "Trade call" runs the rules engine as a dry run, like the public trade machines: YES; NO, naming the rule (second-apron aggregation ban); CAN'T TELL. A rehearsal is recorded as one and crosses nothing. (4) **Send.** The act leaves as an envelope and the ground changes: the page takes the League's register (numbered articles, ruled ledger, no marks); the standing line reads "visiting: League Office · you can see: queue count, this file's status · you cannot: approve"; League hours run, review time UNKNOWN (dashed), Boston's 3 p.m. ET deadline beside it. Three moves: WAIT, GO THERE (observe; only what the League discloses), RE-FORM. (5) **Return.** The yes arrives as a stamp naming the rulebook version; three records (Boston, Chicago, League) show the act from three sides; Boston's ledger, unchanged until the last line filled, moves at once, and E4's sheets show the pin before and after. A non-trade act, "add a home date", needs the arena's free/busy (TIME-yes; Delaware North owns and runs the arena; the Celtics' lease runs to 2035–36, Sportico 2021) and the League's schedule approval.

**BOUNDARY GRAMMAR** (borrows Ostrom and Crawford's rule types, 2005, and makes each visible)

| Person's question | Mark | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Where am I? | GROUND | Each system has its own register (paper, type, verbs) and a standing line. |
| Whose word counts? | WARRANT | Each line names the seat that can fill it and its ground. You fill only your own. |
| What must they give? | YES, five kinds | RULE (check against a published rule; refusal names it; rehearsable) · WILL (discretion, may carry a price; modeled ABOUT, never AS) · TIME (a slot on their calendar; you see free/busy) · MONEY (funds exist; computed) · KNOWING (who must be told). |
| What crosses? | CARRIER | Notice copies (sender keeps it, cannot recall it). Remittance moves (one ledger falls, another rises). Countersignature joins (no act until the last line). |
| Whose clock? | CLOCK | A crossed act runs on the receiver's clock; unknown durations dashed. |
| What may I see? | DISCLOSURE | Content · shape only · count and result · nothing; the far side's rulebook decides. |
| Must I move? | MOVE | When the next unfilled line is not yours: WAIT, GO THERE, RE-FORM. A block always names who, which yes, and what you can change. |

Laws: information leaks, authority is countersigned, money is conserved, time is local. A leak is a notice with no warrant, drawn as a hairline crack in the boundary.

- **Canonical vs representation.** Canonical: the act as signed messages in each destination's log; Boston changes atomically when the last line fills; pending shows as an open matter. Ground, envelope and register are representation. In the spatial Boston the form is the trade file on the owner's desk; a League line is not in the building, so the file leaves by the door.
- **Segments.** ROLE, KNOWLEDGE + AUTHORITY CHANGE, ACT, CANONICAL STATE CHANGES, WHY? (a refusal names the rule).
- **Scale.** Forms are local: an act consults only the systems it names; no global composition graph. The cost is rulebook coverage, which is also the moat.
- **Cross-domain.** Supply chain: a purchase order needs supplier acceptance (WILL), an export licence (RULE) and a carrier slot (TIME); the Handshake board already refuses a 1,200-unit lot for lack of a licence. Chemistry: across a vessel wall matter and energy move, information copies.
- **Label.** PROPOSED PLATFORM CAPABILITY. REAL seeds in the unmerged NBA-World branch: two-owner papers (offer, signed, delivered), the League Office vote record, the CBA rules engine, the Harbor-to-NBA read-only crossing. N-party forms, kinds of yes, dry runs and ground change are not built. AI: NO AI REQUIRED for RULE-yes and the form; WILL-yes is a human seat by lease or a stippled stand-in (SMALL / CHEAP MODEL); FRONTIER MODEL OCCASIONAL if an AI holds a seat.
- **Fails if** it is paperwork for its own sake, the kinds of yes are not perceptibly distinct, or the register change is decoration.
- **Test.** 8 to 10 pairs, live; a human plays the League desk with the real rule. Ten seconds after a block each person must say whose yes is missing, which kind, and what they can do. Passes at 8 of 10 with all three. Also count who rehearses before sending.

### E6 · THE UNFIXED PRINT: a temporary model of the open world (SPECULATIVE FRONTIER)
Nothing here exists. The nearest real thing is BOW Economics' dated, sourced fact store and CBA rules engine: a narrow, hand-verified domain.
- **Interaction.** (1) "Why are semiconductor shortages happening?" BOW says the question is plural: at least three shortages are in the news (AI-driven memory; automotive parts after the Nexperia dispute; the 2021 hangover; trade press, verify). Pick one; the others stay as sibling questions. (2) **Open bench.** Sources arrive as slips clipped to an observed ground (dated series, filed statements), each stamped with a class (PRIMARY DATA, OFFICIAL STATEMENT, REPORTING, ANALYST ESTIMATE), a date and the claim it supports. Unreadable sources sit as blank slips, never paraphrased from memory. Claims cluster under mechanism families (capacity reallocation, inventory drawdown, single-source dependency, capacity lead time, export controls). Reviewed generic parts snap in; wiring and parameters are stippled; parameters with no source stay dashed sockets. (3) **Working model** on **tracing paper** over the observed ground: observed series show through, model lines are graphite, a mismatch is a visible gap. Two representations of one run: a Loom of wafer starts through lead-time queues, and a band chart. A **title block** sits inside the border, so a screenshot carries it: "WORKING MODEL · NOT REVIEWED · compiled 29 Sep 2026 14:02 · 11 sources (9 read) · 4 reviewed parts, 3 generated couplings · 7 assumed, 4 unknown · hindcast: none · CHECKED: — · APPROVED: —". A ticking edge shows a 72-hour life [proposed policy]. (4) **The what-if is the assumptions ledger.** The bottom edge is a row of sockets, each an assumption with a range; your only levers are what the compile admits it guessed. Drag "capacity locked in long-term agreements" and the answer moves as a band stamped "scenario on a working model, not a forecast", each socket showing how much the answer leans on it. Past the evidence range: OUTSIDE EVIDENCE. A lever with no part behind it is refused, naming the missing part. (5) **Promote or discard.** Status, as on drawings: SKETCH, ISSUED FOR REVIEW, REVIEWED v1, SUPERSEDED. *Save* keeps a frozen compile, still SKETCH. *Issue for review* routes it to named reviewers who sign **per knot** (SOURCED, AUTHORED by a named reviewer, or STRUCK) and per parameter (replace, accept, leave UNKNOWN) while a hindcast runs against a named recorded window (the 2020–22 shortage). The ledger shrinks ("7 assumed → 2 assumed, 3 reviewer-authored, 2 sourced"); dissent stays on the knot. Approval yields a registry system with lineage to the sketch and a diff of what review changed; holders of the old sketch see "a reviewed version exists: compare". *Discard* leaves a tombstone (assumption list, receipt hash); a shared link still resolves to the frozen sketch, marked DISCARDED BY ITS AUTHOR. Nothing shared silently changes or vanishes.
- **Honest feasibility.** *Reachable:* reading sources, extracting claims with citations, mechanically checking that each quoted figure appears in the fetched text, proposing qualitative structure. Today's ceiling: an LLM bot recovered about 59% of causal links and 66% of feedback loops from text (Hosseinichimeh et al., 2024); a May 2026 benchmark (BEAMS, arXiv 2605.28994) finds AI modelling tools better at discussion and basic qualitative work than at causal reasoning and quantitative error fixing. *Not reachable:* choosing between structures that fit the same history (2021: demand surge, inventory drawdown, plant outages, double ordering; the Commerce RFI, Jan 2022, found buyers' median chip inventory fell from 40 days in 2019 to under 5 in 2021, but the shares stay contested); quantifying unobserved decisions (allocation, contract terms); validating a live event (2026 has no hindcast, so a live model can only inherit validation from a parent that hindcast a recorded event, and must say "not validated for this event"). *Cost (estimate):* 20 sources at about 10k tokens each plus planning and verification passes is roughly 10⁶ tokens and minutes per compile: dollars, not cents [verify prices]; review is hours of expert time. So compiles cache per freshness window and reviews are rare, earned by popular questions. *The real danger* is a plausible wrong model that runs; execution lends authority. So perception is backed by permission: a working model cannot be followed, embedded in a Moment, used as a seat's ledger, or forked into a Council.
- **Canonical vs representation.** Canonical: the frozen compile (source hashes, part versions, couplings, assumptions, model versions, run log), immutable and content-addressed. Tracing paper and the ticking edge present its status. Reality is untouched.
- **Segments.** DISCOVER, ARRIVE, WHAT IF? (bounded), FORK (sketch to canon), FOLLOW (only after review).
- **Scale.** Compiles are per question and cached: billions of questions become a long tail of unreviewed sketches and a short head of reviewed systems.
- **Cross-domain.** This is the supply-chain case. Chemical equilibrium would compile from well-reviewed parts with an almost empty ledger, a useful contrast. Airline is E1, where reviewed parts exist.
- **Label.** SPECULATIVE FRONTIER. AI: FRONTIER MODEL CONTINUOUS during compile (agentic, minutes) plus human review; NO AI REQUIRED after it.
- **Fails if** people trust the animation, the compile is confidently wrong, or reviewers cannot be found at the rate needed.
- **Test.** Wizard-of-Oz, no compile. An analyst hand-builds a working model of the 2021 shortage (hindcastable) in two skins: polished dashboard plus footnote, or tracing paper, title block and ledger. Plant two flaws (a wrong-sign coupling; a single-source claim shown as consensus). 12 analysts, 12 laypeople; measure flaws found and 7-point reliance. Fails if the second skin does not lower reliance or raise detection.

## 4. What real runtime would have to exist

1. **Executor with ablation and edge search:** disable a part, re-run, diff; binary search along a parameter. Deterministic, cheap, testable. Build first.
2. **Parts contract and registry:** typed ports, versions, per-parameter truth state, source, reviewer signature; recipes content-addressed. Restraint per CLAUDE.md section 12: hand-build the airline (P1) and Boston (P2) separately; extract only when a third system, semiconductors, forces the shape.
3. **Typed relation index and address protocol:** tabs are typed edges to addresses; the address rewrites on each hop.
4. **Seat, warrant and disclosure model:** disclosure modes, envelopes as signed messages, N-party forms derived from rulebooks; extends the NBA-World branch's two-owner papers and League Office.
5. **Domain rule engines as reviewed parts:** the CBA engine exists; FAA duty limits do not. Each is a scarce verified asset.
6. **Feeds with as-of stamps and licences:** flight and weather; licensing terms UNKNOWN.
7. **A causal engine yielding touch sets and room:** the Wave 3 engine, hardened.
8. **Compile artifacts and review workflow:** compile logs, per-knot review, hindcast harness, tombstones, freshness windows, a reviewer economy. The compile pipeline comes last and stays SPECULATIVE.

## 5. What would falsify this area's thesis

- A one-line footer matches the Bench and Plate on recall and calibrated distrust: provenance-as-structure is over-built.
- Lay users cannot predict what removing a part does, or conclude from Unbolt that the world is the model: the Loom teaches the wrong lesson.
- A list with pin and push ties the sheets: scale needs hinges, not a drawing.
- People cannot say, ten seconds after a block, whose yes is missing, which kind, and what to do: kinds of yes is analysis nobody wants.
- Polished and tracing-paper working models draw the same reliance: temporariness is not perceptible and only permission can enforce it.
- No compile reaches hindcast-credible quality on a contested question: E6 collapses into the incumbents' model landscape (competing models, BOW picks none).
- Testers insist on "up" and "down" whatever the sheets say: "change of subject" is a false theory of scale.
