# P7 · THE PARTING — radical Browser: navigate by disagreement across many worlds; forks that live

File: `prototypes/Parting.html` (+ `.steps.json`). Read first: `briefs/00_PRIOR_ART.md`,
`briefs/PROTOTYPE_CONTRACT.md`, `prototypes/_shared/boston-fixture.js` (`world`, `moments`,
`forks`), research `research/H_RADICAL_INTERFACES.md` §H2 and "H2 mandatory builder states"; D §C4
(the seam); A §F (the Fracture); B §2.6 (lockfile composition, closure).

## Exact design problem
Two halves of one model.
(1) THE PARTING: don't compare two worlds side by side — go to where they first part. Lay many
worlds over each other: what never moves is the rule; what spreads is the choice. The rule appears
as a shape made of people's choices.
(2) THE LINEAGE (the fork frontier): a WHAT IF? that does not feel like duplicate / scenario /
preview. A fork persists for years, accumulates its own history, hosts people and agents, is shared
and public, forks again, is graded by Reality as Reality arrives, and composes with other systems
(its Boston plays in a League whose other clubs are pinned to Reality). Design the mental model that
can grow to that scale.

## User
A class of 12 pairs at one door (grades 5–6, fictional names on shared screens); a fan who follows a
public fork for years.

## System
Part 1: the World door "Year Two · Week 6 · Golden State calls about Derrick White" with 12 pairs'
worlds. They are SCRIPTED STAND-INS — stipple them GENERATED and say "scripted for this prototype"
in the reveal layer; compute their payrolls/taxes from their choices with the World rule (flat 1.5×
over $200M; hard limit $250M). Choices at the door: keep White; take Butler (the recorded act); a
counter (authored: a cheaper wing, $41.0M in, illustrative). Part 2: the public fork "Keep No. 1 —
Fultz (diverged 17 Jun 2017)" by "[author]" (fixture `forks`) as a nine-year-old lineage (its
recorded history inside the fork is AUTHORED, illustrative — say so) and Reality's recorded moments
(fixture) arriving against it.

## Mandatory states
1. **Sealed door.** The moment named by its question; no crowd, no counts: "You'll see how other
   worlds went after you act."
2. **Your act.** The moves, unranked; you commit; your dot is fixed.
3. **The sheet opens.** One line per DERIVED fact (payroll, projected tax, wins through Week 9);
   dots for worlds, yours ringed; given facts absent; facts on which all worlds agree despite
   different choices are drawn OUTLINED as rules ("same in every world").
4. **Pile-up.** Dots bunch just under the World's tax line; tap the pile: the rule appears with its
   source ("World rule: flat 1.5× over $200M, authored"). No explanation of behaviour — the teacher
   names the economics (a small "teacher's line" in the reveal layer: "Ask: why are so many worlds
   just under this line?").
5. **Where they part.** Tap a spread line: it scrolls to the first act where dots separate, in plain
   words (the crux).
6. **Two worlds, one crux.** Pick two dots: the Browser walks to the last place they agreed, then the
   first place they parted; offer "adopt her value for this assumption" and re-run: did the gap
   close? If not, "that was not the crux".
7. **Sit where she sat.** Enter another pair's world at its parting with their knowledge and none of
   yours; a ledger of what you gained and lost; fictional names.
8. **The lineage (fork as a place that lives).** Open "Keep No. 1 — Fultz · by [author] · diverged 17
   Jun 2017" as a LINEAGE, not a file: its own clock (nine seasons, authored), its own recorded
   history in the author's ink, inhabitants (seats held: the author as GM; a second person as owner
   by lease; one seat held by an AGENT under a warrant — its seal says model and version, never a
   face), sub-forks forked from it ("… · Fultz traded in 2019 · by [another author]") hanging from
   their divergence points, and a PUBLIC stamp. Never a popularity number.
9. **Reality arrives against the lineage.** Reality's recorded moments walk through it one by one
   (12 May 2025 Tatum's Achilles; late Jun 2025 apron summer; 5 Feb 2026): each gets a seam verdict
   against the fork's declared assumptions — HELD / DIVERGED / IMPOSSIBLE / RE-DECLARE — computed
   from typed assumptions (e.g., "the fork assumes Tatum is not on Boston" → Reality's Tatum injury
   is "not in this world": DIVERGED is not a failure). "No world here had this" appears when
   Reality leaves every world's range.
10. **Composition and closure.** The fork's Boston plays in a League: "League · Year 9 · Boston := this
    fork · 29 clubs := Reality (pinned)". Try a trade with Philadelphia: refused until Philadelphia is
    pinned or declared ("an address is well-formed only when closed" — the rule named). Declare it;
    the trade runs inside the fork.
11. **Fork again, from inside.** Make your own sub-fork from a point in the lineage: it inherits the
    lineage's history up to there (shared prefix — say "no history copied"), gets your ink and stamp,
    and hangs from the lineage at its divergence point.

## Art direction — "Field notebook on graph paper"
Squared paper, pencil strata, dots like specimen pins, a lineage drawn as a strata core or a
bound notebook with pages that branch — NOT a tree/network diagram of nodes and edges. Reality's
tick in solid black; forks each in their author's ink. Fonts: e.g. "Crimson Pro" + "JetBrains Mono".

## Capability labels
REAL CURRENT: /board class reveals in BOW Economics Live (a class result on a projector); the act-log
fold with SHA-256 (Live World page, prototype-grade). PROPOSED: sealed door, the sheet, pile-ups,
first parting, crux concession, sit-where-she-sat, lineages with inhabitants and sub-forks, seam
verdicts, composition with pinned clubs and closure. SPECULATIVE: agents holding seats in public
forks at scale; forks persisting for years with runnable archived rulebooks. AI: parting,
agreement, pile-ups, seam verdicts NO AI REQUIRED; naming a crux in words SMALL / CHEAP MODEL (use
authored text here); scripted worlds for thin doors would be FRONTIER MODEL OCCASIONAL, offline,
GENERATED.

## Prohibited
A leaderboard or "best world"; an average line; a tree or network diagram of forks; headline
percentages ("62% swapped"); showing the crowd before the person acts; popularity counts on forks.

## Acceptance criteria
All 11 states in the steps file; the crowd is never visible before the act; agreement drawn as rule
(outline) vs spread as choice; lineage clearly a living place with inhabitants and sub-forks; closure
refusal names its rule; harness clean at both viewports.
