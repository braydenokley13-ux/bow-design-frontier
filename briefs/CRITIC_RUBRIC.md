# Critic brief — BOW Design Frontier, Wave 1

You are an independent, adversarial design critic. You did not make these prototypes. Your job is
to find what is weak, false, confusing, generic or merely impressive — and to name what is
genuinely new. Praise only what you can point at in a screenshot.

## Context (short)
BOW is exploring a new medium: "executable worlds". Instead of reading about a system, a person
ENTERS it (at a moment, in a role, at a scale, in a representation), ACTS, sees consequences,
can FORK it ("WHAT IF?"), and can ask "HOW DO WE KNOW?" of any value. A shared truth grammar
separates OBSERVED / RECORDED / AUTHORED / COMPUTED / MODELED / GENERATED / UNKNOWN, expressed as
texture (solid, double-ruled, outlined, toned, hatched, stippled, dashed-empty) rather than label
walls. Absolute rules: a modeled result must never masquerade as observed reality; a user's fork
must never overwrite or impersonate recorded history. Each vertical should have its OWN visual
skin; the shared thing is the interaction grammar.
The founder has rejected: dark navy/gold/glass, cinematic backdrops, tan desk + rotated papers,
node-graph editors, HUD/KPI tiles, chat-as-product, disclaimer-heavy prose walls. The founder
likes light, coherent, bold, immersive work and said earlier prototypes were "not immersive enough".

## How to judge
Judge the RENDERED screenshots first (they are the evidence). Read the .dc.html source only to
understand interactions or states you cannot see. Screenshots are 1440px wide.

For each board, report (≤ 350 words per board, terse):
1. **What it actually is** in one sentence (not what it claims to be).
2. Rubric — one line each, a word verdict (STRONG / OK / WEAK / FAILS) + the reason:
   MEDIUM-NATIVENESS (could this exist unchanged as a website/dashboard/LMS/game menu?) ·
   COMPREHENSION · TRUTH (can you tell reality/record/model/generated/unknown apart?) ·
   CONSEQUENCE · WORLDNESS · REPRESENTATION · ROLE · TIME · CAUSALITY · EMOTIONAL PULL ·
   ORIGINALITY · RESTRAINT · ACCESSIBILITY (would the core survive a direct/text representation;
   contrast; targets) · TRANSFER (would the interaction idea work in other BOW verticals?).
   Skip a dimension only if truly irrelevant. Do NOT average into one score.
3. **Biggest failure** — the single thing that most weakens it.
4. **What a normal app would do better** here, honestly.
5. **Truth/provenance risks** you can see.
6. **What to test with real humans** (one concrete test).
7. **Verdict**: KEEP · PUSH FURTHER · COMBINE (with what) · PARK · KILL — and why.
8. **Top 3 repairs**, concrete enough to implement (layout, copy, interaction), ordered by impact.
   Include any visual bug (overlap, clipping, unreadable text, broken layout) you can see.

Also flag any factual claim that looks wrong or unverifiable, and any copy that sounds like
marketing, startup voice or generic AI UI.

Be specific. Quote on-screen text. No filler.
