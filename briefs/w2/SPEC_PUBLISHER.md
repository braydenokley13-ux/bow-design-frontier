# SPEC — Publisher / executable media (four forms)

Question: what happens when the native publishing object is not a page, article or video but an
executable Moment or system? NOT "article + embedded simulation". Each form must change what a
reader DOES and how the thing TRAVELS (share, embed, public fork, continuation into a World,
attribution, provenance). Each: 1440×900, interactive; COMMERCIAL LENS bottom-left; status
bottom-right; one-line thesis ("PUBLISHING, in this form, means …").

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## PB1 · THE STORY WITH A DOOR — sports
File: PubStoryDoor.dc.html
A sports story: "The apron summer: why Boston let Jrue Holiday go" (byline "[staff writer]",
publication "[publisher]", dated "late June 2025 · verify"). Layout: the narrative column (left,
~560px, 5 short paragraphs) and the SYSTEM SPINE (right): Boston's 2025–26 cap sheet at the state
each paragraph describes — as you read (click paragraph or "next"), the spine changes state
(payroll bar vs 2025–26 lines: cap ≈$154.6M, tax ≈$187.9M, first apron ≈$195.9M, second apron
≈$207.8M — reported, verify; Holiday ≈$32.4M, Porziņģis ≈$30.7M — approx., verify). The reporting
is the author's (AUTHORED); the cap math is COMPUTED; the salaries OBSERVED (dated).
THE DOOR: inside paragraph 3, an inline door "Enter the morning before the call →" — the story
dims, and the reader takes the capologist's seat at that moment with sealed hindsight (three
options: trade Holiday / trade Porziņģis / pay the tax and keep both). Their call cuts a branch.
READER BRANCHES: below the story, instead of comments, a thread of published branches, each: author
"[reader]", their call, their declarations (e.g. "Portland takes Porziņģis instead: declared"),
the COMPUTED payroll result, and a "Fork this branch" action. The reader's own branch appears at the
top with "Publish your branch" (attribution, provenance, the address).
Distribution strip: "Share this Moment" (address), "Embed" (a small code line showing the embed
carries provenance), "Continue in a BOW World" (hand-off).
Look: a premium sports-editorial page, light: white (#FFFFFF), black headlines in a condensed
editorial face (Barlow Condensed 800), body in Source Serif 4, the spine in a scoreboard-like mono
(DM Mono). Team accent tiny. Status: DESIGN PROPOSAL.
COMMERCIAL LENS: "Hypothesis: publishers license executable Moments per story (or per season);
readers enter free; reader branches are the publisher's engagement and distribution engine."

## PB2 · THE EXPLAINER INSTRUMENT — business/news
File: PubExplainer.dc.html
Story: "How one fire emptied the shelves" — Harrow Medical (fictional) and its single-source chip
supplier, modelled on the March 2021 Renesas Naka fire (verify). The graphic IS the running system
(the promise-thread drawing, simplified: supplier → stock → Line 2 → five customers). The story
advances in STEPS (buttons/keys: ◀ ▶, 6 steps); each step is an authored STATE of the system with
a caption — the scroll-telling of a live model, not of pictures.
At any step: "Take the controls" pauses the story and hands the reader the system at exactly that
state (e.g. at step 4 — the stock runs out on day 11 — the reader can try "allocate to hospitals
first" or "broker buy"); a banner "You are off the story's line · your branch" appears; "Return to
the story" snaps the system back to the authored state (the story never loses its line). The
reader's experiment can be saved as a branch attached to the article ("Readers tried: [n] branches"
— show as "[count from the publisher]", no fake numbers).
Provenance panel: the story's claims vs the model's computations vs the reporter's sources.
Look: a financial-newspaper graphics desk, light salmon-free (NOT FT pink): pale warm grey
(#F4F3EF), black, one data accent (#0F5FA8). Type: Libre Franklin + Merriweather (body) — or
Source Sans 3 + Source Serif 4. Status: DESIGN PROPOSAL.
COMMERCIAL LENS: "Hypothesis: business publishers pay per executable graphic; the same system is
resold to enterprises as a training/scenario asset (a second revenue line from one build)."

## PB3 · THE DOCUMENTARY MOMENT — history
File: PubDocumentary.dc.html
A documentary "The Summer of 1787" plays as a sequence of shots (a filmstrip across the top; the
current shot large: title cards, a drawn interior of the Assembly Room, narration captions). It runs
on its own (auto-advance every ~3 s, with pause/play). At 16 July the film PAUSES itself: "Your vote,
Mr. Strong." The viewer is handed the Massachusetts vote (Aye — as recorded / No). The film then
CONTINUES ON RECORDED HISTORY regardless ("5–4, Massachusetts divided. Carried.") — while the
viewer's branch appears as a FOOTNOTE TRACK running under the filmstrip, in a different ink: "In
your branch: 5–5. A tie fails. The film continues on the record." At the end, the credits include:
sources (Madison's notes, published 1840; Farrand 1911), actor-model disclosures ("voices are
source-grounded actor models, not the people"), and "Your branch" with its address and "Continue in
BOW" (enter the room itself).
Look: documentary film — the only DARK board allowed here (a cinema: deep warm black #141210, NOT
navy, no glow), shots in muted period colour, captions in white; the footnote track in a clear
paper strip under the film (light), so the branch literally lives outside the film. Type: Cormorant
Garamond (titles) + Work Sans (captions). Status: DESIGN PROPOSAL.
COMMERCIAL LENS: "Hypothesis: documentary producers and streaming partners license 'enterable
moments' per film; schools license the same moments as Native Textbook entries."

## PB4 · THE MOMENT CARD — the unit that travels
File: PubMomentCard.dc.html
The shareable Moment as it appears inside other places (a message thread, a social feed, a news
site). Show three cards side by side in three host contexts (drawn minimally: a chat bubble column,
a feed column, a web article column), each hosting a Moment:
- Sports: "Boston · 17 Jun 2017 · The No. 1 pick. What would you do?"
- Business: "Harrow Medical · 06:10 · The fire. Who gets the last 2,300 chips?"
- Science: "A syringe of NO₂ · Squeeze it. Darker or lighter?"
Each card: a frozen state preview, a door, and ONE decision playable inside the card (no app, no
login): tap → the card expands in place → decide → the card shows your branch stamp + "what
actually happened" (for sports/business: recorded or modeled as appropriate; science: computed) +
attribution line ("Moment by [publisher/creator] · sources ›") + "Continue in the full World".
Also a "Remix" affordance (fork the Moment itself as a creator: new question, same state), shown
as a creator credit chain ("remixed from [creator]").
Distribution questions answered on-card: entering is FREE (no paywall before the door); continuing
into a persistent World may not be.
Look: each card adapts to its host's scale but keeps BOW's grammar (door, stamp, textures); hosts
drawn in neutral light greys; cards white with a single ink. Type: Figtree + Figtree Mono? (use
Figtree + JetBrains Mono). Status: DESIGN PROPOSAL.
COMMERCIAL LENS: "Hypothesis: Moments must be free to enter everywhere (distribution); revenue comes
after the door — persistent Worlds, creator plans, publisher licences."
