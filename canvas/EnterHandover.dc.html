How do we know? Double rule = RECORDED. Written into Boston’s World records by an act: a signature, an act on a matter, a handover, a vacancy. Deadlines and rules are not acts. Hollow double rule = NO ACT. A deadline passed and nobody acted. Recorded as absence, never as a choice, and charged to the seat that held the matter. Outline = AUTHORED. A rule or number someone chose: every deadline marked [authored], the ×60 speed, the $2.7M second, the second-apron rule, the interim’s memos. No model ran. Toned = COMPUTED. Follows from stated rules and state: $222.8M − $2.7M = $220.1M; 15 + 1 = 16 on the roster; an offer that ends at 11:00. Dashed = UNKNOWN. The World does not compute it: how Denver answers, how an agent takes silence, what an empty analytics room costs. Also the outside view you give up on signing. Red is used only for deadlines and their clocks. The interim is a seat with a role, not a person and not a chatbot: its memos are authored text.
ENTER, in this model, means accepting responsibility for what is already in motion: you take each matter or hand it back, you sign, and the signature has a time on it.
