# Browser mental model tournament

BOW Browser Founding Frontier · Wave 1 · 30 Sep 2026 · design reading of nine rendered, interactive
prototypes, each played in headless Chromium by the parent (`critique/BROWSER_TRUTH_RUN.txt`, all clean)
and by two independent critics who judged renders before reading any spec
(`critique/CRITIC_1_MEDIUM.md`, `critique/CRITIC_2_PLATFORM.md`). No person outside this process has
used any of it. A verdict here is a design reading, not proof.

## 1. The Browser Hypothesis Map (written before any prototype existed)

### What all eight researchers converged on without talking (the substrate)

Every model below stands on the same six claims. They are candidates for the runtime, not for a
screen:

1. ◆ **The native address is a CUT, not a page** (B, D, F): one system, one point in its record, a
   valid time, a knowledge edition ("as it was known then"), from one seat. "Now" is not an
   address; a snapshot and a follow are different things (B).
2. ◆ **Authority travels apart from reference** (B Offer, F Warrant, E Warrant, H "affordance
   follows seat", C sign-then-frost). A link can show you anything public; only a present person
   redeeming an offer can hold a seat.
3. ◆ **Press a true mark; typed relations open** (A: Because · Meanwhile · Who · Here · Who says yes
   · What's different; F: TOUCHED · WHY · KNEW · BLOCKS · CHANGES · IF; H3 instruments; E hinges
   and ports-as-tabs). Deterministic, NO AI REQUIRED. This replaces the hyperlink *and* search for
   anything already anchored.
4. ◆ **Absence has kinds** (C: withheld · unbought · unlived · untraced; B: four kinds of nothing; D:
   sealed / not yet; A: the Want; G: Cover). The truth grammar's single UNKNOWN splits.
5. ◆ **A branch is a running world that Reality later grades** (A Fracture, D Seam, B Keeping, H1
   lift, H2 Parting, C unbrick). Never merge; one-way arrival; stitched / pulled / torn / open.
6. ◆ **Every view is a witness of one canonical state** (G Concordance, C Place Manifest parity, F
   claim sets with no free numerals, B chrome only the Browser may draw). Direct (plain text) is
   the spine and the oracle; the AI agent and the screen reader read the same thing.

Economics falls out of these: points are not prompts (F), instances cost a binding, schemas cost
review, domains cost a kit (G), compiles are dollars and review is scarce (E).


### The seven mental models — native object, discovery, orientation, enter, time, role, what if,
address, follow, representation, why it could scale, why it may fail


### M1 · THE INSTITUTION — place-first (prototype P1 `Institution.html`)
- NATIVE OBJECT: a place-state — a building whose every fixture is bound to canonical state (the
  Place Manifest), so absence, investment and history are architecture.
- DISCOVERY: adjacency. Windows, counters and doors show neighbouring systems (the League across the
  water); you reach them by address, never by walking.
- ORIENTATION: posture and sightline — outside maquette → door → rail (observer) → desk (seat).
- ENTER: sit (free rehearsal), then sign with the pen: panes you no longer read frost, the outside
  view is covered, your tray fills. Standing up again restores nothing; leaving is an act.
- TIME: rephotography from a fixed benchmark (plates: Year One, now); residue (wear, mend, scar,
  vacancy); the unmodeled future is a blank plate.
- ROLE: the desk you sit at; the glass says what the seat knows and when each folder opens.
- WHAT IF: unbrick an arch you bricked by an act; scaffold rises; inside your branch the record
  building stays lit across the slip.
- ADDRESS: station or fixture (`boston/ops/ticketing/forecast[0]`), inside the cut.
- FOLLOW: the building remembers — dust on lapsed folders, not banners.
- REPRESENTATION: Room (3D) with Plan and Schedule twins from one manifest; the room answers WHY
  with the room (Touch).
- WHY IT COULD SCALE: rooms compile from typed surfaces; domain kits (every club shares one arena);
  place thins with practice to Plan and Schedule.
- WHY IT MAY FAIL: list parity (Cockburn & McKenzie; the Descent); Chromebook frame time; the
  founder judged the first 3D poor; frost may read as a paywall.

### M2 · THE CUT — time-first (P2 `Cut.html`)
- NATIVE OBJECT: a cut — a sealed cross-section of a system as one knower could know it, in four
  planes: on the books · on the desk · not yet · since.
- DISCOVERY: dated doors on a horizon (what is scheduled; what was open then), named by questions.
- ORIENTATION: the Horizon Rail — the system's own time profile, how far its record reaches, and
  Reality's window, shuttered, always in view.
- ENTER: taking the seat at a cut seals your hindsight; if you already looked, everything you fork
  carries "cut with hindsight".
- TIME: stand, don't travel. Floor, never nearest. Later is refused by the record's own rule;
  asking writes to your private ledger.
- ROLE: the knower the cut is drawn for.
- WHAT IF: a branch born at the cut runs on its own clock (pinned / paced / free); Reality arrives
  and grades it at the seam.
- ADDRESS: system · valid time · knowledge frame · seat · (branch).
- FOLLOW: pinned branches keep walking with Reality; the seam reports without being asked.
- REPRESENTATION: planes and a rail; any representation can be cut.
- WHY IT COULD SCALE: a cut is a computed floor query over an append-only, bitemporal log; nothing
  per cut is stored.
- WHY IT MAY FAIL: curation cost (doors and "asked before the cut" slots are authored); four planes
  overload a 10-year-old; sealing reads as withholding.

### M3 · THE JURISDICTION — system / structure-first (P3 `Jurisdiction.html`)
- NATIVE OBJECT: an act as a form whose lines are held by other systems; the ground you stand on
  always says whose rules apply.
- DISCOVERY: by relation from a pinned quantity (hinges: PEERS · RUN BY · PAID TO · WRITTEN AS ·
  BOUND BY · MADE BY) and by open lines (a line nobody holds is a seat).
- ORIENTATION: GROUND (each system's own register) + a standing line: "You are in … · your word
  counts for … · not for …".
- ENTER: a warrant; you can fill only your own line.
- TIME: every crossing runs on the receiver's clock; unknown durations dashed.
- ROLE: WARRANT per line; five kinds of yes (RULE · WILL · TIME · MONEY · KNOWING).
- WHAT IF: rehearse (dry run against the rulebook, recorded as a rehearsal) and redline (both faces
  re-execute).
- ADDRESS: the form and the sheet, with the pin carried across jurisdictions.
- FOLLOW: hold a paper you have no side in; its clock paces you.
- REPRESENTATION: sheets (different drawings of one ledger) and papers with two faces.
- WHY IT COULD SCALE: forms consult only the systems they name; no global graph; rulebook coverage
  is the cost and the moat.
- WHY IT MAY FAIL: paperwork; kinds of yes not perceptibly distinct; only rulebook-complete systems
  have forms.

### M4 · THE GUEST — nearly invisible (P4 `Guest.html`)
- NATIVE OBJECT: a true mark carried into someone else's surface (an assistant's answer, an
  article's number, a text from a friend, a voice call), plus the Browser's own threshold.
- DISCOVERY: systems come to you — a mark in what you already read, a ring from a seat you hold, an
  offer from a person.
- ORIENTATION: the frame only BOW may draw, at the moment of crossing; kind first ("Recorded.
  Boston. Philadelphia calls about No. 1").
- ENTER: redeem an Offer in BOW-drawn chrome; answering a ring is entering for the length of the
  call.
- TIME: the Round — a finite walk on return that ends "level with the record"; standing orders
  answer while you are away.
- ROLE: seats you hold (capped); standing orders as bounded authority; acts stamped by you / orders /
  agent / default.
- WHAT IF: "hold" — a dry run in a private fork that reads back its receipt before you commit.
- ADDRESS: the Reading (a sentence you can say, with check words) and the Offer (authority, apart).
- FOLLOW: rings and a Standing ledger with end conditions; silence has a receipt.
- REPRESENTATION: whatever the host surface allows; BOW's claim card inside it; the full Browser only
  at thresholds.
- WHY IT COULD SCALE: stateless reads; claim sets cache at every layer; distribution rides surfaces
  people already use.
- WHY IT MAY FAIL: hosts skip the card; value leaks to an API; ring fatigue; the Browser becomes a
  plugin with no home.

### M5 · THE BENCH — discovery / Atlas-first (P5 `Bench.html`)
- NATIVE OBJECT: a mechanism — a recipe of parts with owners, assembled for a question.
- DISCOVERY: a question resolves to a named dynamic; parts arrive on the bench with their origin
  printed on them (reviewed / generated / assumed / gap).
- ORIENTATION: the data plate on the back — parts, couplings (sourced or asserted), ports to larger
  systems as tabs.
- ENTER: the edge of the mechanism is where a seat begins ("a person can extend this, up to 2 h") —
  push to the edge, then take the seat that holds it.
- TIME: the mechanism's own chart (Marey / Ibry time-space).
- ROLE: levers by holder.
- WHAT IF: push (pencil, nothing kept) and unbolt (run it without a part); on an open-world compile
  your only levers are the assumptions it admits.
- ADDRESS: the recipe hash; a compile carries a title block that survives a screenshot.
- FOLLOW: only reviewed systems can be followed; a sketch can be issued for review, promoted or
  discarded (tombstone).
- REPRESENTATION: Loom (time-space chart), plate, tracing paper over observed ground.
- WHY IT COULD SCALE: billions of systems are billions of small recipes over a catalogue of reviewed
  parts; compiles cache per freshness window.
- WHY IT MAY FAIL: a plausible wrong model that runs (execution lends authority); reviewers cannot be
  found at the rate needed; the bench is theatre.

### M6 · TAKE IT OUT — radical: navigation by intervention (P6 `TakeItOut.html`)
- NATIVE OBJECT: the act. Your history is a row of acts you can lift out while the present re-forms.
- DISCOVERY: "what counted" — acts ordered by how much of the present they reach.
- ORIENTATION: the strip is the address; your ink above Reality's ghosted record.
- ENTER: your seat is the set of acts you may lift; another seat's acts are locked ("not your act").
- TIME: causes, not clock — you move through consequences, luck held fixed.
- ROLE: authority is what you can take out.
- WHAT IF: the lift IS the fork; keeping it stamps "cut with hindsight"; later acts that needed it
  turn to dashes.
- ADDRESS: (record hash, omitted acts).
- FOLLOW: clamp a weight — "tell me when Year One's choice stops reaching the score".
- REPRESENTATION: dials with two needles; strip of acts.
- WHY IT COULD SCALE: deterministic counterfactuals memoize on (record hash, omitted act, engine
  version); backward slices bound the cost.
- WHY IT MAY FAIL: luck drift and overdetermination make weights lie; weight reads as blame; only
  rules-derived facts can be lifted on Reality.

### M7 · THE PARTING — radical: navigation by disagreement across many worlds (P7 `Parting.html`)
- NATIVE OBJECT: a door and the crowd of worlds that went through it.
- DISCOVERY: doors where worlds part most (ranked by spread, never popularity).
- ORIENTATION: one line per derived fact; dots for worlds; Reality's tick; yours ringed.
- ENTER: "sit where she sat" — enter another world at its parting with her knowledge and none of
  yours.
- TIME: every world keeps its own clock; Reality's tick walks through the crowd as it arrives.
- ROLE: you act before you see anyone (sealed); then you see where you stand.
- WHAT IF: a fork is a lineage that persists, is authored, hosts people and agents, forks again,
  composes into a League whose other clubs are pinned.
- ADDRESS: content-addressed forks sharing prefixes; first parting computed like a merge-base.
- FOLLOW: follow a door; "no world here had this" when Reality leaves every world's range.
- REPRESENTATION: the sheet (strata of dots), the lineage.
- WHY IT COULD SCALE: sketches per (door, fact), simhash over act sets, shared prefixes — the more
  people fork, the better every door reads (a real network effect).
- WHY IT MAY FAIL: cold start; herding; popularity as authority; trivial agreement.

## 3. How the critics ranked them

**Critic 1 (interaction / medium) — "new medium" evidence:** 1 Jurisdiction · 2 Cut · 3 Institution ·
4 Parting · 5 Guest · 6 TakeItOut · 7 Bench. Strongest: Jurisdiction ("one act crosses seats with their
own clocks; a wrong act is refused by another seat citing the rule; three records exist with no god
view; entering rewrites what 'your word counts for'"). Strangest but potentially most important: Guest
("the Browser stops being a destination; the system calls the seat, and absence is recorded").

**Critic 2 (platform / scale) — verdicts:** KEEP Jurisdiction (protocol layer), Cut, Guest (invocation
spine), TakeItOut (attribution), Parting (lockfile + seam; park the piles), Concordance (the platform
architecture); PUSH Bench; COMBINE Institution as the Room view and KILL it as the navigation model;
PARK the ENTER board. Founder decision: "Is BOW a destination people walk into, or a record protocol:
addressed, signed act logs that any surface renders as checked views? Choose the protocol."

**Where both converged:** "not yet in one place" — every board kept its own record; nine boards drew
nine address bars, nine record columns and one World with three different cash figures. The convincing
flow both named runs across five boards on ONE record.

## 4. Verdicts (parent synthesis — see `briefs/CANON_AFTER_CRITIQUE.md`)

| Model | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **M3 Jurisdiction** | **KEEP — strongest; the ACT grammar** | the only board where a wrong act propagates as a refusal from another seat naming the rule; authority, disclosure and clocks per system; composes without a god view |
| **M2 Cut** | **KEEP — the TIME grammar** (with the seam) | the most original time grammar; the Seam is the one interaction neither a website nor a game can do |
| **Concordance** | **KEEP as substrate** | the view contract that makes every surface a checked witness of one record |
| **M4 Guest** | **PUSH — external invocation + the away loop** | the system calls the seat; standing orders; the Round; claim cards — but scripted, and its truth-in-hosts needs signed claims |
| **M1 Institution** | **COMBINE / KILL as index** | the only place that changes because you acted — keep the Room as ONE view; walking a building is not how a billion systems are found |
| **M6 Take It Out** | **KEEP as an operator** | WHY by experiment; the clamp is a new FOLLOW; not a destination |
| **M7 Parting** | **KEEP the lineage; PARK the crowd** | forks that persist, are inhabited, fork again and compose; piles become herding and popularity at scale |
| **M5 Bench** | **PUSH as the discovery lane** | the honest Want, tombstones and the review ladder are platform primitives; its resolver could not find the one system BOW has |

**The answer is a composition, not a winner.** The strongest Browser is a *record protocol* with an ACT
grammar (Jurisdiction), a TIME grammar (Cut + Seam), a view contract (Concordance), a reach layer
(Guest), operators (lift, fork lineage) and a discovery lane (marks → typed doors; Bench; Want) — on ONE
record per system. The convergence prototype `OneRecord.html` is built to prove exactly that (see
`BROWSER_FRONTIER_WAVE_1.md` and `BROWSER_FOUNDER_PACKET.md` for its result).
