# SPEC — WHAT IF? tournament 2 (five challengers)

Wave 1's working fork (Fork.dc.html — read it) established: a door into a recorded moment, sealed
hindsight, declarations for other actors, COMPUTED consequences, authored guess ranges with a
horizon, unknown events split rather than averaged, a shareable branch address. Wave 2 attacks the
parts Wave 1 did not solve. Each challenger is a different MENTAL MODEL of what a fork is:
- how a fork BEGINS (gesture, not menu),
- how other actors' behaviour is handled without fake omniscience,
- how many MODELS branch from one state and get judged,
- what happens to a PERSISTENT branch as Reality keeps arriving,
- how branching inside a replay can be NOTATED.
"Fork" must never collapse into Save As. A branch is a different possibility history.
All: 1440×900, interactive; forks never wear the record's ink; stamped with divergence point and
author; COMMERCIAL LENS line bottom-left; status bottom-right; a final one-line thesis in small
type ("WHAT IF?, in this model, is …").

## W1 · THE PULL — "a fork begins as a gesture: pull a quantity and watch the rules move"
File: WhatIfPull.dc.html
Subject: Boston Celtics · Reality · 2026–27, payroll ≈ $203.6M; lines: tax $200.428M, first apron
$209.015M, second apron $221.686M (fixture, verify).
Interaction: a large vertical payroll column (the state) against horizontal LINES that are drawn as
strata (tax, first apron, second apron). The user grabs a handle on the column ("Pull payroll")
— a range input styled as a handle, vertical or horizontal — and drags between $180M and $235M. As
they drag, a GHOST branch appears live (hatched column beside the real one) and the consequences
recompute instantly:
- which RULES now apply to Boston (AUTHORED by the 2023 CBA — list them, each appearing/disappearing
  as a line is crossed; e.g. over the tax line: pays luxury tax; over the first apron: can't use the
  non-taxpayer mid-level exception, salary matching tightens, can't acquire in a sign-and-trade;
  over the second apron: can't aggregate salaries in trades, can't take back more salary than sent,
  can't send cash, first-round pick seven years out frozen — mark "simplified · verify");
- the TAX BILL (COMPUTED from AUTHORED simplified brackets: $1.50, $1.75, $2.50, $3.25 per $1 over
  the line in $5M steps, +$0.50 per further step; label "simplified; real brackets are indexed and
  repeat-taxpayer rates are higher — verify"). Show Reality's bill vs the ghost's bill.
- what the pull CANNOT tell you: wins (UNKNOWN — "depends on which players leave; declare a move to
  go further").
Release without committing → the ghost fades (nothing was forked). "Keep this branch" (only
enabled after a pull) → the ghost becomes a stamped branch column with a name field
("Boston −$12.4M · unnamed move") and a required DECLARATION: "Which move gets you there?" with
three options (trade White · waive and stretch [a bench contract] · leave unknown) — the declaration
changes the rule list details (e.g. trade → salary-matching rule applies).
Look: an instrument for rules — warm white (#FAF9F6), strata lines as thick coloured bands (tax
ochre #B7791F, first apron rust #B4461D, second apron deep red #8E1B1B — each also labelled and
patterned so color isn't the only cue), ghost hatched in ink. Type: Manrope + IBM Plex Mono.
Status: DESIGN PROPOSAL.
COMMERCIAL LENS: "Hypothesis: the rule-aware pull is the professional tool (capologists, media,
agents) — licence it; the same pull for fans is free and brings them in."

## W2 · THE COUNCIL — "a fork convenes everyone it affects; each must be declared, modeled or left unknown"
File: WhatIfCouncil.dc.html
Subject: Reality, 5 Feb 2026 — Boston trades Anfernee Simons (~$27.7M) to Chicago for Nikola
Vučević (~$21.5M) (reported in BOW repo sources; verify); Boston's projected tax fell from ~$39.5M
to ~$17M (same source, verify).
Interaction: "What if Boston hadn't made this trade?" — cut the fork at the trade. Instead of a
result, a COUNCIL convenes: a round table drawn from above, with a seat for every actor the fork
touches: Chicago's front office, Boston's ownership, Anfernee Simons (and his agent), Nikola
Vučević, the League office, Boston's coach. Each seat has four chips:
  AS RECORDED (only where logically possible — e.g. the League behaves as recorded) ·
  DECLARE (you state what they do — a short choice list per seat) ·
  MODEL (a bounded model with its basis shown, e.g. "Chicago keeps Vučević: basis — no other
  reported suitor (MODELED, weak)") ·
  UNKNOWN (the seat stays open).
The branch's CREDIBILITY RING around the table fills by composition: solid for recorded, outlined
for declared, hatched for modeled, dashed for unknown — you can SEE how much of the branch is your
say-so vs model vs nothing. COMPUTED consequences that need no one's permission appear in the
center: Boston's tax stays ≈$39.5M (COMPUTED from the source figures); Simons remains on an
expiring deal. Anything depending on an UNKNOWN seat shows as a hole in the center.
"Seal the branch" is disabled while any seat affecting a stated consequence is unset — the design
refuses fake omniscience. After sealing: stamp + address.
Look: a council chamber seen from above — pale stone (#EDEBE6), table as a white disc, seats as
labelled chairs with the chips; ink black; your branch accent a strong violet (#5B3FA0). Type:
Source Serif 4 + Libre Franklin. Status: DESIGN PROPOSAL.
COMMERCIAL LENS: "Hypothesis: councils make forks honest enough for media and education to publish;
the unit is the published branch (free to open, paid to host at scale)."

## W3 · THE FAN — "many models branch from one state; the record keeps score"
File: WhatIfFan.dc.html
Subject: Boston · Year Two (your World), after Week 9 (record at that point: authored "5–4" in the
World; label "World record, authored for this prototype").
Three MODELS branch from the same state and each draws its own fan (forecast cone, like storm-track
cones) of final wins for Year Two's remaining 17 weeks:
- "The World itself" — the World's simulator run forward 200 times (MODELED ensemble; fan = spread).
- "Carry-over rating" — a simple rating updated from World results (COMPUTED from recorded results +
  AUTHORED formula).
- "Your scouts' consensus" — the World's scouting staff (AUTHORED opinions of in-World people; since
  analytics is unfunded, this fan is wide and stippled).
Interaction: the three fans overlap on one chart; toggle each; hover a fan to see its basis and
assumptions. "Advance 4 weeks" (World time) → the World RECORDS results (authored: 3–1, then 2–2,
then 1–3 on further presses, shown as recorded ticks) → each model is SCORED (did the record land
inside its fan? how far off?) and fans narrow. A scoreboard of MODELS (not players): "calls made /
landed / missed". Disagreement between models is itself shown as a band ("where the models
disagree most: Weeks 14–18").
Key idea to make visible: in BOW, models are competing branches from one state, and the World's
record is the referee.
Look: a forecast room — light blueprint-free white (#F9FAFB), three distinct fan textures (hatched,
toned, stippled) in ink with one accent per model, a clean weather-map sensibility. Type: Encode
Sans Condensed + DM Mono. Status: DESIGN PROPOSAL · PLATFORM HYPOTHESIS (model scoring).
COMMERCIAL LENS: "Hypothesis: model providers attach to BOW and are scored in public by the record —
a marketplace of models with reputations; BOW takes a share of model runs."

## W4 · THE LIVING FORK — "a kept branch has to live with a Reality that keeps arriving"
File: WhatIfLiving.dc.html
Subject: your persistent branch from Wave 1: "Keep No. 1 — Fultz · diverged 17 Jun 2017" with
declarations phi=Tatum, health=unknown, summer=held.
Interaction: a DIVERGENCE LEDGER — Reality's events since 2017 in order, each with its status in
your branch:
  HELD (by your declaration; outlined) · DIVERGED (computed as different; toned) ·
  IMPOSSIBLE (cannot exist in the branch — e.g. "2019 Kings pick → Romeo Langford at No. 14") ·
  UNKNOWN (depends on something the branch doesn't know; dashed) · INDEPENDENT (not affected).
Events to include (observed, dates verify): Jul 2017 Hayward signs (HELD by declaration); Aug 2017
Irving trade (HELD); May 2018 East finals Game 7 (UNKNOWN — roster differs); Jun 2019 Kings pick No.
14 (IMPOSSIBLE); Jun 2022 Finals (UNKNOWN); Jun 2024 title (UNKNOWN); May 2025 Tatum's Achilles
(DIVERGED — in your branch Tatum is a 76er: the injury happens in Philadelphia's lane, if at all →
UNKNOWN for Boston); late Jun 2025 apron summer trades (UNKNOWN — depends on a roster the branch
doesn't know).
"Reality arrives" button: appends the next real event (5 Feb 2026 Simons↔Vučević; then "[next event
from the Reality feed]") and checks it against the branch; each arrival either fits silently or
raises a RE-DECLARE flag ("Reality event depends on Boston's 2025 roster, which your branch holds
unknown — declare, or let the branch carry the gap").
BRANCH HEALTH strip: how much of the branch rests on declarations vs unknowns vs computed; an
"aging" indicator (years since divergence) and the guess horizon (3 seasons) marked.
Actions: "Re-declare" (opens a small choice), "Let it carry the gap", "Freeze the branch" (stop
receiving Reality; it becomes a historical branch with no NOW).
Look: a ship's log / maintenance ledger — light buff (#F3EFE6) with ruled lines, the branch's
orange (#C2410C) as its ink (continuity with Wave 1), Reality in black. Type: Courier Prime (log
entries) + Libre Baskerville (headings). Status: DESIGN PROPOSAL · SPECULATIVE FRONTIER.
COMMERCIAL LENS: "Hypothesis: living branches cost ongoing compute and data; they are the natural
subscription unit for fans and analysts who want 'their' timeline kept alive."

## W5 · THE VARIATION TREE — "branching inside a replay, written as notation"
File: WhatIfVariations.dc.html
Subject: your World's recorded last two minutes vs Denver (fixture outline: from 105–108 with 2:00
left to a 112–115 loss; "Butler for three" twice; the last possession: Denver, a switch leaves a big
on a guard, "Jokić scores"). The individual possessions are AUTHORED for this prototype in the shape
of the fixture — label them "authored in the shape of the Worlds-branch fixture".
Borrow the chess annotation book: the MAINLINE is the recorded possession sequence written as
compact notation, one possession per line, e.g.
  P13 DEN 2:00 · Murray pull-up 2 · 105–110
  P14 BOS 1:41 · Butler 3 · 108–110
  … (7–8 lines to the end)
Beside it, a half-court coach's diagram shows the selected possession (X's and O's, arrows).
Interaction: click a possession → the diagram shows it; press "Play a variation here" → choose an
alternative decision (e.g. on P20 defence: "drop instead of switch" / "double Jokić" / "foul with 6
seconds") → a VARIATION line appears indented in parentheses under the mainline, in the branch ink,
with a MODELED outcome as a probability RANGE ("Denver scores 42–58% · modeled"), and the next
lines continue as modeled notation (dashed). You can nest a sub-variation. Two variations can be
compared side by side. Variations are ANALYSIS (ephemeral) until "Promote to branch" makes one a
persistent Lab branch with a stamp — the distinction between thinking and forking is explicit.
Look: an annotated chess book / coaching manual — light paper (#FBFAF7), mainline in bold black,
variations in the branch blue-violet (#3F3D9C) within parentheses, diagram in fine ink. Type:
Crimson Pro (commentary) + Noto Sans Mono (notation). Status: DESIGN PROPOSAL · the recorded Court
game EXISTS TODAY as a fixture in the BOW Worlds branch.
COMMERCIAL LENS: "Hypothesis: coaches, analysts and broadcasters pay for variation analysis on real
film; for fans, variations on a shared Moment are the free, viral hook."
