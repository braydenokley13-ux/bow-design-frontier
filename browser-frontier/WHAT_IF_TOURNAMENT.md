# WHAT IF? tournament — forks that are worlds, not duplicates

BOW Browser Founding Frontier · Wave 1 · 29 Sep 2026 · design reading of rendered prototypes judged by
independent critics; no person has used any of this. Sources: research H (Take It Out, The Parting,
The Paper's redline, On Call's hold), D (the Seam), A (the Fracture), B (the Keeping; lockfile
composition), C (unbrick), E (push / unbolt; working-model assumptions), F (the Receipt; the Position
model); incumbents Council, Living fork, Pull (Wave 2). Prototypes: P7 `Parting.html` (lineage), P6
`TakeItOut.html`, P2 `Cut.html` (seam), P1 `Institution.html` (unbrick), P3 `Jurisdiction.html`
(redline), P5 `Bench.html` (unbolt; assumption sockets), P4 `Guest.html` (hold).

## 1. The question
WHAT IF? must not feel like *duplicate*, *scenario* or *preview*. It must grow to forks that persist
indefinitely, accumulate their own history, host humans and agents, are shared and public, fork again,
and interact with compatible modeled systems. What mental model survives that?

## 2. The mental model this wave converged on

> **A fork is a lineage: a running world with an author, a clock, inhabitants and declared
> assumptions, that Reality later grades — never a copy of a file.**

Its properties, each carried by at least one prototype:
1. **Birth is an act, not a save.** A fork begins where someone acts differently (a lift, an unbrick,
   a redline, a different choice at a cut) — never "Save As". (H1, C, H4, D)
2. **It keeps its own clock** — pinned to Reality's dates, paced by your acts, or free on its own
   calendar (D's coupling). A branch cut at NOW can run ahead of Reality, and says by how much.
3. **It declares what it assumes**, as typed predicates over addresses (A, B) — the only honest
   substitute for omniscience. The Council (Wave 2) convenes every actor an act touches, each set
   Recorded · Declare · Model · Unknown.
4. **Reality arrives one way and grades it** at the seam: stitched (held) · pulled (diverged) · torn
   (impossible) · open (re-declare) — computed, never a score. An assumption index fires only for the
   branches that assumed otherwise (A's Fracture), so cost follows dependents, not forks.
5. **It is authored and signed.** The root carries the author's key; renames never re-attribute; a fork
   cut after seeing the outcome is stamped "cut with hindsight" (stamped, not forbidden — D).
6. **It hosts people and agents.** Seats inside a fork are held by lease; an agent's seat shows its
   seal (model, version, warrant), never a face (F).
7. **It forks again** — sub-forks share the prefix (no history copied) and hang from their divergence
   point; lineage is content-addressed (first parting of two forks is a merge-base walk — H2).
8. **It composes.** A forked Boston plays in a League whose other clubs are pinned to Reality or to
   other forks; an address is well-formed only when closed (B's lockfile rule, the Council's rule made
   protocol): trade with Philadelphia and Philadelphia must be pinned or declared.
9. **It is compared by where it parts,** not side by side (H2): walk to the last place two worlds
   agreed, then the first place they parted; concede one assumption and see whether the gap closes.
   Laid over many worlds, what never moves is the rule; what spreads is the choice.
10. **It never merges and never recolours the record.** Its own ink, its frame, its stamp; the record
    stays lit beside it (C: from inside your branch, through its window, the record building stands).

## 4. What the critics found

- **Critic 1 (medium)** — which fork models are not duplicate / scenario / preview: "the Cut's Council
  branch. Every touched seat is Recorded / Declared / Modeled / Unknown, and Reality later grades those
  claims (held / torn / re-declare). The one that grows: Parting's public fork — people plus an agent
  under warrant, fork again from Year 5, and the closure rule when it touches another club." Everything
  else was named honestly: Institution's unbrick is "an instant kept preview"; Jurisdiction's pencil "a
  preview by design" and its redline "a document fork"; TakeItOut "a subtraction scenario that can
  continue" (and, after Phase 2, a new FOLLOW: the clamp follows a causal relationship); Bench "a
  parameter scenario"; Concordance "a copy with a different act"; Guest "a dry run". Parting's crowd
  "is fake" (eleven of twelve worlds scripted) and its lineage clock stops at "Year 9 of 9".
- **Critic 2 (platform)** — Parting's closed lockfile ("29 clubs := Reality (pinned) · read-only";
  "REFUSED · this address is not closed") has package-manager semantics and scales; every real event
  arriving against forks needs authored preconditions (a Fact cost). Dot piles are a popularity
  histogram at a million forks and invite herding. Toy hashes cannot content-address forks; Parting's
  real SHA-256 covered navigation, not state. Lifting a *real* act feeds stand-in inputs into results
  that looked computed. A cached counterparty archetype (Denver) becomes an exploit every fork shares.
- Both: one vocabulary for the seam (three were in use).

**The mental model:** *a fork is a lineage* — a running world with an author, a clock and coupling,
inhabitants (people and agents under warrants), declared assumptions, a closed lockfile address, and a
relationship to Reality that is graded, never merged. It is not a copy: its storage is its divergence;
its identity is (base hash + its own acts); a sub-fork shares the prefix.

**Laws that make it scale:** SHA-256 over canonical acts only · assumptions as typed predicates so an
arriving fact wakes only the forks that assumed otherwise · closure (an address is well-formed only when
every system it touches is pinned or declared) · counterparty models drawn per fork with a seeded draw,
never a shared cached archetype · every real event that arrives against forks carries its preconditions
(and that is a Fact cost BOW pays).

**What is still missing:** a fork that runs a season on its own (both critics); real inhabitants; the
live Reality feed that makes the Seam more than replay.
