# Representation router frontier — one system, many witnesses

BOW Browser Founding Frontier · Wave 1 · 29 Sep 2026 · design reading. Sources: research G
(Representation Router), C (Spatial: Place Manifest; Room / Plan / Schedule), F (claim sets; no free
numerals), B (chrome only the Browser may draw); prototypes `Concordance.html` and P1
`Institution.html`. Economics are in `REPRESENTATION_ECONOMICS.md`.

## 1. The claim

**A view is a witness, not a window.** The Browser keeps one canonical Surface per system — typed
facts (each with its truth token), the acts a seat may take, the rules that refuse them, the clocks —
and every representation (Room, Timeline, Table, Diagram, Map, Direct text, voice, molecules, film)
is a compiled projection of it that obeys one grammar. The router never asks "which view?". It issues
a **Plan** — a lead view, a companion, one sentence of why — and the person keeps swap, pin and pair.
Sameness is **proved, not asserted**: one act number lands in every open view in the same frame, and a
machine check compares what each view says it shows with the record and fails closed.

## 2. The shared grammar every representation obeys (research G §2.0)

1. **Frame**: address; ledger position with fingerprint ("act 42 · 3f9c"); the view's origin
   (hand-built · kit · compiled · creator).
2. **Bound or decor**: a mark that asserts is bound to a state path; everything else is decor; there is
   no third kind ("encode all the facts and only the facts" — Mackinlay, 1986).
3. **Tokens belong to the kernel**: observed · recorded · authored · computed · modeled · generated ·
   unknown (+ sealed future) come from path metadata; a view only realizes them in its modality —
   texture in 2D, material in a Room (research C's Truth Materials: brass double border for RECORDED,
   stencil for AUTHORED, tracing paper with pencil hatch for MODELED, foam-board speckle for
   GENERATED, dust sheet / shadow-board outline for UNKNOWN), a lead word in text ("Recorded:",
   "Estimate:") and in voice. **Words are the grammar's floor** — never colour alone. (The real Boston
   arena already reached for words when material ran out: "NO FORECAST · NOT KNOWN · NOT PLAYED YET".)
4. **Same acts**: every act the seat may take is reachable in every view, or the view names the one
   that has it and hands off in one step.
5. **Refusals name the rule** — identical everywhere.
6. **Fork ink and sealed future** appear in every view.
7. **One clock name** — shorten, never rename.
8. **Declare what you cannot show** (Covers).
9. **Echo the receipt from state**, never optimistically; fail closed if you can't.
10. **Seat-safe**: a view receives only its seat's Surface; a shared display gets the public projection
    (BOW Economics Live's /board is already structurally seatless — REAL).

**Direct is the spine**: the seat's open matters as ordered acts, facts with lead words, refusals and
spatial relations in words. It is in every Plan (collapsed, docked or leading), it is the oracle the
other views are checked against, and it is the door for AI agents and third-party callers:
*agent-legible equals screen-reader-legible.*

## 3. What each kind owns and cannot show

| Kind | Owns | Cannot show (declared as a Cover) |
|---|---|---|
| Direct | exact wording, every act, relations as words | proportion at a glance, motion |
| Timeline | order, duration, amounts over time | who is where, adjacency |
| Table | exact quantity, many-way comparison | causes, order |
| Diagram | mechanism: stock, flow, rule, loop | who, exact timing |
| Map | geography, distance | quantity, duration |
| Room | adjacency, visibility, felt scale, opportunity cost through glass, absence as object | exact text, precise amounts, many entities, anything with no presence truth (no people are drawn without presence records) |
| Particle / molecular | population-scale mechanism | exact rates, K, history |
| Replay / film | any kind at a ledger position | counterfactual, sealed hindsight |

Instance rule: a view may draw individuals from a count (16,440 seated fans; molecules) only if the
count is bound, the individuals carry no facts, and the view says "drawn, not observed".

## 4. How the router decides (research G R1; PROPOSED)

Inputs: the system's manifests (which kinds it can compile; whether it has place primitives), the
question or task (compare → Table/Timeline; "what kind of thing is this?" → Room at object scale;
"follow" → Replay; "who must agree?" → the Table of seats), the seat (an operator's open matters), the
device tier, a **declared** access need (asked once at the first control and remembered — never
inferred; browsers deliberately hide assistive-technology use), and scale (thirty clubs' books go to a
Table, not a Room). First paint is always Direct-complete; the Plan then upgrades. The router never
changes a view under your hands; offers come at moment boundaries, except for a declared need or a
failed view. A shared address carries `view` as a request with a fallback, never a command.

## 5. How a person knows all views are the same system (Concordance)

- the same act number and state fingerprint in every frame;
- any fact can be followed to its twins ("also in: board · Direct · Table");
- the same tokens, realized natively;
- "Do these agree?" is askable and computed: each view exposes a ShownSet (path, token, text) diffed
  against Direct(state) — "Room and Timeline agree on 14 facts. Only Timeline: payroll by week (3).
  Only Room: the sign on Analytics' door (2). Contradictions: none.";
- disagreement fails closed: a stale view becomes a cover ("This view stopped matching the record at
  act 42") and Direct takes over;
- refusal parity: the same rule, the same text, in Room, Timeline, Direct and voice.
This proves the view was told the right thing, not that the pixels are right — browser truth stays
mandatory.

## 6. The Downshift (device tiers without losing authority)

Room 3D (desktop GPU) → Room 2.5D or lite (Chromebook) → Ledger + Direct (phone) → Direct voice, with
read-back before any act with stakes ("Send Derrick White, get Jimmy Butler. Payroll becomes $222.8M,
$22.8M over the World's line. Say 'confirm'."). A strip says what carried (open matters, every act,
refusals with rule names, tokens, the act number) and what was shed (camera, spatial gestalt). A
sustained low frame rate offers a step down with undo, never silently. A headset gets the same Room;
Direct stays a paper on the desk, not a floating panel. The kernel never learns the device.

## 7. Systems nobody drew (the Compile)

A creator publishes only a typed Surface (stocks, flows, commitments, rules, clocks, places, seats).
Direct and a Ledger appear at once; a Room compiles lazily through a domain kit, stamped COMPILED with
"What the compiler decided" (stock → shelves, lead time → distance, single source → one thread, a
supplier's fab → a covered building "no view inside"); creators may change each mapping; everyone can
read them. Compile only what the Surface has: no place primitives, no Room ("This system has no places;
nothing is hidden"). Make state, not geometry, the variety — lit/dark, full/empty — or compiled rooms
become ten thousand identical bowls of oatmeal (Compton).

## 8. Place: where it earns its cost (research C §2.8)

Better than any other representation: sightline as information boundary; absence as an object; four
kinds of not knowing (withheld · unbought · unlived · untraced) without words; choice versus shock
(a bricked arch vs a cracked stair); distance as causality with latency; residue as memory on the
same object; capacity as a count you stand inside; ambient state for a practiced operator; the ritual
weight of taking a seat; one shared object of attention for a class. Worse: exact magnitudes,
many-to-many structure, long series, enumeration and search, parallel alternatives, the rules
themselves, practiced speed, billions-scale discovery, continuous dynamics, non-visual access. Rule:
where place is worse, hang the better representation IN the room as an object (the roster board is a
chart on a wall); never pretend the room encodes it. Places thin with practice — the first hour has a
building, the thousandth a ledger — and that is a success, not a failure.

## 9. Labels
REAL CURRENT: lockstep act-log fold (Live World page, prototype-grade); state-bound surfaces in the
Boston Room and "covered means not played yet" in the arena (unmerged, no human verdict); Harbor's 2.5D
rooms changing with saved state (unmerged); /board as a seatless projection (BOW Economics Live).
PROPOSED: the Plan, ShownSets and the Concordance check, Covers, refusal and downshift parity, the
deterministic compile, Truth Materials. SPECULATIVE: compiling arbitrary untyped systems; open creator
packs at scale.

## 10. Falsifiers
Receipts and Concordance do not improve seeded-defect detection (sameness is felt without proof) ·
screen-reader users find Direct lacks what the Room gives · compiled Rooms are oatmeal and the Table
wins · newcomers override the Plan's first choice within a minute in most sessions · Covers go unread
and false certainty is unchanged · most new domains need a new primitive.
