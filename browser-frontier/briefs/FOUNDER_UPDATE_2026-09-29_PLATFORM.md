## Summary of the update
1. **BOW is bigger than Browser.** BOW = infrastructure for EXECUTABLE REALITY; Browser = the primary
   native HUMAN interface to that medium. Emerging surfaces: native forms (WORLD — persistent
   responsibility/system; CHALLENGE — bounded consequential/evidence envelope; MOMENT — preserved
   consequential act/episode; LAB — inspect, replay, fork, alter, compare); human surfaces (BROWSER,
   HOME, ATLAS, LENS / WHY?, NATIVE KNOWLEDGE / LEARN); creation (FOUNDRY — compile/create systems;
   STUDIO — create/bind representations); reality + distribution (REALITY, PUBLISHER, INVOCATION);
   platform / economy (CLOUD / RUNTIME, PROTOCOL / SDK, NETWORK, EXCHANGE / MARKETPLACE, ORGANIZATION
   RUNTIME). Do not design these as tabs/apps; ask how Browser makes them one medium.
2. **Three sports laboratories, three different architectural proofs.** BOSTON = ENTER / TRUTH (can BOW
   truthfully represent and operate an institution that already exists?). NEW YORK = CONNECT /
   CIVILIZATION (can many autonomous systems inhabit real geography and form a living executable
   economy?) — the first REALITY CITY, on actual NYC geography and sourced structural state, never a
   god simulation; many systems with separate authority (club, arena, league, agency, media, sponsors,
   finance, transit, port, hospital, university, chemistry lab, museum, historical center, businesses,
   youth systems, creator organizations). HARBOR LIGHTS = CREATE / HISTORY (can a new institution begin
   with almost nothing and become unique through accumulated history?) — killed if it becomes "Boston
   but fictional". Boston: enter history. New York: connect systems. Harbor: create history.
3. **New York is a cross-product stress test** (Browser, Atlas/scale, Home, World, Challenge, Moment,
   Lab, native knowledge, Foundry, Studio, Reality, Publisher, Invocation, Network, Exchange,
   Organization Runtime).
4. **The city as an index to knowledge** (museum → artifacts → people → neighborhoods → laws →
   economies; lab → experiments → molecules → equations; hospital → biology → logistics → staffing;
   port → shipping → customs; financial district → companies → markets → regulation; arena →
   basketball → contracts → media → labor) — without a metaverse or walking simulator.
5. **SCALE may be a first-class Browser verb** beside ENTER, WHY, WHAT IF, TIME, ROLE, FOLLOW — as a
   change of subject along a typed relation, not camera zoom; push it much further.
6. **Reality semantics are non-negotiable**: REALITY · BOW WORLD · RECORDED MOMENT · MODELED ·
   GENERATED · UNKNOWN (with reason). A BOW-authored headquarters on real NYC geography must never imply
   the building exists.
