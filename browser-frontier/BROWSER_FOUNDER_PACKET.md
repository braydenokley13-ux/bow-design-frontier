# BOW Browser — founder packet

BOW Browser Founding Frontier · Wave 1 plus the New York stress test · 30 Sep 2026. It answers the
commission's sixteen questions (§A) and the founder update's fifteen (§B), then lists the decisions
only you can make (§C). Every claim points to its evidence. Labels: **REAL** (exists and runs today),
**PROPOSED** (buildable on known technology; not built), **SPECULATIVE** (depends on capability nobody
has). No person outside this process has used anything here: every verdict is a design reading by
agents and three independent critics, not proof.

**The answer in one paragraph.** BOW Browser should not be a place people walk into. It is the native
human interface to a **record protocol**: every executable system keeps one canonical, append-only,
SHA-256-folded record; an address names a *cut* of that record and never grants authority; authority
arrives as an Offer a present person redeems; and every view (a room, a table, a timeline, plain text,
voice, a card inside someone else's app) is a checked witness of the same record. On that substrate the
Browser composes an **act grammar** (Jurisdiction: an act is a paper whose lines other systems hold, so
a wrong act comes back refused by another seat naming the rule), a **time grammar** (the Cut and the
Seam: stand at a moment with only what the desk could know; keep a branch; let Reality grade it
HELD · DIVERGED · IMPOSSIBLE · RE-DECLARE), a **reach layer** (Guest: systems appear inside assistants,
articles and messages, ring the seat when a clock needs a person, and are answered by standing orders
when you are away), and a **discovery lane** (press a true mark and typed doors open; a question with
no anchor goes to the Bench; a question nothing answers leaves an honest Want). Observing happens
everywhere; **occupying** — holding a seat, signing, keeping a branch, returning — happens in the
Browser.

**Where the evidence stands.** The critics' central complaint — "the pieces are new, but not yet in one
place" — now has a **partial** built answer: `prototypes/OneRecord.html` runs the flow both critics named on
one SHA-256-chained record, addressed in the URL, across two clients, and a third, independent critic
confirmed the hash — then broke it off-script (§A15). The strongest two grammars
were attacked in chemistry, a supply chain and Yellowstone: they **hold where someone holds the system,
bend where nature answers instead of a seat, and break where nobody holds it**
(`BROWSER_CROSS_DOMAIN_ATTACK.md`). On real New York geography the grammar navigates structure and
authority but not sums, **SCALE earns its place as a verb**, and the city is cheap to draw and expensive
to mean (`REALITY_CITY_STRESS_TEST.md`). All of it is PROPOSED design evidence played by agents; none of
it has met a real user.

## A. The commission's sixteen questions

### A1. What is BOW Browser becoming?
The place where you **hold something inside a system that does not stop**. Not a web browser for
simulations, not a game launcher, not a 3D city. Both critics independently arrived at the same
choice (Critic 2 put it as the founder decision: "Is BOW a destination people walk into, or a record
protocol: addressed, signed act logs that any surface renders as checked views? Choose the protocol").
The frontier adopted the protocol as its working canon (`briefs/CANON_AFTER_CRITIQUE.md`); confirming it is
decision C1. The Browser is then the one surface that can
do what the protocol forbids everyone else: draw the threshold where a person takes a seat, keep their
private boards and hindsight ledger, hold their branches, and walk them back "level with the record"
when they return. Place survives as **one view** (the Room), not as the index: walking to a building is
not how anyone will find a billion systems (`BROWSER_KILL_LIST.md`).

### A2. What is the strongest model?
**The Jurisdiction** (`prototypes/Jurisdiction.html`), ranked first by the medium critic and kept as
"the protocol layer" by the platform critic. It is the only board where a wrong act propagates as a
**refusal from another seat that names the rule**, where each system keeps its own record, clock,
ground and disclosure, and where the composition has **no god view** (three records, each showing only
what its holder may show). Its grammar — forms whose lines other systems hold; five kinds of yes
(RULE · WILL · TIME · MONEY · KNOWING); carriers (notice copies, remittance moves, countersignature
joins); the standing line ("you are in … your word counts for … not for …") — is what survived the New
York stress test with eight amendments (lane D) and the cross-domain attack (§B6). The strongest
*Browser* is not one model but a composition: Jurisdiction + Cut + Concordance + Guest on one record
(`BROWSER_MENTAL_MODEL_TOURNAMENT.md` §4).

### A3. What is the strangest but most important model?
**The Guest** (`prototypes/Guest.html`). The Browser stops being a destination: BOW appears inside an
assistant's answer, an article, a text, a voice call; **the seat calls you** (a ring with a brief, a
deadline and what happens if you don't answer); standing orders answer while you are away; and home is
a finite **Round** that ends "level with the record". Critic 1: "the system calls the seat, and absence
is recorded." It is strange because it inverts browsing (you do not go to the system; the system's
clock comes to the person holding it) and important because it is where invocation, trust and the
return loop meet. Its weakness was also the sharpest platform finding: **inside a host, BOW truth is
claimed, not verified, unless claims are signed** and checked by a verifier the reader controls
(`EXTERNAL_INVOCATION_FRONTIER.md` §8).

### A4. What does a newcomer meet first?
**What sent them — never a home page and never a search box** (`ATLAS_DISCOVERY_FRONTIER.md` §3).
Almost everyone arrives holding something true: a link, a figure in an article, a claim card in an
assistant, an Offer from a teacher. That thing is a **true mark**; pressing it opens typed doors with
their contents counted before you open them (Because · Meanwhile · Who · Here · Who says yes ·
What's different). For Boston, the first mark is the one the prior-art fixture forces us to get right:
"≈ $1.7M under the tax line" with cap payroll ≈ $203.6M and tax salary $198,722,406 side by side — and
behind it a door named by its question ("Could Boston have kept him?"), never by its answer. With no
history the Doorstep shows what sent you, else the place you chose, else the nearest shared clock
with its reason on its face — never "nothing here yet". On return the Browser opens inside the first
stop of the Round. **Untested with a real newcomer.** The falsifier: newcomers ignore marks and type
into the address bar as if it were search (§A16).

### A5. What does ENTER mean?
**GRANT → REDEEM → LEASE** (`ENTER_TOURNAMENT.md` §5). Entering is **taking named open matters**, the
Handover: you take specific papers with clocks; a blank line is refused ("a blank line would be a choice
nobody made"); lapses are recorded as NO ACT; leaving is a handover or a vacate. Authority arrives only
as an **Offer** redeemed by a present person in chrome only BOW draws (the address never carries it).
Hindsight is a **seal** that rides every act and fork. The **Ring** is the second way in (the seat calls
you; picking up is entering for the call). **Take a Face** governs shared papers (a seat is the unheld
face; the League's face is not yours). The visible sign you have crossed is the **standing line**
plus the five-part threshold (who offers · which seat · what you will see · what you give up · what
stays private). ENTER changes eight things: identity, role, information, authority, responsibility,
time, place (optional), actions. An AI agent enters the same way, under a warrant with scope, limits,
expiry and an act-rate budget, stamped as the agent, never as the person.

### A6. What does WHAT IF mean?
**A fork is a lineage, not a copy** (`WHAT_IF_TOURNAMENT.md` §5). It is born at a cut by a **Council**
that sets every touched seat to Recorded, Declare, Model or Unknown (Model never preselected); it runs
beside Reality (pinned, paced or free); Reality grades it on its own dates with exactly **HELD ·
DIVERGED · IMPOSSIBLE · RE-DECLARE** — and, where Reality cannot grade (no control, no independent
instrument), with no verdict at all (§C2); it is never merged. Its identity is base hash plus its own acts;
its storage is its divergence; its address is well-formed only when **closed** (every system it
touches pinned or declared). **Lifting an act out** (TakeItOut) is WHY by experiment with luck held
fixed, and its clamp is a new FOLLOW. The private dry run (Hold) stays honestly a preview. Crowds of
worlds are useful up to a class (≤ ~30, sealed until you act); beyond that they are herding
(parked). Still missing: a fork that runs a season on its own, and real inhabitants.

### A7. How does TIME work?
**The Cut and the Seam, as one instrument** (`TIME_NAVIGATION_TOURNAMENT.md` §5). You stand at a cut in
four planes — the books (what was recorded), the desk (what the seat could know then), not yet
(sealed), since (what happened after, behind a seal) — resolved by **floor, never nearest** (the
latest state not after the moment). "Later" is refused by the record's own rule. A **Horizon Rail**
keeps Reality's window in view, shuttered at a cut, and never ticks from the device clock while
claiming liveness. **Three backs**: the trail (pencil, private, discarded freely), the record (print,
never changed) and acts (signature, append-only; no Undo — only Re-take and Amend). That became a
platform law: **two logs, and only the record is hashed**. Time you did not spend reaches you through
the **Round**. The honest cost: the bitemporal record is cheap; the **knowledge frame** ("what could the
desk know then") is curated per moment by people. Build cuts where they earn it; everywhere else, "the
record then, without a desk", labelled.

### A8. How does Browser decide representations?
By a **router over manifests**, with the plain-text view as the oracle
(`REPRESENTATION_ROUTER_FRONTIER.md` §4–6). First paint is always **Direct** (open matters as ordered
acts, refusals, facts with lead words) — the screen-reader, voice and AI-agent view. The router then
offers an upgrade by task (compare → Table or Timeline; "who must agree?" → the table of seats;
"what kind of thing is this?" → the Room), by seat, device tier, a *declared* access need (never
inferred), and scale (thirty clubs' books go to a Table, not a Room). It never swaps a view under your
hands. **Every view is a witness** (Concordance): one act number and one hash in every view, a "Do
these agree?" check, covers that say what a view cannot show, and a **Downshift** (Room → 2.5D →
Ledger → voice) that loses fidelity but never authority or truth. Place is chosen only where it
does something a list cannot — the New York test sharpened this: **a place earns geometry only when a
number computed from the geometry changes a decision the seat can make** (lane C).

### A9. How does representation scale economically?
Cost must follow **shapes and domains, never systems, forks or viewers** — for pixels
(`REPRESENTATION_ECONOMICS.md`). Instance ≈ a data binding; Schema = design + deterministic compile +
one review; Kit = weeks per domain. Every system gets a compiled floor (Direct + Ledger + truth
tokens); Rooms are a luxury with a job; hand-built flagship rooms are rare. **But pixels are not the
bill.** The critics and New York added three units that do not amortize: **FACT** (a sourced, dated,
licensed value over time), **SITE** (binding a system to real ground and authority) and **REVIEW**
(a human signature on anything promoted). In a Reality City **the cost ladder runs opposite to the
depth ladder**: drawing 1.08M buildings is nearly free; making a dozen of them mean something costs
facts, sites, rulebooks and review (lane E). Compile only when pooled Wants cross a threshold; meter
human hours in Studio or every number here stays an estimate.

### A10. Which AI costs are unavoidable?
(`AMBIENT_AI_COST_MAP.md` §5, §10; lane E §3.) (1) **Open-world compilation** of a system nobody
built — FRONTIER, SPECULATIVE, and bounded by review, not tokens (one pass over New York's 34,446
facilities ≈ $10⁵ of compute and 20–70 person-years of review). (2) **Authoring** new models, kits,
glosses and door questions — FRONTIER OCCASIONAL, amortized. (3) **Actors' choices** in forks — cheap
with archetypes, **drawn per fork from a seeded distribution** (a shared cached Denver is an exploit).
(4) **Hosted agents in seats** — continuous only if BOW runs the brain, inside a warrant with an
act-rate budget. (5) **Language in and out** — SMALL, optional everywhere. (6) **`verify` of a free
sentence** — SMALL unless hosts send structured claims. The only AI cost that scales with *audience*
rather than systems is a per-viewer "ask the city" guide: build it only if it beats typed doors.

### A11. What works without AI?
Everything that touches truth (`AMBIENT_AI_COST_MAP.md` §4): following Reality and Worlds; every mark,
door, cut and seal; every reference ask (TOUCHED, WHY, KNEW, BLOCKS, CHANGES); refusals naming the rule
and nearest legal variants; seats, Offers, handovers, warrants, rings, standing orders, the Round; every
fork's rules layer and seeded simulation; Concordance and the Downshift; compiled Direct, Ledger and
kit Rooms; addresses and sharing; the challenge predicate (lane C); SCALE along typed relations; the
classroom on one machine offline (**REAL** today: bow-economics-live, D12, D166, D179). Degradation is
monotone: with AI off nothing spins, and a modeled slot reads "MODELED, not run".

### A12. How does Browser work inside other platforms?
**Invoke by reference. Verify by resolver. Redeem by person** (`EXTERNAL_INVOCATION_FRONTIER.md`).
Six tools over an assistant protocol and plain HTTPS — `resolve` (never guesses), `ask` (returns a
ClaimSet: kinds, provenance, typed unknowns, refusals with rule ids, precomputed deltas so the host
never subtracts), `whatif` (a receipted fork preview), `verify` (match · mismatch · unsupported, per
sentence), `handin` (an Offer the user redeems in BOW), `warrant` (signed in BOW). A host may open,
inspect, ask WHY, run a WHAT IF preview and hand in; it may never sign, hold a seat or keep a branch.
**Truth inside a host is claimed unless signed**: ClaimSets carry signatures and a verifier the reader
controls checks them. **Nothing can be invoked from outside today** (every host in the Guest board is
drawn inside a BOW page). The cheapest real step: serve one signed ClaimSet as JSON for a real record
and check it from a second client.

### A13. What does Browser uniquely provide?
Six things no embedded surface can (`EXTERNAL_INVOCATION_FRONTIER.md` §5, amended): **the threshold**
(the only place a seat is taken, an Offer redeemed, an act signed, a warrant written, in chrome no host
can imitate); **continuity across systems** (the Round, the Standing, seat diffs, rings, standing
orders — a host shows one system at a time); **the fork workbench** (keep, continue, compare, return;
branches graded at the seam); **Concordance** (the same act in every view with one number and hash);
**private boards and the hindsight ledger**; and **verification** (the record, the branch and the drift
side by side — and, after the critique, the verifier that makes a signed claim in someone else's page
trustworthy). What people pay for is occupying, not observing.

### A14. What interaction is impossible as a website or a game?
**The Seam.** A fork of recorded history, kept by a person, graded by real history arriving on its own
dates: "DIVERGED — Reality did something your branch has no room for. You did not cause it." Critic 1:
"A game cannot do this, because its world does not continue without it. A website cannot, because
nothing it shows is yours." A close second, also judged new: **a refusal that arrives from another
seat, naming its rule, across three records with no god view** (Jurisdiction). Honest caveat: the Seam
is the interaction most dependent on a live Reality feed that does not exist; today it is replay of a
dated fixture.

### A15. What is the ONE end-to-end flow to build first?
The flow both critics named, now built as `prototypes/OneRecord.html` (`CANON_AFTER_CRITIQUE.md` §6):
the seat rings you → you pick up and the standing line rewrites → you sign your line of the Denver
paper → the League returns it naming the rule → you fix and resend → the act lands in the Room, the
Table and Direct at once with one act number and one SHA-256 → you leave; a standing order answers
while you are away → the Round on return says what you can no longer do and which kept assumption
Reality broke → you lift the trade out to see what it did → you keep the fork → return to the record —
**on ONE record, addressed in the URL, checked across two clients.**

**Result (built; parent-verified by play; independently judged PUSH by a third critic).** Every step
of the flow runs, in order, at 1440×900, 1280×800, 1024×600 and 390×844 with no page or console errors
(37 screenshots per viewport). What the parent checked independently, beyond the builder's script:
- **One record.** The hash is real SHA-256 (Web Crypto) chained over a canonical serialization of each
  act; navigation (the trail) is kept separately and never hashed. Act 43 appears as "act 43 ·
  a79682bd" in the address bar, the Room, the Table and Direct at once, and "Do these agree?"
  recomputes payroll, roster, tax and the apron gap from what each window is showing, with a second
  reducer and a fresh digest.
- **The refusal comes from another seat.** With $0.5M cash on the paper, the League returns it: "A team
  over the second apron may not send cash in a trade … Nothing changed on Boston's books. No act number
  spent (next: 43)." Take the cash out, resend, and act 43 lands in three records with no god view
  (Denver's payroll after the trade reads "not shown to you").
- **The address law holds.** A fresh browser with separate storage opening act 43's address says: "This
  address names act 43. This copy of the record ends at act 42, so it cannot check it. Showing what it
  has." — and shows the seat as a viewpoint, "You hold nothing". A forged hash in the address is caught:
  "Address does not match: it names act 43 · deadbeef; this record's act 43 is a79682bd. Another copy, or
  a fork." A second tab in the same browser gets a viewpoint, not the seat. Reload restores state.
- **The Round and the seam.** After a stretch away, a standing order you wrote (a blank clause is
  refused) has answered for you, stamped as the order, not you; the Round says "2 stops · 1 closes
  tonight", then "A branch you kept has broken" with HELD, IMPOSSIBLE, DIVERGED and RE-DECLARE drawn
  from one STAGED Reality arrival (5 Feb 2026, labelled SPECULATIVE FRONTIER), and ends "Level with the
  record".

**What it does not prove:** the shared store is localStorage + BroadcastChannel, not a server; Denver
and the League are authored stand-ins; the Room is a drawn SVG, not the 3D office; the day is authored;
the Reality arrival is staged; there is no discovery step (you are called instead). The World uses an
authored flat 1.5× tax on a $200.0M World tax line beside the real 2026–27 second apron ($221.686M) —
a seam between World rule and real rule that the page labels but a critic may still find confusing.
Several CBA readings (cash from a second-apron team; the no-trade rule) are marked [verify]. **No
person outside this process has played it.**

**The independent verdict: PUSH, and "not yet in one place" is answered only partly**
(`critique/CRITIC_3_CONVERGENCE.md`). A third critic who built neither board played it, recomputed act
43's SHA-256 in Node and got the same value, and saw one number and one hash in four views and a live
second tab — "the first time I have seen it hold." Then it went off the script and found the record
lying in four ways:
1. **Two hashes for one act.** Letting Denver's second call lapse with no clause to cover it made act 44
   read `0a646ccb` in the address and `0b0632a2` on the head — while "Do these agree?" said "They agree".
2. **Who acted was rewritten.** After redeeming the seat, the fixture's earlier acts (#35–#42) read "you ·
   GM seat".
3. **What an absence meant was rewritten.** The Round said the lapsed call was "declined by your standing
   order", and an answer to the owner was written for the person.
4. **Anyone could wipe the record.** An observer could press "Restart the stand-in World"; an edited
   local store was accepted by every window; a fork address crashed a fresh browser.
On the scripted path everything held, including the seam (the critic moved WHAT IF / FORK from WEAK to
OK after reaching it). Its summary: answered "for one act in one browser", not across time, forks,
devices or the two boards. "The single most important thing missing before a real person tries this:
a record that a second person on a second device can check, and that does not lie."

**Repaired after the critique and re-checked by the parent; not re-judged by the critic.** The builder
fixed the four lies in a bounded pass (it was cut off by a spend limit before reporting; the parent ran
the checks). Replaying the critic's off-script path now shows:
- With Denver's second call left uncovered, the head reads "no act ×2 after act 44 · 0b0632a2" in the
  address, the bar, the Room, the Table and Direct. "Do these agree?" now checks the address line and
  cash, and says DISAGREE when an address does not match the record.
- Acts #35–#42 read "Boston · GM seat (before you)".
- The Round says "Denver's second call lapsed · no act. None of your clauses covered it". The answer to
  the owner must be typed in the person's own words, or stamped as drafted by BOW.
- Restart sits under "Demo controls" and is refused to observers: "Refused: seat GM is held by a live
  window". An edited store reads "MISMATCH — the stored record does not verify".
- A lift, fork, Denver or malformed address is refused with a named reason and left as written. A seat
  can be handed back.
- The Holiday branch's tax row is now RE-DECLARE: "Reality's ≈ $39.5M [verify] counted Simons, who
  never arrived here."
All three scripts (main, off-script, fresh browser) are clean at 1440, 1280, 1024 and 390. One cosmetic
gap remains: two confirmation notices are hidden at narrow widths.

**Still not in one place:** a second *device* (the store is one browser's), acts *across boards*
(OneRecord and RealityCity keep separate engines and address grammars), and forks that keep acting.
Those are exactly what the next proof (§B15) must add.

### A16. What would falsify the thesis?
Ranked by how much of the thesis each would kill:
1. **People do not want to hold anything.** Outside the builders, nobody takes a seat, returns for a
   Round, or keeps a branch; they read the claim card and leave. (Test: Boston gate, §B14.)
2. **The record cannot be sourced.** One club's facts cannot be kept honest at a cost you accept
   (the Fact unit already needs a person before every class in the shipped product, lane E §1).
3. **Children cannot tell Reality from Authored by frame.** Fewer than nine of twelve testers (B §2.7
   card sort) — then the truth grammar is decoration.
4. **The knowledge frame does not scale.** Curating "what the desk could know" costs more per moment
   than the moment is worth; cuts collapse to "the record then, without a desk" everywhere.
5. **Verticals do not amortize.** Denver from the Boston kit costs nearly what Boston did (research G).
6. **Hosts paraphrase and never hand in.** Invocation becomes a data API; the Browser is never opened.
7. **Signing feels like a EULA.** The threshold is skipped as ceremony.
8. **Newcomers search.** Marks go unpressed; the address bar is used as a search box.
9. **The map adds nothing.** Newcomers do no better with the city than with a list on "what is here?"
   tasks (lane C falsifier).
10. **Review does not fall.** Reviewer hours per promoted schema never drop, so growth is linear in
    people.

## B. The founder update's fifteen questions

### B1. Can the winning grammar navigate a Reality City?
**Yes for structure and authority; no for sums; and only as fast as bridges get authored**
(`REALITY_CITY_STRESS_TEST.md` §2; `prototypes/RealityCity.html` on 4,142 real Midtown footprints). The
condition is architectural: **the city is a directory and a post office, never a state** — no city
record, clock or hash; jurisdictions with their own records, typed relations between them, Forms that
travel between them (lane D). Played on real ground, the grammar did what it does in Boston: "what is
here?" opens a *site* where many authorities meet (the Garden's structure sits on a lot titled to a rail
corporation, hosts an arena the registries do not name, and has a station below that no footprint
sees); taking the arena seat rewrites the standing line; a request for extra post-game trains comes
back **returned at intake naming the transit desk's rule**, then gets a **partial yes on the desk's own
clock**, with money conserved and three records, no god view. Placing a fictional club HQ is a
checkable act: over the Garden it is refused as a building (a tenancy flag only); on a vacant Fifth
Avenue block-front all four tests pass and it renders as a dashed AUTHORED outline; where the lot
boundary is not loaded the checker says **CAN'T TELL** and refuses to draw a guess. Carried up to "the
city", the pinned crowd stops at an honest UNKNOWN — no ridership denominator — and a Want. The city
forces eight amendments on the act grammar (READ lines from observed authorities, one form clock, one
person one side, ports before lines, singleton stand-ins, receipts for knowledge, requester-owned
aggregates, standing at every door). Everything is PROPOSED; the transit desk is a scripted stand-in;
no feed runs. The independent critic judged the board **COMBINE**: keep the converting pin and the site
checks and fold them into OneRecord's World; park the map as a landing page; and its biggest failure
was an ask box that ignored or misrouted questions without a word ("where is the Empire State
Building?" → "Arrived at Club HQ.") After repair (parent-checked): "asdf" gets NO SYSTEM FOR THIS, with the words kept and a Want offered.
An address outside the excerpt says NOT IN THIS EXCERPT. "Where is the Empire State Building?" opens
that building's own site, with a receipt naming the keyword router. Enter submits. The excerpt edge is
drawn, and the 800 m ring states "80.3% covered" with the rest UNKNOWN. The ENTER text now says "a
practice seat taken with one click. This board draws no Offer". One form digest sits in every log that
holds the act.

### B2. Does geography help or decorate?
**Both, and the line is testable.** Geography earns geometry only when a number computed from it
changes a decision a seat can make (lane C). Of thirteen briefed New York systems it does real work for
four (arena, transit, port, historical maps), some work for two (hospital catchment, museum time-key),
and is **decoration for seven** — club, league, agency, media, sponsors, finance, the chemistry lab
(lane E §4). The league, the cluster's most powerful authority, has no useful geography at all; the
market's operative "where" is a New Jersey data centre. What geography genuinely adds is a small set of
**derived relations nobody typed** (building × evacuation zone; channel × bridge clearance), unasked
adjacency (the *Here* door), a shared "this block" everyone can point at, a time-key across maps and
decades, and jurisdiction lines (the arena's lot belongs to a rail corporation). It is expensive
decoration as photoreal tiles, interiors of systems whose knowledge is not spatial, walking, crowds
and citywide massing that promises a door on every prism. **Geography is one relation family
(located-in, near), not the index.**

### B3. Is SCALE a native verb?
**Yes, under conditions — it is the only verb that changes the system itself** (lane B;
`REALITY_CITY_STRESS_TEST.md` §3). Each verb owns one coordinate of the address (ENTER the viewpoint,
TIME the moment, WHAT IF the branch); **SCALE changes the system while carrying one pinned quantity
through a typed bridge, and rewrites the standing line at every hop.** Without the pin it is a
hyperlink; without the typed relation it is a camera; without the standing line it lies about
authority. It has five marks (relation · pin · bridge · ground · end) and six laws, the sharpest being
**SITED-1** — location carries only what is physically there (the crowd reaches the street as a
density, but a clinic 290 m away receives nothing because nothing relates it to the arena) — and **a
pin wears the kind of its weakest bridge** (18,000 AUTHORED × 55% AUTHORED = 9,900 riders, still
AUTHORED). Nine relation families cover the brief's ten (COMPOSES, SITES, SEATS, TITLES, BINDS, FLOWS,
SERVES, KINDS, SHOWS), each with its own algebra for the pin. **It is not zoom and not "up"**: from the
arena, "up" is six different things. Registry joins are free; the bridges that matter (crowd → riders,
riders → street density) are authored one at a time, so **bridges, not tiles, are the scarce object**.
Every chain tested ends on an aggregate or a denominator, never on "the map runs out". Falsifier not yet
run: a plain list with a pin ties the drawn sheets on three questions with ten adults.

### B4. Can a city be a knowledge index without becoming a metaverse?
**Yes as an index; no as a place; and only partially** (lane C §4). The index is *place key → anchored
executable systems → typed relations → a source of record*: point at a block, follow a relation, open
a system at a moment; nobody moves an avatar. Four conditions: **doors, not pins** (a pin qualifies
only if it opens a running system whose WHY-walk ends at a source; otherwise it shows a Want); **the
map is one witness** (the same doors must be reachable by question, person and time with no map);
**place bias is admitted** (molecules, equations and laws have no address and enter through anchoring
institutions, so the Bench stays beside the map); and a falsifier (map vs list, not yet run). Native
knowledge in this grammar is a walk, not a page: act and consequence → WHY down typed hinges to a
source → NAME the term after the experience → CARRY it to a system of the same shape elsewhere → TAKE
IT OUT. A museum has no mechanics; the executable thing is the world its objects are evidence of.

### B5. How do Boston, New York and Harbor differ architecturally?
Three proofs, not three skins (lane D §9; full table in `REALITY_CITY_STRESS_TEST.md` §7):
- **Boston · ENTER / TRUTH** — can BOW truthfully operate an institution that exists? Reality is a
  lineage you can enter at recorded moments but never act on; the scarce resource is *truth*
  (sourcing, rights, as-of); it stresses the cut, the seam and the fact pipeline. Killed if it cannot
  be sourced or rights block real people.
- **New York · CONNECT** — can systems with separate authority transact on shared real ground? The city
  is a directory and a post office, never a state; the scarce resource is *other authorities*
  (independent operators and review); it stresses Forms, ports, the registry, stand-ins, Foundry and
  Exchange. Killed if members are one author's puppets or children cannot tell Reality from Authored.
- **Harbor · CREATE / HISTORY** — can a new institution become unique through accumulated history? No
  Reality; everything authored and honest by default; the scarce resource is *time* (seasons and
  returning owners). Killed if it becomes "Boston but fictional": two Harbors forked at year 0 are
  indistinguishable from their records after N seasons.

### B8. What does Home become across domains?
A **Person record of pointers and last-seen cuts**, never anyone else's state (lane D §3). Home is the
Round: a finite walk on return, in a deterministic order (what lapses first · breaks in your kept
branches · things addressed to you · what's live within a day · drift), each stop a seat diff in that
system's own vocabulary — a club seat reports what lapsed and what the League returned; a lab bench,
"run stopped at step 6; reagent lot changed"; a museum role, "this object's record changed"; a public
fork, HELD · DIVERGED · IMPOSSIBLE · RE-DECLARE. It ends "level with the record". Three rules hold
across domains: **no merged number** (clocks never total; no level, badge or leaderboard — D4);
**authority does not compose** (the arena seat gives nothing at the lab); and for **children**, a
tenure is a teacher-scoped lease, shared-screen names stay fictional, and no cross-institution profile
is built.

### B9. What does Foundry need to add an institution to a city or network?
A **dossier**, compiled without AI and reviewed by people (lane D §4). In order of need: (1) **port and
line declarations** — what lines it can hold, what notices it emits, what it needs from others as
dashed edges that turn solid only on countersignature (a new institution cannot declare an existing one
its sponsor); (2) **a truth kind per field** with weakest-input propagation, or the compile fails; (3)
**registry ids, namespaces and stand-in ownership** (`nyc:bin:…` never equals `bow:sys:…`; one
stand-in per real institution); (4) **a Site checker** that returns OK-AS-TENANCY, OK-AS-PROPOSAL-BESIDE
or REFUSED-OVERLAP and prints "AUTHORED; does not exist physically"; (5) **composition probes**
(closure, cross-port conservation, authority non-escalation, disclosure leakage, no circular WILL
dependence, a dominance sweep); (6) **the tournament** on Foundry's queued third target — a bounded
partner agreement — hand code vs agent-drafted declaration vs hybrid, by independent authors. Foundry's
declaration compiler is **REAL** (unmerged Worlds branch); none of (1)–(6) is. The scarce resource is
human review, not tokens.

### B10. What does Studio need?
Six things (lane E §8): **a manifest per schema** (levels offered L0–L5, truth kind per field, covers,
device tiers, allowed Site relations); **a pack registry with a rights line per asset**; **procedural
compilers as parameterized templates** keyed schema × kit × tier × locale with state and as-of as
parameters; **AI-asset intake** (de-light, compress, hash, screen for likenesses, logos and real
buildings — a generated asset may bind to a real building only as stippled, reviewed decor);
**semantic binding with the Site tests run as CI** (an authored footprint intersecting a real building
fails the build unless declared a tenancy, annex or proposal); and **a gate** — Concordance parity on
every rung, a frame budget on a real Chromebook, projector legibility, a complete rights ledger, an
as-of on every field — **plus a meter of human hours** per kit, skin, binding and review.

### B11. What must Reality provide?
Eight things (lane E §9, lane A §2.5): a **source registry and rights ledger** (per source: may BOW
render · derive · cache · go offline · republish · feed to AI · use commercially; plus obligations);
**dated, hashed snapshots — start now**, because the city's portals overwrite and keep no versions and
knowledge time never recorded cannot be recovered; **an as-of and a truth kind on every field** (a 0
capacity, year or height is UNKNOWN, never zero); **a feed mirror** that stamps lag and, on revocation,
turns OBSERVED into RECORDED-as-of-last-pull and says so; **a privacy filter at ingest**; **an identity
crosswalk and Site relations** (many authorities per parcel); **the Fact pipeline by tier** (registry,
filings, reported, licensed, private — each with a refresh cadence and an independence check, "two
outlets ≠ two sources"); and a **decision on league data** (Sportradar is the NBA's exclusive data
provider; NBA.com statistics are for news or private non-commercial use — a question for counsel).

### B12. What may external surfaces invoke?
Open a cut; **inspect** (a ClaimSet with kinds and provenance); ask **WHY** (the walk to a source);
run a **WHAT IF** preview on the rules layer (receipted; modeled layers metered); **preview a fork** on
a temporary lease; **verify** a sentence against the record; **compare**; **hand in** an Offer; request
a **warrant**. They may never sign, hold a seat, keep a branch, read a private board, carry an outcome
in an address, or render BOW truth without a signature the reader can check. The New York invocation
case showed the discipline under pressure: "How would this transit disruption affect tonight's arena?"
returns the OBSERVED alert with its lag, the RECORDED start time, an AUTHORED crowd estimate, the arrival
share as **UNKNOWN** (no source, no model), and refuses "the operator will add trains" (a G1 authority
issues facts, not yeses) and "about 4,000 fans stranded" (`verify` → unsupported). **Today's honest
answer contains no headcount** (lanes D, E).

### B13. What stays native to Browser?
The threshold (identity, redeeming Offers, signing, writing warrants); private knowledge (private boards,

### B15. What ONE production Browser proof comes after the Worlds/DC convergence?

1. **One served record** for one Boston World on BOW's runtime — append-only, act numbers issued once,
   SHA-256 over canonical acts, the trail kept private and never hashed.

6. **The Round after real elapsed time**, with one standing order that answered while the person was

7. **The address law:** reload restores state; a pasted address never grants the seat.

stand-ins today: the server, the second human and the signed claim.
