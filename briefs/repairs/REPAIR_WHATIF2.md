## One WHAT IF? grammar (canon candidate; only the founder approves)

- **BEGIN** (Fork, Pull):
  - Begin at a door named by its question, and act in the seat.
  - For a quantity governed by rules, pull it instead.
  - Until you press Keep, you are only thinking: let go and the ghost fades.
- **DECLARE** (Council): the cut convenes every actor it touches, each set to Recorded, Declare, Model or Unknown.
  - "Recorded" is refused if the actor's record depended on the cut.
  - "Model" is refused without a named basis.
  - Keep is blocked while any seat that a consequence depends on is unset.
  - Every refusal names its rule.
- **UNCERTAINTY** (Fork, Pull, Living):
  - Whatever the rules can compute is computed.
  - Unknowns split into labelled ranges up to a horizon, and are sealed after it.
  - One strip shows the mix: say-so, computed, modeled, unknown.
- **OTHER ACTORS** (Council): each gets a seat, never an inference. Counterparties are named or left open.
- **PERSIST** (Living):
  - The branch is a ledger beside Reality.
  - Each arrival fits, diverges, becomes impossible, or flags a re-declare.
  - A frozen branch has no NOW.
- **COMPARE** (Fork, Pull, Fan):
  - Record and branch are shown in parallel rows, the record in its own ink.
  - R/P ticks mark each rule.
  - A model is scored only when a named model actually ran, and it never owns the referee. Call size counts.

## Red lines — a BOW fork must never

- fill over or recolour the record;
- become Save As: every branch needs a stamp, an author and at least one declared difference;
- compute a consequence through an actor that has no seat;
- mark an act "as recorded" when it depended on the cut;
- hatch an authored number, including a simple function of one;
- average unknowns, or call something UNKNOWN that rules could compute;
- score a model against its own record, or show fake popularity;
- label a World "Reality", or leave a branch without a clock;
- fit a Reality event that leans on an unknown without flagging it;
- reveal an outcome before the seat acts;
- show the World's flat 1.5× tax as the NBA's tax.
