How do we know?
People — observed. First U.S. census, 1790. Kentucky and the Southwest Territory were counted apart from Virginia and North Carolina [verify]. 1787 counts: unknown.
First House — authored. U.S. Constitution, Art. I §2: 65 seats named state by state, fixed before any census.
Seats — computed here. Largest remainder on a 65-seat House and a 26-seat Senate, sizes chosen so rules compare. A population Senate gets no minimum per state; that is our choice too.
Pass the room? — authored. Positions read from Madison’s notes by the designers (Farrand, Records, 1911), each marked verify. A reading, not a forecast.
Not in the room — unknown. New Hampshire’s delegates arrived 23 July; New York’s had left; Rhode Island never came [verify].
