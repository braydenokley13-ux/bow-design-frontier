# P3 · THE JURISDICTION — structure-first Browser; composable systems (Boston deep)

File: `prototypes/Jurisdiction.html` (+ `.steps.json`). Read first: `briefs/00_PRIOR_ART.md`,

## Exact design problem
Boston's boundaries extend into other systems: the LEAGUE (rules, approval, the trade call), a
PLAYER and his REPRESENTATIVE (consent, incentives), the ARENA (a separate business with its own
calendar), MEDIA (who knows what, when), another CLUB (its own desk). An act in one may need a yes
from another. Show, without a node-link graph, (1) which system the person currently occupies,
(2) whose authority applies, (3) what crosses a boundary (information copies, money moves,
signatures join), (4) when they must move to another system to proceed. And show SCALE as a change
of subject along a named relation with one quantity pinned — not zoom.

## User
A fan who asks "why is Boston so close to the tax line?" (observer in Reality), then the operator of
"Boston · Year Two" (GM seat) acting in the World.

## System
Part A in Boston Celtics · Reality (observer: you cannot act). Part B in the BOW World "Boston ·
Year Two · Week 9" (GM seat; acts are canonical in the World). Fixture: `reality`, `league`,
`world`, `neighbours`. Anything you author (Denver's side, a partner's calendar, other clubs'
payrolls) is AUTHORED and says "illustrative".

## Mandatory states
1. **Arrive at the neck (Reality, observer).** The pin: Boston's payroll against four 2026–27 lines
   — BOTH numbers side by side (cap payroll ≈ $203.6M; tax salary $198,722,406, ≈ $1.7M under the
   $200.428M tax line; verify), never averaged. A band runs from the pin, its width the room left,
   pinching where a line is close; "TIGHTEST HERE" computed. The standing line at the top: "You are
   in: Boston Celtics · Reality · as observer · your word counts for: nothing here (you can observe,
   follow, enter a recorded moment)."
2. **Hinges (scale as change of subject).** Tabs on the sheet's edge, each a named relation: PEERS ·
   RUN BY · PAID TO · WRITTEN AS · BOUND BY · MADE BY. Follow PEERS: a new sheet (30 clubs as ticks
   against the same lines — AUTHORED illustrative distribution, Boston real) opens beside; the
   Boston sheet folds to a thin edge that still reads the pin. Follow BOUND BY: the second-apron
   rule as a plane; drag the payroll across it and verbs are struck (aggregate salaries, send cash,
   use the taxpayer MLE — verify); release and the pencil erases; nothing forked. Follow MADE BY:
   the CBA in its governance reading (League + players' union; term `[verify]`). The pin arrives
   intact on every sheet.
3. **Cross into your World (jurisdiction change).** "Take your seat in Boston · Year Two": the ground
   changes register (paper, type, verbs); the standing line rewrites: "You are in: Boston · Year Two
   (your World) · seat: GM · your word counts for: signing, trading, payroll · not for: approving
   trades, setting the tax line, scheduling the arena."
4. **The Instrument (an act whose lines other systems hold).** Draft: "Send the backup guard
   (minimum deal, $2.449M) to Denver for a future second-round pick" (Denver's open matter). The act
   is a FORM whose lines are derived from the rulebooks it touches: BOSTON accepts (your WILL-yes,
   your warrant); DENVER signs (WILL-yes — Denver's seat is held by another owner; you cannot fill
   it; a stippled note "Denver's side: modeled ABOUT their position, not by them" if you show one);
   LEAGUE approves (RULE-yes); PLAYER consent drawn STRUCK THROUGH with the reason (no no-trade
   clause applies — an absent line is information; verify the rule text); MEDIA has no line (media
   never says yes but will learn). A KNOWING strip shows who knows now (Boston ●, Denver ●, League
   ○, player ○, media ○) and what a leak would do. You can sign line 1 only.
5. **Rehearse.** "Trade call (dry run)": the rulebook answers YES / NO naming the rule / CAN'T TELL;
   recorded as a rehearsal; crosses nothing.
6. **Send — the ground changes under your hands.** The form leaves as an envelope; the page takes
   the League's register (numbered articles, ruled ledger, no marks); the standing line reads
   "visiting: League Office · you can see: queue count, this file's status · you cannot: approve";
   League review time dashed (UNKNOWN), Boston's deadline beside it. Three moves: WAIT · GO THERE
   (observe what the League discloses) · RE-FORM.
7. **Return and propagation.** The yes arrives as a stamp naming the rulebook version; three records
   (Boston, Denver, League) show the act from three sides, no god-view; Boston's ledger — unchanged
   until the last line filled — moves at once (payroll $222.8M → $220.35M; roster 14; projected tax
   recomputed under the World rule); every open sheet shows the pin before and after.
8. **A second composition (different kinds of yes).** "Add a home date for a partner night": needs
   the ARENA's TIME-yes (free/busy calendar; the arena is a separate business — AUTHORED calendar),
   the League's schedule approval (RULE-yes), Partnerships (yours). One date is refused, naming
   whose calendar holds it. The five kinds of yes (RULE · WILL · TIME · MONEY · KNOWING) and three
   carriers (notice copies · remittance moves · countersignature joins) must be visibly different
   marks, not just labels.
9. **WHY and WHAT IF.** WHY on the new payroll traces to the act and the rule (NO AI). WHAT IF:
   "Redline" the Denver form (change the pick to a first-rounder): both faces re-execute, Denver's
   and the League's rules annotate in their own voices; keeping a redline makes a branch in its own
   ink with a stamp; COMPARE shows recorded vs redlined papers face up; fold the redline away to
   RETURN.
10. **Follow.** "Hold this paper" on a paper you have no side in (e.g., the League's pending queue
    or a 2031 second-round pick you're owed in Reality — verify who owes it): its clock paces you.

## Boundary grammar you must make perceptible
GROUND (register) · WARRANT (whose word counts, per line) · YES (five kinds) · CARRIER (copy / move /
join) · CLOCK (a crossed act runs on the receiver's clock) · DISCLOSURE (content · shape only ·
count and result · nothing) · MOVE (WAIT · GO THERE · RE-FORM). Laws: information leaks, authority
is countersigned, money is conserved, time is local.

## Art direction — "Registry office"
Each system has its own ground: Boston (club paper, a green rule), the League (grey ruled ledger,
numbered articles), Denver (its own letterhead colour), the arena (a booking-calendar grid), media
(newsprint clippings with outlet + date `[verify]`). Envelopes, stamps, sleeves. Light grounds.
Fonts: e.g. "Source Serif 4" + "Courier Prime".

## Capability labels
REAL CURRENT (unmerged NBA-World branch / BOW Economics Live): the CBA rules engine; two-owner papers
offer → signed → delivered; the League Office public vote record; pending terms owner-scoped; the
dated fact store. PROPOSED: N-party forms with kinds of yes, dry runs, ground/register change,
standing line, hinges and pins across sheets, papers with two faces. AI: NO AI REQUIRED for RULE-yes,
forms, pins, sheets; a WILL-yes played by a model would be SMALL / CHEAP MODEL (stippled stand-in);
an AI holding a seat FRONTIER MODEL OCCASIONAL.

## Prohibited
Node-link graphs; a tab bar labelled NBA / League / Player; logos or marks to signal authority; a
wizard stepper for the form; a "pending approval" spinner; the Descent's altitude gauge or parallax.

## Acceptance criteria
All 10 states in the steps file; at every state a person can read which system they are in and
whose word counts; the act changes Boston's ledger only when the last line fills, visibly in three
records; harness clean at both viewports.
