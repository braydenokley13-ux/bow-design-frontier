What the map says after Wave 1
  • HOW DO WE KNOW? shows up in every family. It is the strongest candidate for a universal gesture — and the one most likely to become a label wall if it is done lazily.
  • WHAT IF? recurs in eight families but means three different things: a branch of history (P2, P5), a lab state (P3, P6, P8), a governance change (P9). The next tournament should test whether one gesture can carry all three.
  • FOLLOW was not on the brief’s list of primary verbs. It emerged on its own in Chemistry, Biology, Enterprise and Network — follow a molecule, the oxygen, a promise, the money. It is earning a place.
  • SHARE only becomes real once there is an address. Browser A and the Fork both produce one; nothing else does yet.
  • WHO AM I? splits in two: a seat you occupy (Seat, Fork, APUSH) and a warrant an agent holds (Agent). Probably the same primitive.
