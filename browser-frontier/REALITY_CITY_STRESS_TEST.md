# Reality City stress test — New York against the Browser grammar

BOW Browser Founding Frontier · founder update of 29 Sep 2026 · written 30 Sep 2026. A targeted stress
test, not a New York build. Five bounded lanes: A geography and data scout
(`research/NYC_A_GEOGRAPHY_DATA_SCOUT.md`), B city scale and SCALE (`research/NYC_B_CITY_SCALE.md` +
`prototypes/RealityCity.html`), C knowledge city (`research/NYC_C_KNOWLEDGE_CITY.md`), D network and
civilization (`research/NYC_D_NETWORK_CIVILIZATION.md`), E economics critic
(`research/NYC_E_ECONOMICS_REPRESENTATION.md`). The grammar under test is the one that survived the
critics (`briefs/nyc/GRAMMAR_UNDER_TEST.md`). Tags: [M] measured this session, [P] primary source read,
[S] snippet only (verify), [R] BOW repository.

## 0. Verdict

1. **The grammar navigates a Reality City for structure and authority, not for sums, and only as fast
   as bridges get authored** (lane B, built on 4,142 real Midtown footprints). The city is a **directory
   and a post office, never a state**: no city record, clock or hash; jurisdictions with their own
   records, typed relations between them, and Forms that travel between them (lane D). That one rule
   is the mechanical refusal of the god simulation.
2. **SCALE is a native verb, under conditions** (lane B): it is the only verb that changes the
   *system* coordinate of the address while carrying one pinned quantity through a typed **bridge**,
   and it rewrites the standing line at every hop. Without the pin it is a hyperlink; without the
   typed relation it is a camera.
3. **Geography is one relation family, not the index.** It did real work for 4 of 13 briefed systems
   and was decoration for 7 (lane E). A place earns geometry only when a number computed from it
   changes a decision a seat can make (lane C). The League, the most powerful authority in the
   cluster, has no place at all.
4. **The city is an index to knowledge only as a registry projected onto a map** — doors, not pins;
   the map is one witness; the Bench stays beside it (lane C).
5. **Cheap to draw, expensive to mean** (lane E). Drawing New York is nearly free; making a dozen
   systems in it honest costs facts, site bindings, rulebooks and review. The cost ladder runs
   opposite to the depth ladder. Plausible at one depth only: recorded ground for the whole city plus a
   cluster of about a dozen systems at L0–L2.
6. **The scarce objects are bridges, other authorities and reviewers — not tiles.** New York is
   killed as a proof if its members are one author's puppets.
7. **Order: Boston → New York (thin: one night, one district) → Harbor judged last**, gated on
   Foundry's tournament; swap New York and Harbor if no second author or operator appears (lane D).
   **No-regret now:** start the dated, hashed snapshot archive of the city's registries (lane E).

## 1. What the city actually is (lane A)

**The geometry BOW would hand-model already exists, free.** NYC building footprints: 1,083,062
buildings, weekly refresh, roof height on 99.93%, keyed by BIN and BBL; NYC Open Data carries no
licence, registration or use restriction [M][P]. The prototype runs on 4,142 real Midtown footprints,
0.59 MB gzipped, pulled 29 Sep 2026 (`prototypes/_shared/nyc/SOURCE.md`).

**There is no current official 3D city.** The only city model is a 2014 capture. The cheapest honest
3D is procedural massing, footprint × measured height, which reads as COMPUTED and never as
photographed. Photoreal exists in one form (Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles), view-only: no caching, no
offline, no derived data, undated. It can be an optional "as photographed" lens; it cannot be the
substrate. An offline one-machine classroom (D12) can stand on neither Google nor Cesium.

**Live Reality is thin.** MTA GTFS-realtime, Citi Bike GBFS and DOT speeds are live; everything else is
a dated snapshot. Most Port Authority open data was last updated in 2015–16 [M]. So "Reality" in a city
is mostly an **as-of stamp**, not a pulse.

**A parcel is not an institution.** PLUTO lists Madison Square Garden's lot (BBL 1007810001) under the
national rail corporation, year built 0, building area 0 [M]. Barclays Center's lot is owned by a
holding entity, not the team. Owner, operator, licensee and team are four different parties that open
data does not separate. Open data supplies the ground truth of structures, never the authority of the
systems inside them. BOW builds the authority layer or nobody does.

**Traps named by A:** Google's no-derived-data rule (BOW logic must run on BOW's own footprints);
ODbL share-alike (keep BOW layers as separate databases joined by id); MTA's mirror / no-modify / lag
terms; portals that overwrite without versions (BOW must snapshot); owner names on 566,788 one- and
two-family lots (drop at ingest — this is a grades 5–6 product line); museum metadata CC0 but images
mostly not; real names and marks are separate rights.

## 2. Can the grammar navigate a Reality City? (lanes B, C, D)

**Yes for structure and authority; no for sums; and only as fast as bridges get authored.** Lane B
built the test (`prototypes/RealityCity.html`, PROPOSED throughout; nothing here is a current product)
on the real Midtown footprints with a dated PLUTO/FacDB excerpt fetched 30 Sep 2026. What happened when
the grammar met the city:

- **"What is here?" returns a site, not an institution.** Clicking a building opens a sheet where
  authorities meet and none of them is the site: the structure (BIN, RECORDED), the lot (BBL, PLUTO),
  the registered operators (FacDB), BOW tenancies (AUTHORED), namespaces kept apart. BIN 1082908 sits
  on a lot titled to a rail corporation, hosts an arena the registries do not name, and has a station
  below it that no footprint sees (drawn as a dashed ring, UNKNOWN). One 1922 office building on
  Eighth Avenue carries 18 FacDB rows from 16 operators [M].
- **ENTER still works per door.** Taking the arena operations seat rewrites the standing line; at the
  club HQ it reads "your word counts for where the club stands · not for the lot's owner, the City,
  the registries". The seat does not travel with you when you SCALE.
- **Placing an authored institution is a checkable act.** Four candidate sites for a fictional club HQ
  run four tests against the real footprints and lot rows — overlap, vacancy, institution, time. Over
  the arena: refused as a building, allowed as a tenancy flag (the refusal names OVERLAP-1). On a
  vacant Fifth Avenue block-front: all four pass, and it renders as a dashed AUTHORED outline — a
  proposal, never a solid building. On a City-owned lot whose boundary the page does not load:
  **CAN'T TELL**, and the checker refuses to draw a guess. Registry vacancy is not physical vacancy,
  and the row says so.
- **A cross-authority act behaves as Jurisdiction predicted.** "Ask for extra post-game trains" is a
  form whose lines the transit desk holds (RULE, TIME, WILL) plus money on two ledgers. Rehearsal says
  NO naming the rule; the first send is **returned at intake naming R-EV-1**; the re-formed request
  (3 trains, 9,900 riders attached) queues with disclosure "count and result only"; the desk answers
  on its own clock with a **partial yes** (2 of 3); the fee moves and is conserved; the transit sheet,
  the map, the pin ribbon, the neighborhood, the record and three separate logs change at once.
  Three records, no god view. (The desk is a scripted stand-in; no feed runs.)
- **Sums fail honestly.** Carried up to "the city", the pin stops at UNKNOWN because no ridership
  denominator is loaded — "not because the map ran out" — and the hole is filed as a Want. The city
  sheet is a *saved question* with no seat and no owner of its definition, and states its coverage
  (4,142 of 1,083,062 footprints, 0.38%).
- **The record found a real fault.** Replay once ran before the footprints loaded, so the HQ act's
  text differed and the club log read MISMATCH. The lesson is a new law: **an act must pin the as-of
  and hash of the evidence it was tested against**, and replay reads the pin, not today's rows
  (footprints refresh weekly).

**The eight amendments the city forces on the act grammar** (lane D; all NO AI): READ lines — a G1
holder gives a read, never a yes; one form clock — a World joins a Reality-dated Form only through a
pinned coupling; one person, one side of a Form; ports before lines (no "ask anything of anyone");
stand-ins are singletons; scarce goods reserve then move while knowledge travels as receipts;
aggregates belong to whoever asks; standing restated at every door.

## 3. SCALE (lane B)

**Yes, under conditions — the one verb that changes the system itself** (lane B §0–3). Each verb owns
one coordinate of the address `system · moment · branch · viewpoint · view`: ENTER/ROLE the
viewpoint, TIME the moment, WHAT IF the branch, the view switch the representation. **SCALE changes
the system, re-derives the viewpoint, and adds a pin**, keeping moment and branch.

**What it is not.** Not camera zoom (L1 plan and L3 massing change the camera, never the subject; the
camera follows the subject, never the reverse). Not "up and down" (from the arena, "up" is six
different things: the neighborhood, the lot's title holder, the club that runs it, the League that
binds it, the transit that serves it, the kind of thing it is). Not a hierarchy of places. Not a global
graph (only the 5–9 relations that exist for the subject in view render).

**Five marks.** RELATION (typed, printed on the hinge, reversible) · PIN (a quantity or an act chosen
at the start, never "a place") · BRIDGE (the conversion from the pin's terms here to its terms next
door, with an owner, a kind and an as-of) · GROUND (the standing line and register rewrite, because
SCALE changes jurisdiction; a pin carries quantity, never authority) · END (the chain stops where a
bridge has no source, as UNKNOWN with a reason and a fileable Want).

**Six laws.** (1) **SITED-1:** location carries only what is physically there — the crowd reaches the
street as a density, but a clinic 290 m away receives nothing, because nothing relates it to the arena;
the hop is refused. (2) A pin wears the kind of its weakest bridge — 18,000 (AUTHORED) × 55% (AUTHORED)
= 9,900 riders is arithmetic and still AUTHORED. (3) A sum is a definer's claim — any aggregate over a
predicate needs an owner and a coverage line. (4) The camera follows the subject. (5) Hops are trail,
not record — navigation is never hashed. (6) A neck exists only where a limit is loaded.

**Nine relation families**, each with its own algebra for the pin: COMPOSES (parts sum, if membership
is defined) · SITES (a density over what is physically there) · SEATS (a duty; no conversion) · TITLES
(a claim; title is not operation) · BINDS (meets a rule; may re-express) · FLOWS (conserved: one ledger
falls, one rises) · SERVES (demand becomes load on a capacity) · KINDS (a position among peers) ·
SHOWS (the same thing in another form). Registry joins (BIN, BBL, facility id) are free RECORDED edges;
SERVES, BINDS and FLOWS are authored one at a time — so a Reality City's edges are dense where a city
registry exists and sparse where authority lives.

**The chains.** Only the biology chain (molecule → organelle → cell → tissue → organ → organism) is
honestly nested, and even there each level has laws its parts lack. Geography appears once or twice in
the sports chains, as a predicate, and not at all in the chemistry, biology and invoice chains. **Every
chain ends on an aggregate or a denominator, never on "the map runs out."**

**Falsifiers (not yet run).** A plain list with a pin and a push ties the drawn sheets on "who can add
a train? / does the clinic get the crowd? / why can't the club go on the Garden?"; people read the pin
ribbon as a route instead of a receipt; SITED-1 reads as a bug ("but it's close"); bridges need an
expert day each; testers insist on up and down; nobody will own "NYC sports economy".

## 4. The knowledge city (lane C)

**Yes as an index; no as a place; and only partial.** The index is *place key → anchored executable
systems → typed relations → a source of record*. A person points at a block, follows a relation, opens
a system at a moment. Nobody walks an avatar. A molecule, an equation and a rule have no address; they
reach the city only through an anchoring institution (a lab, an agency), so the question-first door
(the Bench) must stay beside the map.

- **Native knowledge is a walk, not a page:** ACT and CONSEQUENCE → WHY-walk down typed hinges (BOUND
  BY · WRITTEN AS · MADE BY · PAID TO · RUN BY · PEERS) until it ends at a source or a labelled hole →
  NAME the term after the experience → CARRY it by a PEERS hop to a system of the same shape in
  another domain → TAKE IT OUT to see the present re-form. NO AI by default.
- **A museum has no mechanics.** Its records are RECORDED claims; the executable thing is the world
  the object is evidence of (lane C's vase → Clinton → the 1817 Canal Fund → the 1825 opening), and the
  museum is the provenance layer of that chain.
- **A challenge is a predicate on the record, not a script** (NO AI): a hard clock inside the horizon,
  slack under a rule-computed constraint a seat's lever can reach, and a line of the act held by
  another system. Boston already shows both halves: the $1.706M under the tax line (Jurisdiction) and
  deadline week (Cut). The predicate became a MOMENT when named by its question with the outcome
  sealed.
- **Geography earns geometry only when a number computed from the geometry changes a decision the seat
  can make.** It passed for hospital flood exposure and port bridge clearance, weakly for the museum's
  time-key, as a lesson for the arena's parcel, and failed for the chemistry lab and the market (whose
  operative "where" is a New Jersey data centre and an Illinois matching engine).
- **Falsifier (not yet run):** give newcomers the same six doors as a map or as a list. If
  moves-to-first-WHY-chain and next-day recall show no map advantage on "what is here?" tasks, the
  geography is decoration.

## 5. The network (lane D)

**The city is a directory and a post office, not a state.** No city record, city clock or city hash.
Jurisdictions (each with its own record, ground, seats, clock), typed relations between them, and
Forms that travel between them. A city-level number is computed by whoever asks, wears its weakest
input's kind, and lists who declined to disclose. That is the structural refusal of the god simulation:
no dynamics exist that are not a line someone can read.

**Four grades of authority.**

| Grade | Example | Can say | Cannot |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 Observed | subway alert feed, DOT speeds | facts, inbound only (KNOWING) | any yes; be asked anything |
| G2 Stand-in ("modeled ABOUT, not BY") | a BOW Transit desk following published service standards | a computed RULE-yes; a scripted or modeled WILL-yes stamped "not the operator's answer" | claim the operator agreed; wear its marks |
| G3 BOW-native | a fictional club HQ, Harbor Lights, the BOW League Office | any yes inside its record | any yes about G1 |
| G4 Operator-run | a teacher's, creator's, later an organisation's own jurisdiction | any yes inside its record, by its human seats | anything outside its declared ports |

**BOW holds authority over records, never over reality.** One stand-in per real institution,
registry-owned and versioned, or the cluster ships three subway operators the way the nine boards
shipped three cash figures.

**Eight amendments the city forces on Jurisdiction's act grammar** (all NO AI): READ lines (a G1
holder gives a read, never a yes); one form clock (a World joins a Reality-dated Form only through a
pinned coupling); one person, one side of a Form; ports before lines (no "ask anything of anyone");
stand-ins are singletons; scarce goods reserve then move while knowledge travels as receipts (REAL
seed: a paid Scouting department yields a receipt that unlocks a Harbor film session); aggregates
belong to the requester; standing restated at every door.

**Correspondence, not physics.** Effects cross a boundary only as a declared notice or a Form; the
receiver's own rules decide the effect. A physical coupling (platform crowd → arena gate) is a Declare
in someone's ink, a MODELED edge with a named model that ran, or UNKNOWN.

## 6. The economics (lane E, critic)

**Plausible at one depth only:** recorded ground for the whole city (L0/L1, L3 massing as context) plus
a cluster of about a dozen systems at L0–L2, at most one L5 place, and no L4 on real buildings.

**Cheap to draw, expensive to mean.** Midtown's 4,142 prisms are 0.59 MB gzipped and hosting the whole
city costs tens of dollars a month (estimate). Making New York *mean* something costs facts, site
bindings, rulebooks and review. **The cost ladder runs opposite to the depth ladder:** L0 carries the
most expensive thing (facts) at the cheapest rendering; L3 is the cheapest thing to produce citywide
and also the most visible. A budget allocated by visual depth goes to the wrong place.

**Three missing units.** The frontier's economics priced pixels (Instance, Schema, Kit); Critic 2 added
FACT; lane E adds **SITE** (binding a system to real ground: which BIN/BBL, which authority, which
relation, plus the overlap, vacancy, institution and time tests) and **REVIEW** (a human signature on
anything promoted — the governor of growth). None of the three amortizes across systems.

**The Fact unit is already real in BOW's shipped code.** `runtime/src/modules/sameLine/world.ts` in
bow-economics-live [R]: ~115 sourced rows; club positions re-checked "before any class, and after any
trade deadline"; three of six rows re-checked on 3 Sep 2026 disagreed with the file; nearly every 2026
dollar figure traces through one publisher to one reporter. One league, one rulebook, the
best-documented sport there is, and it already needs a person before every class.

**Occasional × N = continuous.** One open-world compile at ~10⁶ tokens is about $3–14 at list
depending on model. One pass over FacDB's 34,446 facilities is about $10⁵ of compute — and 20–70
person-years of review. **Review, not tokens, stops it.** The one AI cost that scales with *audience*
rather than systems is a per-viewer "ask the city" guide; build it only if it beats typed doors.

**Per useful door.** A first heterogeneous cluster (about 12 first-of-kind schemas) is not cheaper
per useful door than an isolated World. Once verticals exist, a cluster built from them becomes the
cheapest door source, because its marginal cost is Sites plus RECORDED edges on schemas already paid
for. **Order: vertical → flagship → cluster from verticals.** The founder's showcase question ("how
would this transit disruption affect tonight's arena?") needs a MODELED edge, the most expensive and
least validatable kind of door. **Today's honest answer to it contains no headcount** (lanes D, E): the
RECORDED lines at 34 St, the OBSERVED delay with its lag stamp, "MODELED: not run; no model couples
transit to attendance", and a Want.

**Geography does real work for 4 of 13 briefed systems** (arena, transit, port, historical maps),
some work for 2 (hospital, museum), and is **decoration for 7** (club, league, agency, media, sponsors,
finance, chemistry lab). The league — the cluster's most powerful authority — has no useful geography
at all.

**Cluster estimate:** about 1–3 person-years for a cluster honest at L0–L2, under 5% of it spent
drawing the city (estimate; Boston's hours were never recorded — measure before quoting).

**No-regret move:** start the dated, hashed snapshot archive of footprints, PLUTO, FacDB and the MTA
feeds now (~$10¹/month). Knowledge time that was never recorded cannot be recovered; the sources
overwrite and keep no versions.

## 8. First path

Lanes D and E converge, with one amendment from lane B's bridge finding:

1. **OneRecord** (built, `prototypes/OneRecord.html`) — one record, two clients, the address in the URL,
   SHA-256 over canonical acts. No city before it.
2. **Boston**, the vertical and flagship — it faces the two costs that grow fastest in a city (sourced
   facts, real-people rights). Gate: people outside the builders enter a recorded moment and make a
   checked act.
3. **Foundry's partner-agreement tournament**, in parallel, before anything in New York is compiled.
4. **New York, thin: one night, one district, five or six systems** drawn from what the verticals
   already cover — arena desk; a club HQ AUTHORED beside a real building with the tests visible; the
   BOW League Office; a Transit stand-in on an alert mirror or a dated fixture; one non-sports member;
   media as KNOWING only. Its first MODELED bridge (crowd → riders) is authored and reviewed as a named
   model or it stays a Declare. Gate: two independent operators hold at least two members; a Form
   completes, is refused and lapses across their records; children of 10–12 tell Reality from Authored
   by frame.
5. **Harbor: judged last, run first** — its proof is elapsed seasons with returning owners.

**Swap New York and Harbor** if Foundry shows no faster second author or no second operator can be
recruited. **Do not** start with a heterogeneous twelve-system cluster: first-of-kind everywhere is the
worst case for amortization (lane E §7).

## 9. Decisions for the founder (ranked, from lane E, amended by D)

1. **Truth posture per system (the Fact budget).** REALITY only where facts are cheap and authoritative
   (registries, the CBA, filings, the MTA feed); elsewhere AUTHORED institutions on RECORDED ground,
   no real operator implied. *Changes if* a one-month two-system pilot measures fact hours under a
   ceiling you set, or a user test finds AUTHORED-on-real-ground misread as real.
2. **Build order: vertical → flagship → cluster from verticals**, not cluster first. *Changes if* a
   second club from the Boston kit costs nearly what Boston did (verticals do not amortize).
3. **Depth ceiling and substrate.** Own the ground (footprints + self-hosted tiles); L3 as context
   only; L2 per domain; at most one L5 place; no L4 on real buildings; photoreal only as a dated
   optional lens. *Changes if* L3 fails a real Chromebook frame test (drop to L1), or a task test shows
   map/massing beating a list on a cross-system question (raise L3).
4. **Liveness and licensing.** Dated snapshots by default, MTA as the one live pulse; no official
   league or market-data licence until a named product needs one. Sportradar is the NBA's exclusive
   data provider [P]; NBA.com statistics terms allow only news or private non-commercial use [P];
   whether any BOW use qualifies is a question for counsel.
5. **Review capacity governs growth.** A fixed monthly review budget; compile only past a Want
   threshold; no generated institution without review; Studio meters human hours.
