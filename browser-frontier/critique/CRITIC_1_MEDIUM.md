# Critic 1 — Interaction / Medium

Lens: is this a new interaction model, or search / a game / a dashboard / chat / a 3D environment /
a knowledge graph with new language? Phase 1 was written from the rendered product only: the builders'
screenshots plus my own harness plays (`harness/shots/critic1-<Name>/`, 1440×900), including wrong
moves. No spec, hypothesis map, research or tournament doc was opened before Phase 1 was saved.
Quotes are on-screen text I reached.

## 1. Per board

### Institution (3D)

**What it actually is.** A three.js warehouse: you look at it as a model ("maquette"), arrive at its
door, and move between eight fixed camera stations. A side panel holds every verb. Signing at the desk
changes lit rooms, signs, the record and the address.

**Reduces to.** A point-and-click virtual tour with a control sidebar. The eight stations are camera
bookmarks. Every act is a sidebar button: clicking the 3D model did nothing in my play. Compare and
Schedule leave the building for a flat event log and a table.

**Moments a website or game could not have.** After "Fund Scouting · $2.0M", the Scouting sign changes
from "Not funded · $2.0M would open it" to "Funded · Week 9 · $2.0M", the room behind the glass lights
up, the record gains "43 Fund Scouting · $2.0M" in blue ink and the blotter shows act 43. That is one
act drawn in three places. In the Reality maquette, the rooms are sheeted: "nobody outside knows their
funding. The World knows more than Reality because someone wrote it." "What if?" on the Week 6 arch
puts an orange frame on the viewport ("YOUR BRANCH · WEEK 6 TRADE NOT MADE · … CUT WITH HINDSIGHT"),
puts scaffolding in the arch, and recomputes the roster board to $196.3M. The door plate reads
"Owed: 3 · Lapsed while you were away: 1".

**Rubric**
- MEDIUM-NATIVENESS — OK: the place is bound to state, but take the 3D away and nothing about operating changes.
- EXECUTES OR TOURS — STRONG for the one act offered (room, sign, record, clock). Only one act is offered.
- CORE-SEQUENCE — about 12 of 16. Missing: DISCOVER, CONTINUE ("Week 9 is not replayed"), FOLLOW.
- ENTER — STRONG: "Sitting is free. Signing is the act." / "YOU GIVE UP: the outside view … everything after Week 9 (sealed). YOU GET: 3 matters, the pen, your clock." Two clocks sit on the desk. But "Ledger check … (asserted)".
- TIME — OK: World clock and your clock; history drawn as bricked arches. Time never advances.
- WHAT IF / FORK — OK: a real branch in its own ink with its assumptions listed. It forks instantly on click, with no Council, and it cannot run on.
- TRUTH GRAMMAR — OK: "four ways not to know" (Withheld / Unbought / Unlived / Untraced) is strong. Reality and the World blur (see risks).
- COMPREHENSION — WEAK: the first screen is an isometric box plus a sidebar.
- EMOTIONAL PULL — STRONG: the only board where I felt inside Boston.
- RESTRAINT — WEAK: the sidebar holds more than 20 buttons.
- ORIGINALITY — OK.

**Biggest failure.** The place is a display, not the interface.

**Truth / provenance.** In Reality mode, the panel still shows "THE RECORD · YOUR INK IS BLUE" with the
World's acts 39–42. The address stays "boston · year-two … maquette", and the toggle still reads "Show
Boston · Reality instead". The "What's real?" layer calls a rail station "REAL CURRENT PRODUCT
CAPABILITY · UNMERGED BRANCH".

**Test.** Five newcomers: "Make one decision and show me where it changed the building." Can they point
at Scouting without reading the sidebar?

**VERDICT: PUSH** — combine with Concordance, where the Room becomes one view of one ledger.

**Top 3 repairs**
1. Put the verbs on the objects: click the file, the pen, a room.
2. Fix the Reality/World bleed: rewrite the address, swap the record, label the toggle.
3. Let the branch play forward, and compare across the footbridge in the building rather than in a table. Also fix the clipped orphan "pick" row at the top of the record list and the two unlabeled buttons that appear after signing.

### Cut

**What it actually is.** A typeset "moment" instrument. At NOW (live clock, two payroll numbers, a rail
of doors), you arrive at last year's deadline door, pick a cut (2 Feb or 5 Feb), and stand in four
planes: On the books / On the desk / Not yet / Since. You take the seat, choose one of three moves,
seat a Council, keep a branch and advance it while Reality arrives on its own dates.

**Reduces to.** A branching historical scenario page with a date picker. The act is a three-item menu
of moves each tagged "AUTHORED, illustrative".

**Moments.**
- "Later ▸" → "Nobody at this desk could know what comes next."
- The Council: "Recorded needs a source dated on or before the cut … Declare it yourself, or leave it Unknown." / "MODELED needs a named, versioned model that actually ran." / "BLOCKED · 2 SEATS UNSET".
- The Seam at 6 Mar: "TORN · impossible — Simons had already left in your branch … Reality did something your branch has no room for. You did not cause it."
- Return to NOW: "Reality's clock ran 0 min 17 s … Your branch b1 is pinned: it waits at 6 Mar where you paced it."
- Backs: "Trail · pencil — Mine. Private. Discarded freely / Record · print — Never changed / Acts · signature — Append-only. No Back, only acting."
- The scrubber baseline: "LEAK COUNTER 4" against "Facts on screen dated after the cut: 0".

**Rubric**
- MEDIUM-NATIVENESS — OK.
- EXECUTES OR TOURS — OK: branch tax recomputes (≈$39.5M → ≈$22.0M), but the salary effect is authored.
- CORE-SEQUENCE — about 14 of 16, the widest in the set. Missing: PLACE responds.
- ENTER — OK: "HINDSIGHT: NONE / SELF-DECLARED" stamp; knowledge narrows ("2026–27 lines are not on this desk"). No obligations and no clock of your own.
- TIME — STRONG: the shuttered Reality window ticks "about 8 months ahead of this cut".
- WHAT IF / FORK — STRONG.
- TRUTH GRAMMAR — OK (see risks).
- COMPREHENSION — WEAK: the first two things I tried (the scheduled door, the 2017 door) were both refused.
- EMOTIONAL PULL — OK.
- RESTRAINT — OK.
- ORIGINALITY — STRONG.

**Biggest failure.** The Council is richer than the act. The act is a lookup, and 4 of the 5 doors
behind you are refused.

**Truth / provenance.** "Lost in the first round to Philadelphia, 3–4, after leading 3–1" is drawn as a
solid FACT from "web search … [verify]", so a solid texture sits on an unverified claim. The headline
"≈ $17M" sits beside a correction reading "≈ $0.84M UNDER the tax line". Named reporters are cited;
verify them.

**Test.** Three fans who know the outcome and three non-fans at 2 Feb. Do the fans press "I already
know"? Can the non-fans explain TORN?

**VERDICT: KEEP** — the time and fork grammar. Combine with Jurisdiction's paper as the act.

**Top 3 repairs**
1. Replace the three-button move list with a rule-checked instrument, so the branch's numbers are computed.
2. Show unauthored doors as unlit, not as traps.
3. Fix the solid-on-[verify] rows. Also fix the rail where the branch bar overwrites "outcome · empty", and the What's-real tags that overlap and clip content.

### Jurisdiction

**What it actually is.** A registry office. You ask at a counter and are placed at a Payroll sheet,
where the pin (tax salary) sits against four CBA lines. You open relations: Peers, Run by, Paid to,
Written as, Bound by, Made by. You push the pin in pencil. Then you take the GM seat in your World and
file a trade paper that needs lines from Boston, Denver, the League and the player: rehearse it, send
it, wait on other seats' clocks, and read three records.

**Reduces to.** Linked dashboard sheets plus a multi-party approval workflow (a routing slip). The
trade flow resembles the ESPN trade machine or NBA 2K's "trade rejected".

**Moments.**
- Bound by: at $225,022,406 the verbs are struck ("send cash in a trade [verify]" …); "release and the pencil erases. Nothing forks."
- The paper: "DENVER — Not yours to fill." / "PLAYER … Struck … An absent line is information." / "MEDIA · no line · KNOWING — Media never says yes; it learns."
- Dry run: "CAN'T TELL Denver — … That reads Denver's payroll, which sits on Denver's desk, not yours."
- The wrong move, which I made on purpose: I sent with $0.5M cash. Denver signed "on Denver's clock", then "The League returned the file … a team over it may not send cash in a trade", and the record says "Nothing changed on Boston's books".
- Resent without cash: "Payroll $222.8M → $220.351M; roster 15 → 14 … 2nd-apron verbs restored", under "THREE RECORDS OF ONE ACT · NO GOD VIEW" ("Denver's payroll after: nothing shown to you"). The Reality edge stays "unchanged: $1.706M under the tax line".

**Rubric**
- MEDIUM-NATIVENESS — OK.
- EXECUTES OR TOURS — STRONG, the best in the set: a wrong act propagates as a refusal from another seat, naming the rule.
- CORE-SEQUENCE — about 13. Missing: CONTINUE, PLACE.
- ENTER — STRONG: the header rewrites from "your word counts for: nothing here" to "signing, trading, payroll · not for: approving trades, setting the tax line, scheduling the arena", and the ground colour and the address change with it.
- TIME — OK: "League hours, not yours"; review time UNKNOWN.
- WHAT IF / FORK — OK: the pencil is a preview by design; the kept redline is a static document fork.
- TRUTH GRAMMAR — STRONG: CONTENT / COUNT AND RESULT / SHAPE ONLY / NOTHING.
- COMPREHENSION — WEAK: jargon ("the neck", "hinge") and three dense columns.
- EMOTIONAL PULL — OK: "What you can change: what you ask (RE-FORM), never their answer."
- RESTRAINT — WEAK.
- ORIGINALITY — STRONG.

**Biggest failure.** The front door. I typed "Will Boston trade Jaylen Brown?" and got "Refused: no sheet
here holds that … Try one of the three doors."

**Truth / provenance.** "18 of 30 clubs sit within $5M of a line" rests on 29 illustrative payrolls.
They are labelled, but the sentence reads as fact.

**Test.** Two people as Boston and Denver on two laptops with the same paper.

**VERDICT: KEEP** — this is the ACT grammar.

**Top 3 repairs**
1. Lead arrival with the pin and the paper; relations on request.
2. Let the redline branch run (the Denver stand-in answers, the League rules on it).
3. Place free questions somewhere honest. Also fix the "6 verbs struck at this payroll" label that overlaps the pin marker at 1280.

### Guest

**What it actually is.** Ten tabs that simulate a person's day across fictional host surfaces:
assistant, article, text message, voice, offer, ring, orders, round, standing, floor. BOW appears as a
framed card that verifies, offers, rings, answers and reports, beside a running Record.

**Reduces to.** A carousel of integration mock-ups: a fact-check widget, an invite link, a push
notification, auto-reply rules, a subscriptions list.

**Moments.**
- The claim card: "'…Boston is past the tax line this season…' DOES NOT MATCH THE RECORD · True of cap payroll. Not true of tax salary", plus "Claim set hash 47dc5c26".
- The Offer: "Sign is blocked until each named matter is taken on." A second device gets "Not for you".
- The Ring: "On call · in the seat … You are in the seat until you hang up."
- Away 14 days: "BY YOUR STANDING ORDER §2 · not by you · Answered accepted", with "Take it out" producing a branch beside the record.
- The Round: "YOU CAN NO LONGER DO: Convert the two-way call-up … The World recorded: no act by you."
- Voice: "Did you mean 'harbor, linen'? … A near-miss never lands on a neighbour; it asks."

**Rubric**
- MEDIUM-NATIVENESS — WEAK as a place, which is intentional; the premise is the external-invocation frontier.
- EXECUTES OR TOURS — OK: acts carry into the books (payroll $222.8M → $220.35M, roster 15 → 14) and the Round's seat diff. But it is linear ("Nothing can ring you: you hold no seat.").
- CORE-SEQUENCE — about 12.
- ENTER — STRONG: WHO OFFERS / WHICH SEAT / WHAT YOU WILL SEE / WHAT YOU GIVE UP / WHAT STAYS PRIVATE.
- TIME — STRONG: the World runs while you are away.
- WHAT IF / FORK — OK: a private dry run; "Take it out".
- TRUTH GRAMMAR — OK: the hollow squares read as unchecked checkboxes. §1 and §2 show empty boxes, yet §2 fired.
- COMPREHENSION — OK room by room; the day does not cohere without the tabs.
- EMOTIONAL PULL — OK: the lapse happened without me.
- RESTRAINT — STRONG.
- ORIGINALITY — STRONG.

**Biggest failure.** A scripted tour. I typed my own question into the assistant; it was silently
replaced by "Is Boston over the tax line?", and the Record logs the scripted question.

**Truth / provenance.** Every host is labelled fictional. Good.

**Test.** Send a real text with a Follow phrase and open it three weeks later. Is the drift note
welcome? Also: a real ring at a real deadline.

**VERDICT: PUSH** — the away / standing order / round loop. Combine with EnterTournament E4.

**Top 3 repairs**
1. Never silently replace user input.
2. Turn ten tabs into one day with a clock you advance.
3. Give the textures a form that does not read as form controls.

### Bench

**What it actually is.** A sheet-tabbed workbench. A question is restated as a claim plus a "dynamic";
parts arrive with provenance and wire into a Marey-chart "Loom" you push until it breaks. You can take
a practice seat, lift parts, keep a branch, turn the mechanism over to its "Plate", split a plural
question, compile an unreviewed model, review it, or file a Want.

**Reduces to.** A systems-modelling notebook (Loopy / Insight Maker) inside a wizard of sheets A–H.

**Moments.**
- My own paraphrase, "Why do my flights keep getting delayed?", resolved to "A delay at one airport shows up later, elsewhere."
- Pushing the ORD hold: "At 101 min leg 4 is cut; at 100 min it still runs." → "A person can extend this, up to 2 h (14 CFR 117.19, verify)" → "Who holds it?"
- The practice seat: "Cannot: release the ORD hold (air traffic control)"; the Release button is refused.
- The Plate: "c2 · CLOCK · wired by a generator · 5 unsound inputs · 0 sourced".
- "WORKING MODEL · NOT REVIEWED · life left 71 h 59 min".

**Rubric**
- MEDIUM-NATIVENESS — OK.
- EXECUTES OR TOURS — STRONG inside the Loom: every thread, part verb and record entry moves.
- CORE-SEQUENCE — about 13.
- ENTER — OK: a practice seat on "an authored day".
- TIME — OK.
- WHAT IF / FORK — OK: a parameter branch.
- TRUTH GRAMMAR — STRONG: "Ports: 0 LIVE (no feed exists) · 1 FIXTURE · 1 ASSUMED · 2 EMPTY".
- COMPREHENSION — OK.
- EMOTIONAL PULL — WEAK: no Boston, no people.
- RESTRAINT — OK.
- ORIGINALITY — STRONG.

**Biggest failure.** I asked the question every other board answers, "Why is Boston close to the tax
line?". It returned "No system for this … It will not invent one", with the Want "Filed under … North
Station [verify]". That is the Green Line's place stamped on an unrelated question, which looks like a
bug. The resolver is three authored prompts.

**Truth / provenance.** US Commerce RFI and 14 CFR are cited with verify. Good.

**Test.** Ten people bring their own "why". Count systems against Wants, and ask whether the Want felt
honest or like a 404.

**VERDICT: PUSH** — as the discovery lane. Wire Boston first.

**Top 3 repairs**
1. Resolve the tax-line question to the payroll mechanism Jurisdiction already has.
2. Fix the Want filing place.
3. Let a kept branch run a second day.

### TakeItOut

**Reduces to.** A counterfactual toggle panel — the what-if dashboard genre, applied to your own log.

**Moments.**

- "Put it back" → the hashes match (7d4d2e).
- Reality: "impossible without Jun 2025 Holiday trade — Simons arrived in that trade" and "results are not derived from rules, so they are not guessed."
- "Week 10 played · Won 117–109 at Phoenix. AUTHORED: the toy engine's output."

**Rubric**
- MEDIUM-NATIVENESS — OK.
- EXECUTES OR TOURS — STRONG.
- CORE-SEQUENCE — about 9. It is the only board where a fork continues with new acts.
- ENTER — FAILS: you start as GM.
- TIME — OK.
- WHAT IF / FORK — STRONG: fixed luck makes it a controlled comparison.
- TRUTH GRAMMAR — OK: toy-engine scores are drawn in the same red ink as computed payroll.
- COMPREHENSION — STRONG: "Hold an act to lift it out."
- EMOTIONAL PULL — OK.
- RESTRAINT — STRONG.
- ORIGINALITY — OK.

**Biggest failure.** It analyses a fixed log rather than operating a system. The forward acts are four

**Truth / provenance.** "engine toy-0.1" produces scores that look like results.

**VERDICT: KEEP** — as the WHY / WHAT-IF operator inside another board.

## 2. Across boards

**(a) Ranking the seven boards by evidence of a new medium**
1. **Jurisdiction.** One act crosses seats with their own clocks; a wrong act is refused by another seat citing the rule; three records exist with no god view; entering rewrites what "your word counts for".
2. **Cut.** The most original time grammar (four planes, a shuttered Reality, the Seam, three Backs), but a menu act.
3. **Institution.** The only place that changes because you acted (Scouting lights). The verbs live in a sidebar.
4. **Parting.** Forks that grow, are inhabited and hit composition limits — but scripted.
5. **Guest.** BOW rings you, and your standing orders act while you are away — shown as a tour of fake apps.
6. **Bench.** A mechanism assembled from a question, with its edge found by pushing. Off the proving ground; the Boston question fails.
7. **TakeItOut.** The best-made and least new: a counterfactual toggle panel on a log.

**(b) Strongest and strangest.** Strongest: Jurisdiction. Strangest but potentially most important:
Guest — the Browser stops being a destination; the system calls the seat, and absence is recorded.
Runner-up: Parting's agent seated "under warrant W-2 … may not sign".

**(c) ENTER — the seven lanes, ranked**
1. **E0 Handover.** Owing, blank lines refused, leaving is an act.
2. **E4 Ring.** Being called; the default runs; standing orders answer while you are away.
3. **E5 Take a Face.** The seat as the unheld face of a shared paper; the right model for multi-party acts.
4. **E2 Seal.** Right as a rule (the stamp rides every act), not as a door.
5. **E3 Offer.** Permission plumbing.
6. **E1 Sit, then Sign.** Spatial dressing of E0; Institution already does it.
7. **E6 Lever.** Its own strip says "Nothing about who you are changes."

**Canon:** E0 as the core, with E2's stamp and E3's redemption as its rules, and E4 as the second
mode of entry (being called). E5 governs multi-party acts. **Kill** E6 as an ENTER (keep it as the Pull
instrument). Fold E1 into Institution. Both Jurisdiction's "your word counts for / not for" line and
Guest's five-part threshold beat every lane as the visible sign of having crossed. Adopt them.

**(d) TIME.** The Cut with four planes wins for standing at a moment, and the Seam wins for time after
divergence. Treat them as one instrument. The scrubber baseline is the control that proves the cut
("LEAK COUNTER 4" against 0). Keep it as a test, not as UI. Plates-in-place (bricked arches) is
decoration. Lifting acts is a causal operator, not time navigation. Missing from the list and
necessary: Guest's Round — how time you did not spend reaches you.

**(e) WHAT IF.** Not a duplicate, scenario or preview: the Cut's Council branch. Every touched seat is
Recorded / Declared / Modeled / Unknown, and Reality later grades those claims (held / torn /
re-declare). The one that grows: Parting's public fork — people plus an agent under warrant, fork
again from Year 5, and the closure rule when it touches another club. The others:
- Institution — an instant kept preview.
- Jurisdiction — the pencil is a preview by design; the redline is a document fork.
- TakeItOut — a subtraction scenario that can continue.
- Bench — a parameter scenario.
- Concordance — a copy with a different act.
- Guest — a dry run.

**(f) The one flow that would convince a stranger.**
1. Your phone rings from the seat (Guest Ring).
2. You pick up and the header rewrites "your word counts for" (Jurisdiction).
3. You sign your line of the Denver paper; Denver signs on Denver's clock; the League returns it, naming the second-apron rule. You take the cash out and resend.
4. The building's roster board and lit rooms change as act 43 (Institution).
5. You hang up. A standing order answers Denver's second call while you are away.
6. On return, the Round says what you can no longer do.
7. You lift that trade (TakeItOut), keep the fork, and later the Seam tells you which assumptions Reality tore (Cut).

That flow needs one record shared by five boards. Today none of them share one.

**(g) What these boards still reduce to, honestly.** Beautifully typeset, rule-honest, branching case
studies over one hand-authored fixture.
- Every act is one of one to four authored buttons with authored effects.
- Other seats (Denver, the League, other pairs, agents) are stand-ins.
- Nothing runs without you except scripted clocks.
- Nine boards bring nine address bars, nine record columns and nine home screens: the same three-column web app (nav / content / record).
- The truth textures, drawn as small squares, read as checkboxes.
- The Institution, Jurisdiction's trade flow and TakeItOut each have a familiar twin: a virtual tour, the 2K trade machine, a what-if dashboard.

**(h) The interaction impossible to explain as a website or game.** The Seam. A fork of recorded
history is graded by real history arriving on its own dates: "TORN — Reality did something your
branch has no room for. You did not cause it." A game cannot do this, because its world does not
continue without it. A website cannot, because nothing it shows is yours. It is also the interaction
most dependent on a live Reality feed that does not exist.

**Is there a new medium here yet?** Not yet in one place. The pieces are real and new: the Seam, the
multi-seat paper with no god view, a place that changes with state, being rung by a seat you hold.
None of them executes a system beyond authored lookups, and no act made in one board appears in
another board's record. That shared record is the test.
