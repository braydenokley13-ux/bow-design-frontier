# BOW Browser Founding Frontier — shared prior-art brief (read first, every agent)

Date: 29 Sep 2026. Author: parent session. This brief is compressed on purpose; cite it instead of
re-reading the sources. Sources are named so you can verify one fact, not so you read them all.

## 1. What BOW is trying to become

BOW is exploring a new medium: MAKE SYSTEMS EXECUTABLE. A person should be able to ENTER,
UNDERSTAND, OPERATE, CHANGE, FORK, COMPARE, FOLLOW and SHARE a system rather than read about it.
BOW Browser may become the native human interface for navigating reality as systems: billions of
executable systems, some continuously updated by live Reality; systems inside systems; humans and
AI agents occupying roles; public forks that persist for years; creators and third-party runtimes;
other apps invoking BOW; spatial devices rendering the same system; moving from a global system
down to one institution, person, object or rule; moving from observed Reality into modeled
possibility without confusing the two.

BOW Browser is NOT: Chrome with BOW tabs · Google with World cards · ChatGPT with buttons ·
Netflix for simulations · a dashboard · an app launcher · a file explorer · a 3D metaverse shell ·
a search bar plus immersive pages. If an idea reduces to one of these, say so.

First flagship proving ground: SPORTS / BASKETBALL, specifically the BOSTON basketball
institution. The winning grammar must later survive chemistry, supply chain / enterprise, and one
unrelated awkward domain. APUSH / history is NOT the conceptual center.

## 2. What three earlier design waves already found (bow-design-frontier, 60+ boards)

These are design readings of rendered prototypes judged by independent critics. No human has
used any of it. Treat them as INCUMBENTS to beat or to build on — never as settled truth, and
never re-propose them as if new.

Canon CANDIDATES (founder has not ruled):
- Truth grammar: seven epistemic states drawn as texture, words on request —
  OBSERVED solid · RECORDED double-ruled · AUTHORED outline · COMPUTED toned · MODELED 135° hatch
  (only when a named model actually ran) · GENERATED stipple · UNKNOWN dashed-empty.
  A fork is a frame in its own ink with a stamp ("Your branch · diverged <moment>"), never a fill
  over the record. The unmodeled future is SEALED (dashed, "you find out by living it").
- A door into a past moment is named by its QUESTION, never its answer ("17 Jun 2017 ·
  Philadelphia calls about No. 1", not "Boston trades No. 1"). Sealed hindsight: the outcome is
  revealed only after the person in the seat acts, or to an observer who asks.
- Home = "the Clock Hall": each system you are in is a presence that keeps its own KIND of time
  (live clock / World calendar / frozen date / run counter / divergence stamp); a World that won't
  wait records what lapsed while you were away. Following is keeping.
- Search = "Arrive": a question places you inside the state that answers it, with four edges:
  provenance above, a river of doors (past moments) below, seats left, WHAT IF? right.
- Address = one editable line "system · moment · branch · seat · view" that rewrites on every move
  and is what you share.
- ENTER = "The Handover" (winner): you sign for named open matters line by line; your clock starts
  beside the World's; knowledge narrows and a ledger says what you gave up / got; your ink appears;
  NO ACT is recorded as absence, never as a choice; leaving is an act (hand over in your own words,
  or vacate). Runner-up: "The Clock Starts" (the scrubber leaves your hand). Killed: the Narrowing
  as a model, the Descent (four altitudes lost to a plain list).
- WHAT IF? = "The Council": a cut convenes every actor it touches, each set to Recorded / Declare /
  Model / Unknown; Keep is blocked while a needed seat is unset. "Living fork": a kept branch is
  reconciled as Reality arrives (held / diverged / impossible / re-declare). "Pull": drag a governed
  value across rule lines; release and nothing forked. Fan killed as a fork (it forecasts).
- Atlas = "Mechanism": a question resolves into a running mechanism assembled for it, stamped
  GENERATED STRUCTURE until a reviewed system replaces it; you find its edge by pushing ("At 96 min
  the crew's limit runs out and leg 4 is cut. At 95 it still runs."). Excavation lens: the drill
  line turns dashed below what can be traced.
- Network = "The Handshake": one cross-institution act signed by both sides, stamped once by the
  League, recorded three ways, no god-view (even the League lens can't read Denver's desk).
- Publisher: a story with a door; the Moment card is the unit that travels.
- Real people are observed with sources, not worn; actor models are stippled and answer ABOUT a
  person, never AS the person. Refusals name the rule that refused.

What the critics said was NOT yet achieved: "Is this a new medium? Not yet in one place." The
flagship composition "ticks rather than executes" — acts did not propagate through state until a
late repair (unjudged). Wave 3 moved to engines: a causal engine (every number knows why), a seat
engine (knowledge belongs to the seat), lockstep (same World for everyone from seed + act log), a
SHA-256 record that verifies forks. A clone test showed every screen could be copied from
screenshots in ~12–22 tool calls. The moat is not screens: it is (1) one canonical World and record
everyone can check against, (2) a verified, sourced rulebook correct on both sides of every trade,
(3) accumulated history of what real people did in real seats. Wave 3 recommendation: stop inventing
front doors; converge; run it for real; put it in front of people.

Open questions the founder has NOT ruled: which does a newcomer meet first (Clock Hall or an ask
line)? Does signing create stakes or friction? Are Worlds that run while you're away compelling or
punishing? May a branch be cut with hindsight? Can a 10–12-year-old read seven textures without
words? Rights posture for real teams/players/likeness. Smallest real test that would FALSIFY "new
medium"?

This wave must push FARTHER than the incumbents, especially on: scale (billions of systems),
discovery beyond search, time as a navigation dimension, spatial representation, systems whose
boundaries cross into other systems, external invocation, open-world compilation, representation
and AI economics, trust/provenance for third-party systems.

## 3. What EXISTS TODAY in real code (label: REAL CURRENT PRODUCT CAPABILITY)

Only these. Anything else you draw is PROPOSED PLATFORM CAPABILITY or SPECULATIVE FRONTIER.

- BOW Economics Live (repo `bow-economics-live`, main): a live classroom product for grades 5–6.
  One Node server, three surfaces per session — /play (student device, private), /teach (teacher
  control room), /board (projector, public). Track 101 lessons ("The Window", "The Season",
  "The Deadline") on Boston's and other clubs' real books. A sourced, dated fact store
  (`runtime/src/modules/sameLine/world.ts`, `asOf` per row) and a CBA rules engine proven by
  exhaustive sweep. Snapshot persistence; survives refresh and restart.
- An NBA League "World" (same repo, branch `codex/bow-worlds-complete-ultra-20260925`, unmerged):
  server-owned; real clubs and players, a simulated season ("a BOW league: real clubs and players,
  a simulated season"); owners hold clubs; trades, votes, staff loans, drafts; a League Office with
  a public vote record that shows only count and result; two-sided "league tables" whose papers move
  offer → signed → delivered; public notice shows signed facts while pending terms stay owner-scoped.
  Plus "Harbor Lights", a fictional club World with eight places (Home, owner desk, arena,
  basketball, staff, people, Moments, Lab), 2.5D rooms that change with saved state, and a
  read-only route connecting a Harbor memory to an NBA choice while their saves and authority stay
  separate (a real seed of composable systems). Place rule: "an object must show something that
  happened, something promised, something owed, or something the owner can decide."
- The Boston spatial work (branch `claude/quirky-maxwell-s26mxx` @ 09051acf, unmerged; screenshots
  copied to `browser-frontier/evidence/boston-spatial/`): a three.js interior — "Boston Celtics ·
  Basketball Operations", a converted harbour warehouse. From the owner's mezzanine rail you see the
  ops floor, four department rooms behind glass (lit and working when FUNDED in the save, dark with
  chairs stacked when not — "opportunity cost you can see from the rail"), the practice court, a
  window wall onto water and the arena. Surfaces are bound to canonical World state: a roster board
  "Celtics · Who we pay — Payroll $222.8M, $22.8M over the tax line, projected tax $34.2M, arrived by
  trade: Jimmy Butler" (a BOW World fact, not NBA history); a trade file on the owner's desk you can
  pick up ("Trade file with the Golden State Warriors: send Derrick White, get Jimmy Butler. Signed
  by both owners." → "What did this trade touch?" walks a chain of consequences); a film room; a
  game-day door. The arena bowl renders THREE kinds of night differently: PLAYED (seated crowd,
  "Won 135–115, 16,440 in the seats of 18,624"), ESTIMATE (bowl covered, "About 18,454 expected —
  an estimate, not a played night"), NOT KNOWN (dark bowl, "No seat forecast · Demand, in words:
  fans turned away"). Eight fixed camera stations are listed as buttons (the floor from the rail,
  owner's desk, ops floor, practice court, analytics & scouting, ticketing & partnerships, film
  room, game-day door). Pipeline: Blender-baked lightmaps + CC0 materials, live facts re-texture
  state-bound surfaces; budgets ~15k triangles, one draw call. HONEST STATUS: founder judged the first
  3D trial poor; the newer scenes have screenshots from automated runs but no human verdict, no
  device frame-time, no accessibility pass. People are NOT drawn (no presence truth exists).
  Founder-approved North Star mood images (AI-generated, GENERATED) sit at the repo root:
  waterfront campus, departments visible to each other through glass, the city in every window,
  consequential objects in the foreground, history as architecture, "scale changes as a gesture:
  object → person → department → possession → game → League". Their lesson: "transparency is the
  organization"; the product can beat the mockups because "every mark in our frame is true".
- bow-design-frontier: the prototype boards above; the Live World page (`live/live-world.html`): one
  persistent shared World, Boston and Denver GM seats by lease, real deadlines, private boards,
  every browser folds the same act log to the same SHA-256 state. Built; not yet run on the real
  shared store.
- BOW Decision Challenges (separate product): post-instructional personal-finance assessment
  worlds with a strict evidence chain. Not the Browser; do not merge its assessment semantics in.

Does NOT exist anywhere: a Browser runtime; a registry of systems; an address protocol; a live
Reality feed (only dated sourced fixtures); open-world system compilation; third-party runtimes;
cross-surface invocation; production AI actors in seats; composition across more than the one
read-only Harbor↔NBA crossing.

## 5. Capability labels (mandatory on every concept and prototype)

- REAL CURRENT PRODUCT CAPABILITY — exists in a BOW repo today (section 3 only).
- PROPOSED PLATFORM CAPABILITY — plausible to build on known technology; does not exist.
- SPECULATIVE FRONTIER — depends on unsolved problems (e.g., automatic reality compilation).
Never imply a mocked platform behavior already exists.
