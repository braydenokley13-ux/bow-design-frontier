state as the substrate on which many autonomous executable systems (a basketball club, an arena, a
league, an agency, media, sponsors, finance, transit, a port, a hospital, a university chemistry lab, a

authority — without becoming a god simulation, a metaverse or a walking simulator, and without letting
3D realism blur REALITY · BOW WORLD · RECORDED MOMENT · MODELED · GENERATED · UNKNOWN (with reason)?

institution that exists); NEW YORK = CONNECT / CIVILIZATION (connect autonomous systems on real
geography); HARBOR LIGHTS = CREATE / HISTORY (create history from almost nothing). Do not collapse them.
