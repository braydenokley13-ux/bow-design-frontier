What this changes about the design

  • Transparency stays. Printing the formula taught the copier — and it is still right. BOW’s value is that anyone can check it; the moat was never going to be secrecy.
  • Every surface becomes a view onto three shared things: the canonical record, the verified rules, the accumulated history. A board that doesn’t read from them is a demo, however good it looks.
  • Invention moves from screens to substrate. Waves 1–2 found the grammar. The work now is breadth and correctness under it — which is slow, and that is the point.
  • Engines must prove more than they display. The seats engine guards what is rendered, not what can be inferred; the rules engine checked one side of each trade. Both overclaimed on their own boards, and both lines are now corrected.
