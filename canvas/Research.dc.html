The pattern across all twenty

Each paradigm owns one dimension well — the map owns scale, git owns branches, the spreadsheet owns recomputation, the game owns attachment, the terminal owns density. None owns them together, and none carries provenance as a first-class property.

Borrow outright

Semantic zoom (maps) · append-only history and blame (git) · dependency recomputation and "show formulas" (spreadsheets) · variation notation and the opening explorer (chess) · predict-before-reveal (explorable explanations) · fog of war and the after-action review (wargames) · time-travel debugging (devtools).

Refuse outright

Ranked lists as answers · chat as the primary surface for a system · the dashboard of tiles · the infinite canvas as the only authoring metaphor · opaque formulas behind a game's numbers · merge as the goal of branching · realism as the goal of 3D.
