## The job
Re-express the model's core loop — ARRIVE → ORIENT → ENTER → ACT → STATE CHANGE → WHY → WHAT IF →
FORK → COMPARE → RETURN — in THREE domains, keeping the grammar identical and changing only the nouns
and the art direction. Do not bend the domain to fit the grammar: where the grammar does not fit, SHOW
the misfit and say so.

1. **Chemical equilibrium — ONE state, four representations**: the lab (a sealed flask of N₂O₄ ⇌ 2 NO₂,
   colour from the NO₂ fraction), molecules (a small particle view: counts bound to the state, labelled
   "drawn, not observed"), the equation (Q = [NO₂]²/[N₂O₄] vs K), the graph (concentrations over time).
   Acts: heat the flask; halve the volume; add N₂O₄. COMPUTE everything from the formula (K at 25 °C
   from the fixture, temperature dependence via van 't Hoff with ΔH from the fixture — label the
   simplification). All four representations re-render from ONE state on every act.
2. **Supply chain — Harrow Medical (fictional)**: company, single-source MCU supplier Kaito (fab fire at
   06:10), the Harrow plant, transport (a carrier), commitments (five customer promises over eight
   weeks), 2,300 MCUs on hand at 210/day ≈ 11 days (fixture; everything else AUTHORED, illustrative).
3. **The awkward domain — Yellowstone's northern range after wolves returned** (fixture `ecology`):
   nobody holds this system; there is essentially one act (the 1995–96 reintroduction, by agencies); the
   causal story downstream is contested science; the state is continuous and sparsely observed. Use only
   the fixture's facts and [verify] slots; show the scientific disagreement, never pick a winner.

## Model-specific requirements
**CrossJurisdiction** — the act as a form whose lines other systems hold; kinds of yes (RULE · WILL ·
TIME · MONEY · KNOWING); carriers (copy · move · join); the standing line ("you are in … your word
counts for … not for …"); registers per system; refusals naming the rule; three-record outcome with no
god view. Chemistry: a reaction request in a teaching lab (the chemistry itself is a RULE-yes computed
from K — "nature never signs; it answers"; the safety officer, instrument booking, stockroom). Supply
chain: a purchase order to a second source (supplier WILL, change-control RULE, carrier TIME, finance
MONEY, customers KNOWING). Ecology: the 1995 reintroduction as a recorded moment you may fork: lines for
the agencies and states (roles only, no invented positions; [verify]), and a NATURE row with no line
("nature does not say yes; it responds — and what it did next is contested").
**CrossCut** — the cut with four planes (books · desk · not yet · since), the seal and the hindsight
ledger, the Horizon Rail with Reality shuttered, the branch with coupling and the seam verdicts HELD ·
DIVERGED · IMPOSSIBLE · RE-DECLARE, three backs. Chemistry: a cut 3 s after halving the volume (books:
true concentrations — computed; desk: the instrument's lagged reading; not yet: the new equilibrium —
computable but withheld by the cut's rule; since: the settled value). Supply chain: the morning of the
fab fire (what procurement knew at 06:10 vs later). Ecology: a cut in January 1995 (books: [verify]
counts; desk: what managers could know then; not yet: thirty years, contested); branch "no
reintroduction" — and show honestly that Reality cannot grade a counterfactual with no control: most
seam verdicts become UNKNOWN, and say why.
