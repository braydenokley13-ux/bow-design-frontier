An executable world is a document you can stand inside.

BOW is trying to invent the medium that comes after the page, the clip, the cell and the chat message. Its atom is not a page. It is a moment of a system — its state, its rules, the people in it and what it remembers — with an address you can share, a door you can walk through, a branch you can cut, and an answer to how do we know? attached to every value in it.

TEXT describes a system atom: the sentence
IMAGE shows what it looks like atom: the frame
VIDEO shows what happened atom: the clip
SPREADSHEET manipulates its numbers atom: the cell
CHAT answers questions about it atom: the message
BOW lets you stand inside it, act, and branch it atom: the moment of a system

Pages have addresses. Systems need coordinates.

Every BOW object is a point along the same dimensions. Moving along one of them is navigation. Sending the coordinates is sharing. “Open it in BOW” means: resolve this address and put me there.

Truth is a texture, not a label.

Every world gets its own skin. The epistemic grammar underneath does not change: solid, double-ruled, outlined, toned, hatched, stippled, open. Words appear on request — how do we know? — not stamped on everything.

OBSERVEDMeasured or reported from outside BOW.Boston drafted Jayson Tatum at No. 3, 22 Jun 2017.
RECORDEDWritten into BOW’s own history. Append-only.You kept the pick at 11:52 in your branch.
AUTHOREDA rule or number someone chose on purpose.This seat may commit up to $250,000.
COMPUTEDFollows exactly from known rules and state.5 ayes, 5 noes: the motion fails.
MODELEDA bounded simulation’s answer. Shown as a range.Your branch, 2019: 41–55 wins.
GENERATEDMade to represent, not to assert.The actor model’s spoken phrasing.
UNKNOWNA marked hole. Never filled in silently.What Washington privately thought of the vote.

A modeled result never wears the texture of an observed one.

A fork never wears the ink of the record. It is stamped, dated and signed.

Six working laws, proposed

FOR FOUNDER APPROVAL · NOT CANON
  1. State once, show many. A change happens to the system once; every drawing of it is a view of that one state.
  2. History is append-only. Branches are declared, never disguised as the record.
  3. Every value can answer “how do we know?” One step away, never zero, never ten.
  4. A role bounds knowing, doing and answering for — for people and agents alike.
  5. Intelligence occupies seats; it never owns the world. Swap the model and the seat, its authority and its history stay.
  6. An outcome is not the quality of the decision. The world keeps what was knowable at the moment you chose.

Laws 3 and 6 already exist in working code: Economics Live stamps every fact with a basis (real / derived / modeled / fiction) and a date; Decision Challenges keeps a receipt of every act, refused ones included, with what was knowable at the time. The frontier question is whether they can become the grammar of a whole medium.
