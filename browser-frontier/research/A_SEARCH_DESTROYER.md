# A · Search Destroyer
RESEARCH · 29 Sep 2026 · design reading, not evidence of use

## 0. The claim

Search exists because web links carry no meaning: untyped, one-way, unverified. Only a corpus-wide guess can find anything. Executable systems can publish relations that are typed, executed, two-way and dated. Discovery then becomes following a relation from something true you already hold, and everyone always holds something: the link that sent them, the place they stand, the clock they are on, a seat, a kept branch, a number on the screen.

One gesture: press a true mark. It offers only the doors that exist, each showing what lies behind it before it opens: **Because** (cause), **Meanwhile** (shared clock), **Who** (seat), **Here** (place), **Who says yes** (consent), **What's different** (your branch against Reality). BOW infers nothing about you. Caring is an act (sign, keep, follow, assume, ask) and the runtime indexes acts. Discovery by declared holding, not inferred interest.

**Home does not survive as a page.** It becomes the **Doorstep** (first arrival), the **Round** (return: a finite walk that ends in "level with the record") and the **Standing** (an edge of every frame, not a place). Search shrinks to anchor lookup: a name, a place or a time, never a question. Nearly every step needs no model; models sit at the edges.

## 1. What the incumbents get wrong or leave out

**Clock Hall.** Holds: kinds of time are true; a follow should cost something; lapse is recorded, not hidden. Breaks:
- It inventories what you already found. A newcomer meets "Nothing hangs here yet" and a two-query search field, so its discovery mechanism is search.
- Its unit is the container. One Reality change touching three presences prints three "since" lines; the change is what the person cares about.
- At dozens of presences it is a dashboard of clocks: nothing ranks by what lapses first, nothing expires, nothing says "you are level".
- "Since you left" says time passed, not what your seat can now see or no longer do.

Right for a handful of Worlds you run; wrong as a front door. Its faces survive as the time-stamp on a line.

**Arrive.** Holds: landing in a state beats a list; the four edges. Breaks:
- It needs a question. "Or try one of these" is a hand-picked menu that at scale becomes trending, the banned feed.
- It resolves silently to one state. At billions, "Boston basketball" has thousands of candidates; the resolver becomes an invisible editor, and "How do we know?" covers facts, not why this state.
- Cost is per question. Its dearest branch, assembling a GENERATED mechanism when nothing answers, is where long-tail questions land (a FRONTIER MODEL per ask; SPECULATIVE FRONTIER).
- Pull-only: nothing reaches you about an unasked system that changed one you hold.

Both treat discovery as one act. It is a trail, and neither keeps the route: the address shares a destination, not the reason. Proposal: add `via` to the address ("… · view · via cause: your tax"). A chain of `via`s is a **wake**; a wake is what you share.

## 2. Concepts

Pass/kill lines are for hand-run paper tests: small n catches gross failure and estimates no rate. Billions: A, C, E, F carry the scale claim (reverse-dependency, calendar-interval, cell and assumption indexes); B and D are local by construction; G and H ride the same anchors.

**2.0 The substrate.** Every published system emits, at registration and each run: *reads* (address to address, with rule id), *clock bindings* (system time to a shared calendar), *seats* (position, entity, interval), *place attachments* (cell or container), *assumptions* (a branch's predicates over addresses). Consent is computed from rules, not stored. Records name **shared anchors** (calendars, institutions, places, seats, rule sets), so systems never link pairwise: a hop is leaf, hub, leaf.
Honesty rules: an edge is solid only if a BOW-hosted or attested runtime executed it (unreviewed edges are dashed and off by default: no SEO for causes); a hub shows its top k by a deterministic domain weight (materiality, imminence, live-ness, never popularity) plus a maintained count of the rest; every arrival states its `via`.
Precedent and warning: OpenAlex indexes 271.3M works on typed edges, yet 64% of records list no references (arXiv 2512.16434, Nov 2025). Following reaches only what declares, so declaring must be a condition of publishing. Anchor layers have proven sizes: Wikidata ≈123.5M items (wikidata.org, 29 Sep 2026); H3 (Uber, 2018) and S2 index the globe in hierarchical cells, so "what's near" is a cell lookup. A pull is one to three index lookups and no model; its cost scales with local degree, not corpus size.

**Test.** 8 people (4 newcomers, 2 fans, 2 non-sports), a printed Boston books page, no search; a facilitator plays a hand-made three-hop chain into a second institution. Pass: ≥6/8 reach it in four pulls and say "because…". Kill: ≤3/8, or most ask to type.

### B · The Table — touch an act and the room fills with everyone whose yes it needed, including those who have not said it

**Boston.**
1. A fan meets a live change: "Boston reported to send X to Y", a report (RECORDED) of an act not yet observed. Door: Who says yes 3.
2. The Table, computed from the rules: Boston's owner SIGNED; Y's owner PENDING; League Office review PENDING; one UNKNOWN seat where the rulebook cannot speak (back channel). Only pending seats are lit. Pending terms stay in the seat.
3. Press a pending seat: you are at that institution's desk while it waits, with the rule it will apply. Push to find the edge ("at $X over the second apron it cannot clear"). Follow the seat: a follow that ends when the matter does.
4. Newcomer from "13 Aug 2025 · the NBA approves a sale": the Table shows who had to agree, so they meet the institutions.
5. Flight: who had to agree to leave (crew duty clock, dispatch, ATC slot); pending is the ground-stop release.

**Canonical vs drawn.** Canonical: rule-derived consent set and signature/vote log. Drawn: the table.
**Segments.** DISCOVER → ARRIVE → ORIENT → ROLE (every seat can be taken) → KNOWLEDGE + AUTHORITY CHANGE.
**Scale.** The consent set is computed from the one rule set you touch: bounded, dozens of parties. Parties are shared institution anchors, so one Table hops to that institution's other Tables.
**Cross-domain.** Equilibrium: the constraints that must hold jointly (mass, charge, K); pending is the unsatisfied one. Supply chain: change order needs buyer QA, supplier, regulator.
**Label.** PROPOSED PLATFORM CAPABILITY; seed REAL in the unmerged World branch and CBA engine (League Office vote record; offer → signed → delivered papers; pending terms owner-scoped). NO AI REQUIRED; SMALL / CHEAP MODEL to propose seats from third-party rule text, stamped GENERATED.
**Fails if.** Formal consent is not real power, so it teaches a too-clean world; it reads as paperwork; it covers acts, not states.
**Test.** 10 fans and newcomers, a printed "reported trade" headline, "what happens next?", then a hand-made Table. Pass: ≥7/10 name the pending party and what blocks it; ≥5/10 ask to go to that seat. Kill: "so did it happen?"

**Canonical vs drawn.** Canonical: clock bindings and schedules. Drawn: a slice.

### D · The Seat Line — follow the seat, not the person; a seat has authority, knowledge and a decision record, and it remembers

1. On a Moment a fan presses a name: Brad Stevens (public facts, sourced, no likeness). Door: Who 2.

**Canonical vs drawn.** Canonical: occupancy records (entity, seat, interval, source) and signed acts. Drawn: the line.
**Segments.** DISCOVER → ROLE / RESPONSIBILITY → ENTER.
**Scale.** Occupancy index keyed by entity id; person → seats is O(tens), seat → decisions a range scan, predecessor a local join. **Fails if.** Fans want the person; "seat" is abstract at 10–12; occupancy data is the cost.
**Test.** 10 fans, one real name: "the last decision that mattered, and something like it before." Pass: ≥6/10 reach the predecessor's decision unprompted. Kill: they ignore the door.

### E · Underfoot — a place is a lens, anchored by where you stand or name, never by what you type

**Boston.**
1. Open BOW at North Station on a game night, or name the place. No ask. A containment ladder runs down the edge: object · room · building · block · city.
2. What runs here shows only what passes the Place rule (happened, promised, owed or decidable): the Celtics game-night operation, the arena bowl (PLAYED / ESTIMATE / NOT KNOWN nights).
3. Dashed doors are wants others left here: "Will the Green Line run after the game?" No system exists. TD Garden sits on North Station (tdgarden.com). Unmet demand is legible where it happens.
4. Lean into the arena: the ladder goes down (seat 12) and up (Boston). A classroom running BOW Economics in this room is the same lens; at ORD, gate turn, ground stop, crew clock. On a headset the ladder becomes distance.

**Canonical vs drawn.** Canonical: place registry (cell and container), attachments, live-ness. Drawn: the lens. Location is chosen, coarse, never logged.
**Segments.** DISCOVER → ARRIVE → ORIENT; zoom is the North Star gesture.
**Scale.** H3/S2-style hierarchical cell index plus an institutional containment tree for indoors. "What runs here" is the cell and its ancestors, filtered by live-ness, O(k) live systems. Geography is the graph; no social graph needed.
**Cross-domain.** Equilibrium: inside the flask, equilibrium and heater loop. Supply chain: inside the port, the berth schedule.
**Label.** PROPOSED PLATFORM CAPABILITY (Place rule and arena three-night states REAL, unmerged spatial branch; no registry). NO AI REQUIRED.
**Fails if.** Most valuable systems have no place (a cap rule); dense cells; privacy; the founder judged the first 3D trial poor, so it must work as a plain ladder first.
**Test.** 12 people in a real place, "find something running here you care about": no ask vs search box. Pass: entry at parity or better and ≥8/12 say why it is here. Kill: they ask to search and quit.

### F · The Fracture — a kept branch is a standing question Reality answers at its own pace; you arrive where it left you

**Boston.**
1. Two weeks ago you kept a branch cut at the Brown-to-Philadelphia Moment [date verify]: "Brown stays". The runtime indexed its assumptions (Brown's salary stays; the return never arrives).
2. Today Reality makes a move that needs that return on Boston's books (staged; live needs a feed). The assumption index fires for exactly the branches that assumed otherwise.
3. You open at the fracture: Reality's record left, ruled; your branch right, in its own ink: "Impossible here: the return never arrived, so this act cannot happen." Reality's numbers show; your branch's number is SEALED.
4. Follow it into the other club's side, a system you never asked about; or re-declare (the Council), hold, let go. Peers share the same fracture record: "N other branches broke here", never ranked by popularity.
5. Buildable now as replay: "Holiday kept" (cut with hindsight, stamped) breaks at 5 Feb 2026; Simons never arrived, so his trade cannot happen.

**Canonical vs drawn.** Canonical: assumptions as typed predicates over addresses and reconciliation events (held / diverged / impossible / re-declare). Drawn: the arrival.
**Segments.** FORK → CONTINUE ALTERNATE HISTORY → COMPARE → RETURN TO RECORDED; discovery through your own assumptions.
**Scale.** Assumption index (address → dependents) with event-driven reconciliation: work per update ∝ dependents, not branches (spreadsheet-recompute shape). First divergence by bisect over the act log; peers by record hash; one fracture per cause, so twelve branches broken by one trade is one arrival.
**Cross-domain.** Equilibrium: a run assumes T = 350 K; the first measurement outside the model band is the fracture. Supply chain: "supplier B ships week 12" → fab fire → impossible.
**Label.** PROPOSED PLATFORM CAPABILITY (Living fork: board only; SHA-256 fork record: Wave 3 prototype). Live half needs a Reality feed; replay is buildable. NO AI REQUIRED.
**Fails if.** Newcomers have no branch; "impossible" feels punishing; volume does not collapse.
**Test.** 8 fans each cut a paper branch at a recent Boston Moment with a one-line assumption; 3 days later each gets a fracture sheet from a real or staged act. Pass: ≥5/8 ask to enter the system that broke it, ≤2/8 feel told they were wrong. Kill: reverse.

### G · The Want — an unanswerable ask becomes an addressable empty room on the anchor it names; it fills when a system appears

**Boston.**
1. At TD Garden: "Will the Green Line run after the game?" BOW has no system for the MBTA. No generated answer. It gives the nearest true anchor (the arena's state tonight), says "no system for this", and shows the shape of the missing thing ("needs: line status, post-game timetable, who can hold a train").
2. Leave a want: filed under North Station, not in a global chart. Identical wants merge (typed slots: anchor, relation, window; a small model only for near-duplicates). When a system appears it fills the room, stamped with who built it and whether REVIEWED, UNREVIEWED or GENERATED; the asker is addressed.

**Canonical vs drawn.** Canonical: want = (anchor, relation, window) plus askers. Drawn: a dashed empty room.
**Scale.** Wants ride the anchors; builders see demand where it is, never a popularity chart.
**Cross-domain.** Any domain; it is the failure state.
**Label.** PROPOSED PLATFORM CAPABILITY. SMALL / CHEAP MODEL (canonicalise, dedupe); FRONTIER MODEL OCCASIONAL only when a reviewer chooses to compile a system for a high-demand anchor (SPECULATIVE FRONTIER; never per ask).
**Fails if.** Most wants stay empty, a promise BOW cannot keep; spam; it turns into a feature-request board.
**Test.** 10 people ask something the fixtures cannot answer. Arm A: a plausible generated paragraph. Arm B: the honest Want. Measure trust ("would you rely on it?"), willingness to leave a want, next action. Kill: B < A on trust, or abandonment at "no system".

### H · The Round — the verdict on home

**Home is not a page.** A page is where you go to look. The newcomer has nothing to look at; the fan cares about a change with causes, not a container; the operator is owed an interruption; a flight follow should end itself. Three things replace it:
- **Doorstep** (no history). What sent you (link, notification, an address with its wake), else the place you chose, else the nearest shared clock, with the reason on its face. Never "Nothing hangs here yet."
- **Round** (return). The Browser opens inside the first stop and states its length: "4 stops · 1 closes tonight". Deterministic order: (1) open matters that lapse first, (2) fractures, (3) addressed to you (sent, wants filled), (4) live within a day, (5) drift, one unopened line per institution. Each stop places you in the system with a **seat diff**: what your seat can see now that it could not, what it can no longer do, what is due. Act, pass (recorded as absence) or leave. Two weeks away groups by cause: one trade that moved three things is one stop with three doors. The last stop says "Level with the record" and the frame goes quiet.
- **Standing** (an edge). The finite ledger of relations you hold (seat, kept branch, follow, assumption, want, last wake), each with an end condition and Clock Hall's time-stamp. You follow relations, not things: "this seat", "the pending yes". No counts, no tiles.

**Boston.** Operator: Week 10 Monday, two acts lapsed as record, seat diff. Fan: a fracture, the live preseason, a friend's trade file inviting them into Golden State's seat. Newcomer: Doorstep only.

| Mandatory state | What the person meets | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Cold start | Doorstep: sender, else place, else nearest clock | C, E, H |
| Live change now | Reported trade opens as a Table | B, C |
| Responsibility waiting | Round stop 1, with seat diff | H |
| Branch diverged | Arrival at the fracture | F |
| Shared with them | Invitation to the counterparty's seat, with the wake | D, H |
| Unanswerable | Nearest true anchor, "no system", a Want | G |
| Two weeks away | Round grouped by cause, ends "level" | H |

**Canonical vs drawn.** Canonical: private Standing store (relations, end conditions, last-seen ledger, wakes). Drawn: stops.
**Segments.** RETURN TO RECORDED, FOLLOW; ROLE, KNOWLEDGE + AUTHORITY CHANGE.
**Scale.** Subscriptions are index entries (anchor, relation, predicate), so change pushes to holders instead of crawling. A Round is bounded by what you hold and ends because holds expire.
**Cross-domain.** Flight follow "until landed"; equilibrium run counter; supply chain purchase order.
**Label.** PROPOSED PLATFORM CAPABILITY (persistence and lapsed acts REAL in the classroom product; seat engine prototype). NO AI REQUIRED; SMALL / CHEAP MODEL only for phrasing.
**Fails if.** The route feels paternalistic; lapse-guilt (open founder question); the Standing becomes the home page.
**Test.** 8 returning operators or fans after two weeks (or simulated), Round vs a Clock Hall wall. Measure time to first act on what lapsed first, whether they feel finished, whether they ask for "everything". Kill: they skip to the Standing every time, or ≥3/8 feel scolded.

## 3. Two to prototype

### Prototype 1 — Because, landing from a Doorstep
Mandatory states:
1. **Doorstep from a link (cold start).** A friend's Moment link, "5 Feb 2026 · the deadline". No ask line, no wall; the address ends "via link from Maya" (fictional).
2. **Marks at rest.** Pressable marks carry a hairline; pressing "≈$17M projected tax" shows Because n · Meanwhile n · Who n before opening. Only real doors.
3. **Backward pull.** Strata in their inks with shares; ink changes at the authored-rule boundary; each stratum offers "cut here".
4. **Boundary crossing.** The thread exits into Chicago's side of the Simons trade; the address gains "via cause"; Back walks the thread.
5. **World/Reality seam.** The thread hits the fork point and changes ink from RECORDED to OBSERVED.
6. **Forward pull.** Top effects by materiality, "+3 thinner", a SEALED tail.
7. **Hole.** An unreviewed mark: the thread goes dashed (UNKNOWN) and offers Leave a want. Nothing generated silently.
8. **Non-sports run.** The rotation fixture, same gesture, same grammar.
9. **Share the wake.** Send the route with its reasons; the receiver opens the same stops in their own seat.

**The impossible moment.** A number's cause is a running place you can stand in, and the thread changes ink where your World stops being Reality. A website gives a number a footnote; a game authors every number.
**Anti-pattern.** A node-link graph or a "related" list; an LLM paragraph explaining "why" in place of executed causes (chat in disguise); many shallow threads instead of one chain that truly crosses a boundary; a 3D flythrough.

### Prototype 2 — The Round
Mandatory states:
1. **Doorstep, no history.** Sender's address or the nearest clock with its reason; no empty wall.
2. **Round opens after 14 days.** The first frame is a place, not a list; the edge says "4 stops · 1 closes tonight".
3. **Stop 1, responsibility.** Boston · Year Two, Week 10 Monday. Seat diff: lost 112–115, two acts lapsed (recorded as no decision), what you can no longer do. Act / pass / leave.
4. **Stop 2, fracture.** A branch kept 14 days ago meets a staged Reality act it cannot contain: two columns, SEALED branch number, four options.
5. **Stop 3, addressed to you.** A friend's trade file as an invitation to Golden State's seat, with its wake; a filled Want beside it, stamped by status.
6. **Stop 4, live.** Preseason game (fixture-driven): open matters, enterable, seals at tip-off; a reported trade opens as a Table.
7. **Pass recorded.** "No decision" in absence ink; no verdict wording.
8. **Cause grouping.** One Reality change touching three of your things shows once, with three doors.
9. **Level with the record.** The frame goes quiet; only marks are pressable; the Standing edge shows relations and end conditions.

**The impossible moment.** A Browser that says "Level with the record" and stops. A site never finishes with you; a game shows a menu.
**Anti-pattern.** An inbox of cards or a four-tile dashboard (owed, moved, sent, live); engagement ranking; streaks; a "recommended for you" tail after the last stop; one cause printed as three "since" lines.

## 4. What real runtime would have to exist

Exists today: dated fact store, CBA engine, snapshot persistence, the League World's papers and vote record, the arena's three night states, one authored "What did this trade touch?" chain. Everything below is PROPOSED.
1. **Registry and addresses** for systems, moments, branches, seats, plus `via`.
2. **The Reference Ledger** (2.0): runtimes emit reads, clock bindings, seats, place attachments, assumptions; the registry inverts them into five indexes (cause, clock, seat, place, assumption). Consent is computed from rules.
3. **Shared anchors** with stewards: calendars, institutions, places, seats, rule sets, plus an alias index for name → anchor.
4. **Trust tiers.** Solid edges only from BOW-hosted or attested runtimes that executed them; third-party edges dashed, off by default. Publishing requires declaring.
5. **Deterministic rank functions** per axis (materiality, imminence, live-ness), versioned and inspectable, with maintained counts for hub tails.
6. **A Reality feed** with rights: dated, sourced, drives the assumption index. Only fixtures exist.
7. **Causal and seat engines** in the runtime. Wave 3 prototypes only.
8. **A private Standing store** with end conditions, wakes and retention of absence.
9. **A wants registry** with typed-slot canonicalisation.

Cost shape: pulls are index reads; the dear parts are executing systems to verify edges and storing provenance. Rights: public names and facts only, no likeness.

## 5. What would falsify this thesis

- Newcomers given only a blank ask line reach a first meaningful entry as fast, and say why they are there as clearly, as those given the Doorstep or a mark to pull. Then search stays primary.
- People use one door (probably Because) and ignore the rest. Then six axes are over-built; keep two.
- Public systems will not declare relations, so edges are as sparse as scholarly references (64% empty in OpenAlex). Then the substrate degrades to untyped links plus search.
- Engineering falsifier: build the indexes over synthetic systems with power-law attachment to a small anchor set. If the biggest hub's top-k is unstable or unexplainable, or a pull is slow on one machine, the scale claim fails. Set both lines before running.
- Returning users skip the Round to open the Standing, or the finite walk feels scolding or paternalistic. Then home is a ledger.
- Most discovery wants have no anchor at all ("show me something good"). Then following serves only the already-anchored, and curation returns.
