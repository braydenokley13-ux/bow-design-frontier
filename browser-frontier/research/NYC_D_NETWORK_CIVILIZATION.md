## 0. Verdict

1. **The grammar holds for a city on one condition: the city is a directory and a post office, not a state.** No city record, clock or hash. Jurisdictions (each with its own record, ground, seats, clock), typed relations between them (located-in and near are two of about a dozen), and Forms that travel between them. A city-level number is computed by the requester, wears its weakest input's kind, and names who declined to disclose.
2. Jurisdiction's act grammar survives NYC with **eight amendments** (§2.3). The load-bearing one: real institutions are **observed authorities**; they issue facts, never yeses. That is the mechanical guard against a god simulation.
3. Home spans domains as a **Person record of pointers and last-seen cuts**, never others' state. Clocks never merge (§3).
4. Foundry's queued third target, a bounded partner agreement, is a Form: make it the first network primitive (§4).
5. Exchange carries declarations, never authority (§5).
6. **Cluster verdict.** Clusters grow *reach* fastest; vertical kits grow *coverage* cheapest; flagships grow *belief*; generated systems grow nothing countable until reviewed. Build a cluster with a keel: one deep anchor, thin neighbours on a kit, only true edges (§7).
7. **Order of proof: Boston → New York → Harbor**, amended: New York starts as one night in one district, gated on Foundry's tournament; Harbor's clock runs from now but is judged last. If Foundry or the second-operator test fails, swap New York and Harbor (§10).

## 1. What a city network is

### 1.1 Directory, not state · PROPOSED · NO AI
- A **registry** of system ids (BOW prefix), kept apart from real namespaces (`nyc:bin:1082908` never equals `bow:sys:…`), each with trust frame, declared ports and edges.
- **Typed relations**, each edge countersigned by both ends or dashed: located-in, near, contains, part-of, operates, employs, owns, governed-by, pays, supplies, serves, instance-of. *Near* is COMPUTED from footprints; *located-in* is a site claim.
- **A parcel is not an institution.** The arena's lot is a rail corporation's in PLUTO (lane A §2.2). A BOW system claims a BIN/BBL only as a *site* (tenancy, annex, proposal beside), passing lane A's overlap, vacancy, institution and time tests, and reads AUTHORED.

### 1.2 Four grades of authority

| Grade | Example | Can say | Cannot | Its fields |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G1 Observed authority** | subway alert feed; DOT speeds | facts, inbound only (KNOWING) | any yes; be asked anything | OBSERVED with lag stamp; else RECORDED with as-of; internals UNKNOWN |
| **G2 Stand-in** ("modeled ABOUT, not BY") | a BOW "Transit desk" following published service standards | computed RULE-yes; scripted or modeled WILL-yes, stamped "not the operator's answer" | claim the operator agreed; wear its marks | AUTHORED plus cited RECORDED rules; MODELED only if a named model ran |
| **G3 BOW-native** | fictional club HQ; Harbor Lights; the BOW League Office | any yes inside its record | any yes about G1 | AUTHORED |
| **G4 Operator-run** | a teacher's, creator's or later an organisation's own jurisdiction | any yes inside its record, by its human seats | anything outside its declared ports | AUTHORED by them; standing in the frame |

G1 data REAL; the BOW mirror PROPOSED (MTA terms: BOW-side, unmodified, lag stated over a minute, revocable). G3 REAL in NBA World and Harbor. G2, G4 PROPOSED; G4 for real organisations SPECULATIVE.

**Rule: BOW holds authority over records, never over reality.** One stand-in per real institution, registry-owned and versioned; third parties bind to it, or the cluster ships three subway operators, as the nine boards shipped three cash figures (Critic 2).

### 1.3 Correspondence, not physics · PROPOSED · NO AI
Effects cross a boundary only as declared **notices** (KNOWING) or **Forms**; the receiver's own rules decide the effect. A physical coupling (platform crowd → arena gate) is a Declare in someone's ink, a MODELED edge with a named model that ran, or UNKNOWN. Notice chains are receiver-subscribed, rate- and depth-limited, each hop a visible record entry. No dynamics exist that are not a line someone can read: the structural refusal of the god simulation.

## 2. Cross-authority transactions

### 2.1 The Form in city terms
A Form is drafted in the initiator's record; each line names a **holder, seat, kind of yes, carrier, disclosure back, clock and expiry**, addressed to a declared port.

- **RULE**: deterministic, held by a rulebook, no seat. **WILL**: a seat; silence is NO ACT, never yes. **TIME**: a slot on the holder's calendar (countersignature join). **MONEY**: reserve, then remittance move; AUTHORED currency only, conserved across both records. **KNOWING**: notice copy; the holder learns and cannot refuse.
- Completion is *derived*: every required line held, evidenced by the holders' own hashes. Nobody holds the Form's whole state. NO AI.
- Seeds. REAL (unmerged branch; no two-human session observed): NBA World's two-sided league tables (offer → signed → delivered), owner-scoped pending terms, public signed notices, League votes as count and result, club-to-club payment on signing (WORLDS/NETWORK). Forms as such are PROPOSED.

### 2.2 Worked Form: "Move tonight's tip-off 30 minutes"
An AUTHORED decision reacting to an OBSERVED disruption: a service alert on a Penn-area line (G1). Club Ops (G3, an authored HQ) wants to hold the start.

| Line | Holder | Kind · carrier | Disclosed back | Clock |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Guard | subway alert feed (G1) | **READ**, not a yes | content, OBSERVED, lag stamped | live, seconds |
| Ask | Club Ops Director seat | WILL · join | — | World hours |
| Window | BOW League Office | RULE (start-window), then WILL (commissioner seat) | count and result | League hours |
| Building | Arena desk (G3) | TIME · join | shape only: free / busy | its calendar |
| Overtime crew | Club → Arena | MONEY · remittance | content | — |
| Broadcast | media partner | KNOWING · notice copy | nothing | learns after the stamp |

Outcomes: the League **returns the file naming the rule** (Jurisdiction board: "What you can change: what you ask (RE-FORM), never their answer"); a silent League **lapses** as NO ACT and the schedule stands; a stale Arena slot forces a **re-form**. The subway operator is never asked; it is not a party.

### 2.3 Eight amendments the city forces · PROPOSED · NO AI
1. **READ lines.** A G1 holder gives a read, not a yes. Forms guard on reads; they cannot obtain them. (Jurisdiction had no observed party.)
2. **One form clock.** A Form is dated in Reality time or a World tick at drafting. A World joins a Reality-dated Form only through a **pinned** coupling (Cut's pinned / paced / free): it replays and takes Declares; it cannot act on Reality. Unpinned mixed clocks are refused.
3. **One person, one side.** No person signs two lines of one Form. REAL seed: "No owner can sign on behalf of another club" (WORLDS/NETWORK).
4. **Ports before lines.** A line addresses a declared, versioned port. No "ask anything of anyone".
5. **Stand-ins are singletons** (§1.2).
6. **Scarce goods reserve then move; knowledge travels as receipts.** REAL: a paid Scouting department yields a receipt unlocking a Harbor film session; no NBA money, players or arena rights cross (WORLDS/INSTITUTION_RELATIONSHIP_ADR). That ADR wants the next crossing to be "another institution's contested/limited action"; an arena slot is that.
7. **Aggregates belong to the requester:** computed from disclosures, weakest kind, non-disclosers listed.
8. **Standing per door.** Every door restates "you are in … your word counts for … not for …". A seat in one jurisdiction confers nothing in the next.

**Cost.** Mechanics are NO AI. A stand-in's WILL-yes played by a model is SMALL and must draw per fork from a seeded distribution, or the first player to find its boundary beats every copy (Critic 2). An AI in a seat is FRONTIER CONT. if BOW hosts it, with act-rate budgets as well as scope.

**Debugging test.** If a stuck Form can only be diagnosed by reading every party's books, the disclosure schedule is wrong. Each party sees the Form's shape, status and returning rule id.

## 4. FOUNDRY: adding an institution to the city network

**REAL** (WORLDS/WORLD_FOUNDRY): a JSON-only declaration schema, compiler, shared resource ledger, two declared systems (event staffing; arena/facility) and six probes. Declarations state roles and authority, information, actions and guards, resource changes, commitments, time, outcomes, Moments/Lab questions, visual anchors, a model card. The compiler rejects executable values, invalid references, conflicting resources and wrong probes. **Not earned:** no tournament against hand code, no editor, no proof a second author is faster, no critic attack, no migration test. The queued third target, "a bounded partner agreement with typed terms, authority, a deadline, refusal/counter actions, rights consumed, cash, and a Moment", is a two-party Form.

### 4.1 The dossier · PROPOSED
1. **Identity.** Registry id; trust kind (Official, Verified, Unverified, Local, Temporary; B §2.7). A real institution's name is place-name-only, no marks, stamped AUTHORED or MODELED-ABOUT.
2. **Site claim** (BIN/BBL; tenancy, annex, proposal beside). The checker returns OK-AS-TENANCY, OK-AS-PROPOSAL-BESIDE or REFUSED-OVERLAP, and prints "AUTHORED; does not exist physically". NO AI.
3. **Seats and authority.** What each seat's yes counts for; human-only seats; permitted warrant classes. The standing line is *generated from this*.
4. **State and rules.** Ledger, actions, guards, deterministic reducer, clock kind. Declaration, not code.
5. **Ports.** Lines it can hold (kind, carrier, disclosure, expiry) and notices it emits; lines it *needs*, as dashed edges that turn solid only on the counterpart's countersignature. A new institution cannot declare an existing one its sponsor.
6. **Truth manifest.** Every field has kind, source and as-of or fails compile; the compiler propagates kinds (weakest input wins).
7. **Model card, probes, composition probes:** two declared systems and a Form that must be refused by rule R at input X, held, or lapse.
8. **Representation manifest.** L0 (Direct) and L1 (diagram, map, timeline) automatic; higher levels are Studio's.
9. **Rights ledger.** Source, licence, as-of per fact; PLUTO / ACRIS personal names dropped at ingest (lane A §2.4).
10. **Digest and lineage.** Editing a verified declaration drops changed parts to unchecked; upgrade is a new identity with a lineage link; forks run on pinned declarations.

**Review ladder.** Compile → probes → composition checks (closure, cross-port conservation, authority non-escalation, disclosure leakage, no circular WILL dependence, dominance sweep) → human review for real names and rights → independent attack → promote (Draft → Local → Unverified → Verified → Official).

**Cost.** Compile, probes, site tests, composition checks: NO AI. Wizard slot-fill: SMALL. Drafting from a description: FRONTIER OCC., once per system, output promoted (F §2.12 rule 7). **Human review is the scarce resource**, not tokens. Sourcing facts per system over time is the real city-scale cost (Critic 2, amendment A): an honest cluster has mostly AUTHORED or UNKNOWN rooms; only existence and place are RECORDED.

### 4.2 What Foundry needs first, in order
(1) Port and line declarations in the schema. (2) Truth kind per field, weakest-input propagation. (3) Registry ids, namespaces, stand-in ownership. (4) The site-claim checker. (5) Composition probes. (6) The tournament on the partner-agreement target: hand code vs agent-drafted declaration vs hybrid, independent authors.

## 7. Clusters versus flagships, generated systems, catalogs

A **useful door** is where a question can land and a person can inspect, ask WHY, or operate, with honest kinds. Discovery, not construction, binds at scale (Critic 2: Institution FAILS at discovery; Bench's are islands).

| | Isolated flagship | Generic generated | Vertical catalog | **Connected cluster** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cost per door | high; #2 costs about #1 | tokens low (FRONTIER OCC. per compile), **review high** | falls with the kit | declaration plus edges; **cluster tax**: stand-ins, port versions, composition probes per edge |
| Grows with n | belief, not reach | count, not truth | coverage; edges only inside the vertical | **edges**: each member adds another party that can say yes |
| Discovery | search or promotion | Want-gated | inside the vertical | typed doors (Who says yes · Meanwhile · Here) cross members |
| Truth risk | low if sourced | GENERATED until reviewed; synthetic crowd | low | authored rooms read as "a city"; fact cost per system × time |
| Fails when | it is the only door | the question long tail is unique | domains do not connect | members are one author's puppets |

**Verdict.** Clusters grow *reach* fastest: a new door arrives already reachable from neighbours and from geography (*Here*), where an isolated door arrives with only search. That holds only while (a) edges are true and countersigned, (b) at least two independent operators hold members, (c) neighbours are thin declarations on a kit (L0/L1 first), (d) one deep anchor gives edges something to be about. Vertical kits are the cheapest coverage. Flagships earn belief. Generated systems compile only when merged Wants cross a threshold (Critic 2). Geography adds *Here* and site claims; it is expensive decoration where a list of relations would do.

**Falsifier.** A typed question lands in one member and the second is reachable only by new search.

## 10. The first credible path

**Precondition (decided, CANON §6): the OneRecord proof:** one record, two clients, address in the URL, SHA-256 over canonical acts, Concordance's agreement check, one Guest claim card. No city before it.

1. **Boston.** It faces the two costs that grow fastest in New York, the sourced fact pipeline and real-people rights: if one club cannot be sourced, twelve systems cannot. Gate: people outside the builders enter a recorded moment and make a checked act.
2. **Foundry's third-system tournament**, in the Worlds repo, in parallel, before New York is compiled. Without it New York is hand-built: the linear cost Critic 2 marked WEAK.
3. **New York, thin: one night, one district, five or six systems.** Arena desk; a club HQ (AUTHORED, site claimed beside the real building, overlap test visible); the BOW League Office (REAL seed); a Transit stand-in with an alert mirror (a dated recorded fixture if feed terms fail); one non-sports member (hospital or museum) to test generality; media as KNOWING only. **Gate:** two independent operators hold at least two members; a Form completes, is refused and lapses across their records; children of 10–12 tell Reality from Authored by frame (B §2.7 card sort).
4. **Harbor: judged last, run first.** It exists, so its clock is running. Judge it after seasons with returning owners, on the divergence test (§9). It is also where a stranger first uses Foundry to create an institution and the Exchange first meets a creator.

**Other orders.**
- *Harbor first* proves Foundry safely but never faces Reality; the hard claim (truth) is deferred.
- *Boston → Harbor → New York* is the strongest alternative: New York would compose proven parts. Not the default: Harbor's proof is elapsed time, so it cannot finish first, and multi-operator authority is seeded in server-owned NBA World seats (no live two-human session yet), not Harbor's browser-local saves (WORLDS/NETWORK).
- **Swap New York and Harbor if** Foundry's tournament shows no faster second author, or no second operator can be recruited. Then New York is one author playing many parts, the god simulation this test exists to prevent.

## 11. Traps and falsifiers

- **One hand, many puppets.** Counterparts guaranteed to say yes make a decision-card menu with sports nouns.
- **Realism laundering.** A stand-in wearing the real name is read as the institution. Falsified if under nine of twelve testers, children included, can tell observed feed, stand-in and BOW-native system apart by frame (threshold borrowed from B §2.7).
- **The god view returns** through debugging or aggregation.
- **Fact cost.** If the sixth member costs as much as the first, the cluster has no economies.
- **Terms drift.** MTA may change terms any time; Citi Bike is revocable (lane A §1d). Every feed needs a dated, hashed fixture behind it.
