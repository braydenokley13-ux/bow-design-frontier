# P2 · THE CUT — time-first Browser (Boston deep: NOW → last year's deadline → branch)

File: `prototypes/Cut.html` (+ `Cut.steps.json`). Read first: `briefs/00_PRIOR_ART.md`,
`briefs/PROTOTYPE_CONTRACT.md`, `prototypes/_shared/boston-fixture.js` (especially `cuts.deadline2026`
— note `firstReport` and `beforeFirstReport`), and research `research/D_TIME_NATIVE.md` (your main
source: §0–1.3, C1–C5 and §2.7 the Boston flow B0–B9). Also B §2.0 (snapshot vs follow).

## Exact design problem
The founder's time frontier, end to end: OPEN BOSTON · NOW → MOVE TO the trade deadline last year →
Browser shows what state existed, what was known, what had not happened → WHAT IF? creates a
branch from that cut → Reality stays perceptually anchored the whole time → Reality arrives and
grades the branch → return to NOW. Prove that standing at a CUT is not version history: a cut has a
KNOWER, a SEAL, a Reality that never merges, and consequences instead of edits.

## User
A fan who remembers the deadline; a 10–12-year-old who does not know basketball (the teacher holds
the unseal key); a historian (frozen 1985–86).

## System
Boston Celtics · Reality (live, `reality`), its recorded moments, a public/historical system
(`historical`), and your branch. Use ONLY fixture facts; a fact you need that is not there is a
visible `[verify: …]` slot (e.g., rumored talks, Boston's record that morning).

## Mandatory states
1. **NOW + the Horizon Rail.** A persistent edge (not a page): "Boston Celtics · Reality · live ·
   recorded through 29 Sep 2026 [verify time]" with a ticking clock in Reality's ink — the only
   moving thing; the gap between now and "recorded through" drawn. Ahead: dated doors (next
   preseason game `[verify]`, the 2026–27 deadline `[verify date]`) with solid outlines and dashed,
   EMPTY outcomes (never hatched — no model ran). Opening one: "You cannot enter a scheduled day.
   Acts close here." Behind you: last year's door.
2. **Arrive at last year's door.** Named by its question: "Deadline week, Feb 2026 · What does
   Boston do about a ≈$39.5M tax bill?" Inside, the door offers cuts named by what was still open:
   "2 Feb · before the first report" and "5 Feb · deadline day (the move was already reported)".
   Choosing 5 Feb shows why it already knows (fixture `firstReport`); the default is 2 Feb.
3. **The cut (2 Feb 2026).** A still room of four planes seen together: ON THE BOOKS (Tatum out —
   right Achilles, 12 May 2025, no return yet; Simons on Boston's roster; projected tax ≈$39.5M;
   Boston's record `[verify]`; the 2025–26 lines, verify), ON THE DESK (the rulebook in force; the
   legal action space; dated sources as slots `[verify]`; "the 2026–27 lines do not exist yet — not
   shown"), NOT YET (dashed: the deadline's closing hour; games left; the playoffs as scheduled with a
   sealed outcome; the line "This is not a list of what follows"), SINCE (closed). The rail keeps
   Reality's window SHUTTERED at the edge: its clock ticks, its content does not come through
   ("Reality · about 8 months ahead").
4. **The seal test.** Press "Later" (or drag the dashed edge): refused, naming the rule — "Nobody at
   this desk could know what comes next." Offer "Ask as an observer": one way, written to YOUR
   ledger, not the system's. (In the steps file, do NOT ask yet.)
5. **WHAT IF — the branch is born.** "Take the seat at this cut" (Boston's decision-maker). Choose
   from the legal action space, never from a list of what happened (e.g., keep the roster and pay;
   shed a bench salary with a second-round pick; look for a cheaper big in exchange for Simons —
   mark all AUTHORED, illustrative). An act that touches another club asks for that club's seat to
   be set (Recorded / Declare / Model / Unknown); Keep is blocked while unset. On Keep: the cut
   becomes the left frame in record ink; a new frame opens in its own ink: "Your branch · diverged 2
   Feb 2026, before the first report · by you · seat Boston · hindsight: none · coupling: PINNED".
6. **Two clocks.** Left: Reality's record at the branch's date (shuttered beyond it). Right: your
   branch. Edge: Reality NOW, shuttered. Advance your branch one week: its numbers recompute in
   branch ink (COMPUTED from your act under the 2025–26 rules — simplified, labelled "first-time
   payer rates, illustrative" if you compute tax); the left frame moves only its date.
7. **Reality arrives (the seam).** Advance past 5 Feb and 6 Mar: seam marks appear, each with both
   sources side by side — e.g., "Reality: Simons traded to Chicago for Vučević (5 Feb). Your branch
   assumed Boston keeps Simons → PULLED (diverged)." "Reality: Tatum returns vs Dallas (6 Mar). Your
   branch assumed his timeline → STITCHED (held)." No score, no total, no verdict wording.
8. **Hindsight, honestly.** Now "Ask as an observer": SINCE opens — later facts, corrections and
   arguments, each dated, ruled in a different hand (fixture: first report 3 Feb; ≈$17.7M and
   ≈$0.84M-under reports; Tatum 6 Mar; 56–26; first-round loss to Philadelphia 3–4 after leading
   3–1; Brown to Philadelphia reported 1 Jul — all verify). A ledger mark "seen through 29 Sep 2026"
   appears; a second fork made now is stamped "hindsight: through 29 Sep 2026".
9. **Return to NOW.** Tap the shuttered window: the address rewrites to Reality · NOW · observer;
   "since you left" lists what Reality did; your branch stays on the wall (pinned: still walking).
10. **Three backs.** A sheet: TRAIL (pencil, yours, private — Back names its destination), RECORD
    (print — Earlier / Later, Later stops at the horizon), ACTS (signature — "Kept: your branch";
    Re-take forks from before the act and retires the old branch; Amend adds a compensating act; no
    Undo on Reality).
11. **Baseline for the tournament.** A toggle "Same trip with a scrubber": a plain horizontal
    scrubber over the same record with no seal and no knower; a leak counter lights ("At this
    position the screen shows 4 facts nobody at the desk could know").
12. **No NOW.** Open "1985–86 Celtics": rail "No now. Frozen." Nothing ticks but Reality's
    shuttered window ("Reality, elsewhere").

## Art direction — "Press archive at dawn"
Newsprint paper, carbon ink, rule lines, one register red for the shutter and seal stamps, your
branch in a distinct ink (e.g., deep blue-green) with its own paper edge. Not sepia — the past is
the same institution on another morning, lights on, clocks stopped. Fonts: e.g. "Libre Caslon Text"
+ "IBM Plex Mono".

## Capability labels
REAL CURRENT: dated `asOf` fact rows and the CBA engine (BOW Economics Live); an act-log fold with
SHA-256 (Live World page, prototype-grade). PROPOSED: the cut with four planes, the floor rule, the
seal and hindsight ledger, the rail with Reality's shuttered window, branch coupling, seam
verdicts, three backs. A live Reality feed does NOT exist (dated fixtures only) — say so on the
rail's "recorded through". AI: NO AI REQUIRED throughout (doors and slots are hand-authored; mining
"asked before the cut" questions would be FRONTIER MODEL OCCASIONAL, human-reviewed).

## Prohibited
A timeline with a lock icon; tabs that separate books / desk / not-yet; sepia filter or rewind
whoosh; "what happened next" in a tooltip; git-graph of nodes; win/lose tallies; prediction-accuracy
badges; hatch on the unmodeled future.

## Acceptance criteria
All 12 states in the steps file with screenshots; at no state after 3 is Reality's clock or the
recorded past off screen; the seal refusal names its rule; the hindsight stamp rides on later
forks; one canonical state object; harness clean at both viewports.
