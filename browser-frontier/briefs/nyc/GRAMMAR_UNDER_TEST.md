# The grammar under test (after the critique wave) — for NYC lanes B–E

Read `briefs/CANON_AFTER_CRITIQUE.md` for the verdicts. The Browser grammar that survived both critics
is a COMPOSITION on one shared record, not a single screen:

- **The record protocol (substrate):** one canonical, append-only, SHA-256-folded RECORD per system;
  addresses name state (system · moment · branch · viewpoint · view) and never grant authority;
  authority travels as Offers redeemed by a present person (grant → redeem → lease); kinds propagate
  (a value wears the kind of its weakest input). (Critic 2: "a record protocol that any surface renders
  as checked views".)
- **The ACT grammar — Jurisdiction (strongest model):** an act is a form whose lines other systems hold;
  five kinds of yes (RULE · WILL · TIME · MONEY · KNOWING); carriers (notice copies · remittance moves ·
  countersignature joins); disclosure (content · shape only · count and result · nothing); each system's
  own ground/register; the standing line ("you are in … your word counts for … not for …"); refusals
  name the rule; three records, no god view.
- **The TIME grammar — Cut:** stand at a cut (books · desk · not yet · since); floor, never nearest; the
  seal and the hindsight ledger; the Horizon Rail with Reality shuttered; branches coupled pinned / paced
  / free; the seam verdicts HELD · DIVERGED · IMPOSSIBLE · RE-DECLARE; three backs (trail · record ·
  acts).
- **Views — Concordance:** every representation is a witness of the one record (Room, Table, Timeline,
  Direct, voice, map); one act number in every view; "Do these agree?"; covers declare what a view cannot
  show; the Downshift keeps authority and truth on every device.
- **Reach — Guest:** BOW inside other surfaces (claim cards, Readings, Offers, rings, standing orders);
  home is a finite Round that ends "level with the record".
- **Operators:** lift an act (TakeItOut: WHY by experiment, luck fixed; clamp = follow a causal
  relation); forks as lineages with closed lockfiles (Parting: an address is well-formed only when
  closed); discovery by marks and typed doors (Because · Meanwhile · Who · Here · Who says yes · What's
  different), the Bench for questions without an anchor, and the Want for the honest hole.
- **Place — the Room is ONE view, not the index** (Institution was killed as the navigation model;
  kept as a view that changes with state: lit/dark rooms, four ways not to know, sit-then-sign).
- **SCALE (to be pushed by lane B):** a change of subject along a named, typed relation with one quantity
  pinned (hinges: PEERS · RUN BY · PAID TO · WRITTEN AS · BOUND BY · MADE BY), not camera zoom.
