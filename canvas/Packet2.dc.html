What must be free for the medium to spread

Hypotheses, not prices (full lens on the Commercial board and in “BOW Economic Architecture”). Free: asking and arriving; observing Reality and the public record; entering a recorded Moment; cutting and sharing a fork; the address; reading provenance. Paid: presences that keep running while you’re away (persistent Worlds — they cost compute), signed seats with warrants (organisations, agents), metered model runs, hosted publishing at scale, classroom licences with the teacher’s screen and evidence.

Wave 2 sharpened one line: observing must stay wide and free, and occupying must be the thing people pay for — which only works if ENTER actually costs something (omniscience, a signature, a running clock). The boards now show that cost; no buyer has yet paid it.
