# Browser cross-domain attack — the strongest two grammars outside sports

BOW Browser Founding Frontier · wave 2 · 30 Sep 2026. The critics chose the two strongest models:
**Jurisdiction** (the ACT grammar) and **the Cut** (the TIME grammar) (`briefs/CANON_AFTER_CRITIQUE.md`
§5). Two builders re-expressed each model's full loop — ARRIVE → ORIENT → ENTER → ACT → STATE CHANGE →
WHY → WHAT IF → FORK → COMPARE → RETURN — in three domains, keeping the grammar identical and changing
only the nouns and the art direction. They were told to **show the misfit, not bend the domain**, and
each board carries a **collapse report** that grades every grammar element HELD (unchanged), BENT (works
with a named change) or BROKE (does not apply), written from what the prototype actually does
(`briefs/specs2/CROSS_DOMAIN.md`).

Boards: `prototypes/CrossJurisdiction.html`, `prototypes/CrossCut.html` (open "Collapse report" on
either). Domains:
1. **Chemical equilibrium** — a sealed flask of N₂O₄ ⇌ 2 NO₂; one state, four representations (the
   lab, the molecules, the equation, the graph); K and ΔH from the fixture [verify], temperature by
   van 't Hoff (a named simplification); everything else computed.
2. **A supply chain** — Harrow Medical (fictional): a single-source microcontroller supplier's fab
   fire at 06:10, 2,300 parts on hand at 210 a day ≈ 11 days, five customer promises.
3. **The awkward domain** — Yellowstone's northern range after the 1995–96 wolf reintroduction:
   nobody holds the system, there is essentially one act (by agencies), the downstream story is
   contested science, and the state is continuous and sparsely observed.

Secondary evidence: New York lane C ran the grammar through six city flows (museum, university
chemistry lab, hospital, port, financial district, arena; `research/NYC_C_KNOWLEDGE_CITY.md`), and lane B
traced six SCALE chains across sports, biology, chemistry, documents and invoices
(`research/NYC_B_CITY_SCALE.md` §5). Labels: everything on both boards is **PROPOSED**; live feeds are
**SPECULATIVE**; no AI runs. Nobody outside this process has used either board.

**The finding in one sentence: the grammar transfers wherever someone holds the system, bends where
nature answers instead of a seat, and breaks where nobody holds it.** The substrate under it — two
logs, SHA-256 over signed acts, the address in the URL, a second client as an observer, one state
drawn many ways — held in every column, including Yellowstone, where the record is one entry long.
The one grammar element that held in all six columns is **a door named by its question**.

## 1. The ACT grammar (CrossJurisdiction)

**Chemistry — BENT (15 · 10 · 1).** A reaction request at Bench 4 is a paper whose lines the lab's
other systems hold: the booking desk (TIME, a slot on *its* calendar; a busy slot is refused naming
whose calendar it is), the safety officer (RULE, computed exactly from block limit and peak pressure;
a later request is returned naming lab rule 5), the stockroom keeper (WILL — accepts twice, then
declines when the shelf is empty), money (computed from the lab budget, conserved), and the lab log
(KNOWING, a copy that cannot be recalled). Taking the bench lead's seat by an Offer rewrites the
standing line: "your word counts for heating, compressing and adding to your flask · not for the lab's
safety limits, the block's calendar, the stockroom shelf, **or what nature answers**." One state draws
four ways (lab, molecules "drawn, not observed", equation, graph) and every landed act changes all four;
the chemistry is computed (heating 25 → 45 °C moves K from 0.00461 to ≈ 0.0197 by van 't Hoff, and the
flask settles at [NO₂] ≈ 0.0155 mol/L — checked by the parent). What bent:
- **A row with no holder.** Nature is drawn as a dashed "NO LINE" row: "Nature does not say yes; it
  answers." It is not a sixth kind of yes. The balance law acts like a RULE computed from K "with
  nobody applying it — a rule with no officer."
- **Refusal needs a second form.** The keeper's refusal names no rule — it is a will. "The grammar needs
  a second form: *no rule; a will*."
- **The seal does not fit.** "The law answers at once, so nothing is sealed except a real flask's
  departures from the law." The dry run is exact for Nature: "the one line you can know in advance is
  not a yes."
- **The seam never fires** (the one BROKE): the law answers the same in branch and record at once, so
  no verdict is ever needed.
- **COMPUTED and MODELED blur:** "the law is the model."

**Supply chain — HELD (19 · 7 · 0).** A purchase order to a second source has six lines: yours, the
supplier's WILL (answers on its own clock; declines an order above its capacity — again "no rule; its
capacity is its will"), change control's RULE (the approved-supplier rule refuses the shortcut, and
**the refused file cost five of the eleven days**), the carrier's TIME, finance's MONEY (a No blocks
Send; value conserved) and five customers told their new dates (KNOWING). Plus a row for the burned
supplier, who will not answer. Three records, no god view: the second source's capacity and the
pallet's contents are never shown to Harrow. What bent: TIME is a cadence ("next departure after
ready"), not a slot; the silent supplier's absence means "will not answer", not "not needed" — **two
meanings under one mark**; WHY's numbers all wear AUTHORED ("it explains the model, not a company");
and the seam has nothing to grade it without a feed or a scripted future.

**Yellowstone — BROKE (1 · 7 · 18).** "Nobody holds the range, so nobody can offer." The page refuses
ENTER and names the rule; all you can take is **a chair for a fork, not a seat**, whose standing line
reads "your word counts for what you assume in a branch · not for anything an agency signed, anything
nature did, or any count nobody recorded". The 1995–96 reintroduction paper is drawn with roles only;
every WILL line is a hole, because inventing an agency's position would break the no-invented-positions
rule; the service's RULE cannot be run ("a RULE-yes cannot be computed from a name"); there is no
calendar, no amount, no join. A fork that releases no wolves changes one input and shows four rows of
"not computed". The seam can give only the trivial DIVERGED (the record says 31 released); the other
three verdicts "need a control that does not exist". What held — and held *best* — is the truth
grammar: "mostly UNKNOWN dashed, one OBSERVED solid, the dispute shown, not settled." Wolves, hunting
outside the park, drought, bears and cougars appear as candidate causes, every share "disputed", no
weights drawn.

## 2. The TIME grammar (CrossCut)

**Chemistry — BENT (8 held · 7 bent · 0 broke).** The grammar fits a flask better than expected,
because a flask has a **desk**: the photometer's lagged reading is "what this seat could know, wrong by
a known lag" — the best fit in the set. Forks are exact and cheap (the same integrator with different
acts), and PINNED coupling costs nothing because a flask does not react to being watched. The seat's
legal action space is checked by computation, not prose — the repair the Cut's critic asked for. It
bends in three load-bearing places:
- **The seal is a rule, not an ignorance.** The new equilibrium is computable at the desk ("work it
  out" computes the endpoint from K without opening Since). The seal can withhold the *path*, not the
  endpoint.
- **Nature does not sign; it answers.** In the Council, Recorded is refused for nature; only Model (van
  't Hoff, a named model that ran) and Declare survive.
- **Reality cannot grade nature without an independent instrument.** The seam grades people's acts
  well (bath: DIVERGED or HELD; syringe: IMPOSSIBLE), but the "measurement" is authored, so HELD shows
  the mechanism, not that van 't Hoff is right.
Kind propagation inverts the sports pattern: **the path is less certain than the endpoint** (K is
OBSERVED [verify], the path wears AUTHORED because a rate constant was chosen).

**Supply chain — HELD (10 held · 5 bent · 0 broke).** "The domain the grammar was built for": the books
know the fire and the desk does not (the 06:00 ERP export says 2,300 parts and eleven days); the
supplier's restart date is unknowable at the cut; Since has all three hands (facts, a correction, a
post-mortem argument); hindsight bites (declare "day 42" with Since open and be HELD against day 47 —
the stamp says so). All four seam verdicts are reachable: DIVERGED (the supplier's estimate),
IMPOSSIBLE (the quality rule strands broker chips), HELD, RE-DECLARE. It bends where:
- **the world reacts to you** — PINNED coupling cannot say that a 1,500-chip order moves the broker's
  price;
- **one rail hides many clocks** — the supplier's calendar, the carrier's, the customers' due dates and
  Quality's are all real, and the rail draws only Harrow's day count;
- **change control is prose**, not an engine, so nothing stops a move the contracts forbid;
- **WHY stops at people** — why the supplier took six to eight weeks is on no page, and the chain says
  so;
- **the truth textures say nothing**, because a fictional company is all AUTHORED.

**Yellowstone — BROKE (2 held · 8 bent · 5 broke).** Doors, cuts, the three backs and the rail's
shutter survive. The books, the Council, the branch, the seam and WHY all assume someone keeps records
and that Reality can grade a counterfactual. Yellowstone has neither:
- **No books.** Counts at cut granularity are UNKNOWN in the fixture; the plane is five empty tracks.
- **The Council degenerates into a declaration form.** Recorded is refused for both seats and Model is
  refused (no named, versioned model of the range); only Declare and Unknown remain.
- **A branch has nothing to compute.** Its frame is a stamped statement with holes.
- **The seam has no control.** Reality has one history. The act itself is gradable (release: HELD;
  none: DIVERGED); everything downstream is UNKNOWN — **a fifth state outside the canon's four**.
- **"Contested" is not a kind.** The board draws two readings and leaves the dispute in words.
- **WHY breaks exactly where the science does**: five candidate causes drawn dashed, in parallel, with
  no weights. Only the signed act traces.
- **The seal hides an argument, not a fact.** "You find out by living it" fails: thirty years of living
  it did not settle it.
What survived is the part that matters most for this kind of system: **a door named by its question**
("Wolves are about to be released. What will the northern range do?") — "the question is the whole
product here".

## 3. What transfers, what bends, what breaks

Merged from both boards, New York lanes B and C, and the earlier waves:

1. **Holders are the precondition.** Both grammars assume someone keeps books at cut granularity,
   holds a line, keeps a clock and applies a rule. That is true of a club, a company, a lab bench, a
   hospital's staffing office, a port's berth schedule, a museum's collection record (lane C) and a
   legislature at a recorded moment (Philadelphia 1787, earlier waves; the 1817 Canal Fund, lane C). It
   is false of an ecosystem, an economy as a whole and "the city" (lanes B, D: a saved question with no
   seat). **For systems nobody holds, the Browser is observe-only:** doors named by questions, the few
   recorded acts, the dispute drawn with its sources, declarations in your own ink, Wants. It must not
   offer a seat, a Council or a seam it cannot honour.
2. **Nature answers; it does not say yes.** Both boards independently needed a row for something that
   answers and cannot be asked (a NO LINE row on the paper; Recorded refused for nature in the Council).
   The five kinds of yes stay five. A law is a **response line**: computed, exact when the law is
   exact, held by nobody.
3. **A silent seat is not a law.** The burned supplier "could answer someday; nature never will." Two
   marks, not one.
4. **Refusals name the rule — or say that no rule applied.** WILL refusals (the stockroom shelf, the
   supplier's capacity) cite no rule. The honest form is "no rule; a will".
5. **The seal must say why.** Boston and Harrow: nobody can know yet. Chemistry: computable, withheld by
   the cut's rule (a policy — the desk can "work it out"). Yellowstone: thirty years of living it did
   not settle it (an argument). Three reasons, three marks — the same move as absence having kinds.
6. **Reality grades acts, not nature, and never a counterfactual without a control.** The seam graded
   people's acts well in chemistry and all four verdicts were reachable in the supply chain. It could not
   grade van 't Hoff (no independent instrument; the "measurement" was authored) and could not grade a
   Yellowstone with no wolves (Reality has one history).
7. **"Contested" has no mark.** Both boards drew two readings and left the dispute in words.
8. **Kind propagation is correct and, in a fully authored world, uninformative.** Harrow is all
   AUTHORED; the chemistry's path is AUTHORED because a rate constant was chosen, while its endpoint is
   more certain than its path — the reverse of sports. The textures earn their place at the boundary
   between real and authored (Boston, New York) and in domains full of holes (Yellowstone).
9. **Clocks are per holder.** One Horizon Rail hides the supplier's calendar, the carrier's, the
   customers' due dates and Quality's; a flask's clock is nobody's and the reader moves it; the range's
   clock is decades and kept by no one. The act grammar already gives every system its own clock; the
   time grammar's rail must draw them.
10. **PINNED coupling cannot say the world reacts to you.** A 1,500-chip order would move the broker's
    price. Either a declared counterparty response model (drawn per fork, per the critique) or an honest
    "this branch assumes the market did not notice".
11. **One state, many drawings is domain-neutral.** Four chemistry representations and three supply
    views all moved together from one state. Concordance transfers wherever a state exists.
12. **WHY ends honestly at people and at disputes** ("why the supplier took six to eight weeks is on no
    page"; Yellowstone's five causes drawn dashed, in parallel, unweighted).

## 4. What changes in the canon

Adopted now as frontier law (they change no existing meaning):
- **Response line.** A law that answers is drawn as its own row: no holder, no signature, not a yes;
  computed, and exact only when the law is. Distinct from a **silent seat** (a holder that does not
  answer).
- **Refusals name a rule or say "no rule; a will".**
- **The seal states its reason:** unknowable yet · withheld by the cut's rule · contested.
- **Clocks per holder on the rail** when a paper touches more than one clock.
- **A pinned branch that a counterparty would react to** either declares a response model (drawn per
  fork) or says it assumes no reaction.
- **Systems nobody holds are observe-only** — no seat, no Council, no seam beyond the recorded acts.

**For your ruling** (both builders asked; the canon fixed the seam at exactly four verdicts):
1. **When Reality cannot grade a claim** (no control, no independent instrument). Recommendation: keep
   the four verdicts for what Reality *can* grade, and add a typed **absence of verdict** — "NO
   VERDICT · no control" or "NO VERDICT · no instrument" — drawn like an UNKNOWN with its reason, never
   counted as a fifth verdict. It keeps the four honest and makes the silence explicit.
2. **Contested.** Recommendation: not an eighth truth texture. A contested value wears UNKNOWN with the
   reason "contested", and opens onto the competing claims side by side, each with its own kind and
   source, with no weights and no winner ("shown, not settled").

## 6. Labels and limits

Both boards: PROPOSED; no AI; the chemistry is computed from a fixture K and ΔH marked [verify] with an
authored rate constant (real settling is far faster [verify]); Harrow Medical and its customers are
fictional; Yellowstone uses only the fixture's facts and [verify] slots and never picks a side in the

critic has judged them. They are evidence of where the grammar is honest, not of whether anyone wants
it in these domains.
